MW3 Zombies Acquisitions & Schematics

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-11

You Can Literally Spawn In With A Ray Gun, A Wunderwaffe, A Juggernog Perk Can Or An Aether Tool To Upgrade Your Weapon From Gray To Purple. Schematics Are Extremely Powerful, And Acquisitions Are Directly Tied To Them.

Where To Find Them

  1. Go To Your Gear Section.
  2. Select A Slot In Your Rucksack To Add An Item.
  3. You Have Acquisitions (Items You Already Have) And Schematics (Craft A New One).
  • Rucksack Sizes, Gear And Operators: MWZ Rucksack Guide.

Acquisitions

  • You Get Acquisitions Either By Crafting Them Via Schematics, Or By Playing The Game, Finding Stuff And Bringing It Out Through Your Rucksack.
  • Your Acquisition Stash Has A Limit: 10 Items.
  • Rewards, Like The One For Completing The First Mission Of Act 1, Don't Seem To Count Toward That.

Add An Item

  1. Select A Rucksack Slot.
  2. Go To Acquisitions.
  3. Select The Item. When You Spawn In, You'll Have It.

Put An Item Back

  1. Hover Over The Item In Your Rucksack.
  2. Hit Unequip (R In The Video).
  3. It Goes Into Your Acquisition Stash To Use In A Different Game.
  • Exfil With An Item You Didn't Use And You Still Have It For Your Next Game.
  • The Rucksack Is A Little Less Secure Than Acquisitions. Fail To Successfully Exfil And You Lose Everything In Your Rucksack.
  • You Can Go Down And Be Revived By Another Squad, It Doesn't Matter. As Long As You Successfully Exfil, You Keep All The Items In Your Rucksack.
  • You Always Have Insurance With That Acquisition Stash.

Schematics

  • A Schematic Is Not The Item: The Juggernog Schematic, Not The Juggernog Perk Can.
  • A Schematic Is Just Around The Map Somewhere In A Match. Pick It Up.
  • You Need To Exfil With It Successfully. Then You Have That Schematic Permanently.
  • There's A Cooldown For How Often You Can Use It.

Craft An Item

  1. In Your Gear Section, Select A Rucksack Slot To Add An Item.
  2. Go To Schematics. A Schematic Is Only Unlocked Once You've Exfilled With It.
  3. Craft The Item. It's Added To Your Rucksack.
  4. Spawn In With It, Or Unequip It Into Your Acquisition Stash.

What You Can Craft

  • Perks: Juggernog, PhD Flopper, Elemental Pop, Death Perception And The Rest Of The Perk-A-Colas.
  • Ammo Mods: Like Dead Wire, Cryo Freeze And Brain Rot.
  • Wonder Weapons: The Ray Gun And The Wunderwaffe.
  • Aether Tools: Craft An Epic Aether Tool, Equip It On Your Weapon And It's Upgraded From Gray To Purple Right Off The Bat.
  • Refined Aetherium Crystal: Use It To Pack-A-Punch Your Gun To Level 2.
  • You Can Literally Spawn In With A Ray Gun And Pack-A-Punch It To Level 2.
  • Rarity And Tiers Explained: Gun Rarity And Pack-A-Punch.

Cooldown Timers

  • There's Timers On All The Schematics, And They All Have Different Timers.
  • The Cooldown Starts As Soon As You Craft The Item.
  • You Can't Use That Schematic Again Until The Timer Completely Goes Down To Zero. Exfil Or Die, It Doesn't Matter.
  • Perks: I Know For Perks The Timer Is 3 Hours. Even If You Complete All The Missions And Successfully Exfil, You Must Wait The Full 3 Hours.
  • Ray Gun: I Don't Know The Exact Timer, But I've Heard It's Something Crazy Like Up To 40 Hours.

Craft As Soon As The Timer Ends

  • Regardless Of What's In Your Rucksack Or Acquisition Stash, You Can Always Craft A Schematic Of Any Item At Any Time, So Long As It's Unlocked And Not On A Cooldown.
  • Not Going To Use It In Your Current Match? Craft It Anyway And Store It In Your Rucksack Or Acquisition Stash For Later. The Timer Immediately Starts To Bleed Out For The Next Time You Can Use The Schematic.
  • With The Large Rucksack You Can Store Up To Nine Items, And An Additional 10 In Your Acquisition Stash.
  • Have Stuff Super Rare On A Longer Cooldown? Craft It And Save It In Your Stash, And Take Other Stuff Less Important To You In.
  • The Better The Player You Are, The Less Scared You Are To Store It In Your Rucksack, Because You Have More Confidence You'll Successfully Exfil.
  • Even When You End Your Day And Have A Schematic Available, Craft It And Save It. Tomorrow You Have It Ready To Use, And You Can Craft Another One Right Away For Your Second Game.

Rarer Schematics

Classified Schematics

  • Aether Blade, Dog Bone And Golden Armor Plates.
  • From The Elder Sigil Side Of The Bad Signal Dark Aether. Complete All Three Contracts Within The Area For A Guaranteed Classified Schematic From One Of The Reward Rifts.
  • The Route: Act 4 Mission 1, Beat The Worm And Claim The Locked Diary, Then The Gold Dog Collar, Gold Pill Bottle And Gold Camera, The Pedestals At F5, And A Sigil Run Where You Always Claim Elder Sigils From The Reward Rifts.
  • Full Steps: Bad Signal Dark Aether Guide.

Countermeasures Schematics

  • Mags Of Holding, Blood Burner Key And V-R11.
  • The Only Way To Get Them Is Elder Sigil Contracts In The Countermeasures Dark Aether. They're Not Guaranteed: You Can Earn As Little As Zero But As Many As All Three In Any Given Run. A Regular Sigil Gives No Chance At The Schematics.
  • The Route: Act 4 Mission 2 And Kill The Boss Mangler For A Golden Drum, Then Three Seals For The Gloves, Target And Mirror, The Gold Gloves, Gold Target And Gold Mirror In Your Next Game, Then Place All Items On The Matching Pedestals At G4.
  • Full Steps: Countermeasures Dark Aether Guide.

Red Worm Schematics

  • Tier 3 Flawless Pack-A-Punch Crystal, Scorcher Wonder Weapon Case And Gold Legendary Aether Tool.
  • Full Steps: USB Red Worm Easter Egg.
