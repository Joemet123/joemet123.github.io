BO7 Zombies Tips And Settings

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-10

Short Tips And Settings, One Short Each.

Charged Melee

  • Like Before, You Can Hold Down Your Knife Button To Pull It Out.
  • New In BO7: Charge Your Knife Attack By Aiming And Then Knifing.
  • These Charged Melee Kills Do More Damage Than A Traditional Melee Attack.
  • Pro Tip: There's A New Tier Of Points. Headshot Melee Kills Get You More Points Per Kill Than Other Methods.
  • More Melee Tips In The Ashes Early Game Strategy.

Crush GobbleGums

Your Bad GobbleGums Now Have Great Use: Crush Them For Essence.

  1. When You're In Game, Look Under Your GobbleGums. For Each One There's A Keybind Or Controller Bind.
  2. Hit The Button It Says Under It To Open Your GobbleGum Menu. You Can Use A Gum Or Crush It.
  3. Use Your D-Pad Or Mouse Wheel To Scroll Through The Gums.
  4. Hit The Key Shown Next To Convert To Crush The Gum.
  • Whimsical (Rainbow): 100 Essence.
  • Rare (Blue): 500 Essence.
  • Epic (Purple): 1,250 Essence.
  • Legendary (Gold): 2,500 Essence.
  • Ultra (Red): 5,000 Essence.
  • Pro Tip: If A Double Points Is Active, It Doubles Your Points For Gum Crushing.
  • Values As Shown In The Video. Patches Can Change Them.

Prestige

  1. From The BO7 Zombies Menu, Go Over From Play To The Career Tab.
  2. Hit Progression.
  3. Hit Prestige, Then Select Prestige 1.
  4. Hit Prestige Details. It Tells You Exactly Everything That Happens.
  5. Continue, Then Hold The Button It Tells You To Reset Your Level And Enter Prestige 1.
  • This Wipes Your Level Back To 1.
  • You Don't Lose Any Of Your Weapon Builds Or Camos, But Some Stuff Has To Be Re-Unlocked.
  • Your Level Does Carry Between Multiplayer, Zombies And Campaign.

Cleaner HUD

The Round Counter Goes To The Bottom Left, With No Compass, No Minimap And No Orange Indicators In Your Face.

  1. Pause Your Game, Go To Settings, Then Interface, Then Select Gameplay HUD.
  2. Under HUD Widget Visibility, Make Sure It's On And Hit Show More.
  3. Disable Compass, Mini-Map And Mission Tracker.
  4. Scroll Up To Zombies HUD Presets. Under Theme, Select Aetherium.
  5. Lastly, Go To Layouts And Select Classic.
  • This Feature Is Currently Bugged And May Reset On You Repeatedly. Set It Again If It Does.

Private Match

  1. From The Main Menu, Go To Your Settings. Change Your Party Privacy To Closed If You're The Host And You're Inviting Everybody, Or At The Very Least To Invite Or Code Only.
  2. If You Want Somebody Who's Not Your Friend To Join, Give Them Your Party Code. On Their Screen They Hit The Icon Shown In The Video And Input That Code.
  3. Already In Game: Settings, Party Settings, Click On Your Party, Then Party Settings Again. Close It Or Change It To Invite Only From There.
  4. The Most Important Part: When You Hit Select Match, Turn Off Squad Fill In The Lower Left-Hand Corner. That Stops Randoms From Joining Your Game.

Turn Off Pack-A-Punch Camos

There Are Three Different Ways, And They Each Kind Of Do Different Things:

  1. Before You Start The Match: The Button On The Bottom Of The Screen Turns Off Pack-A-Punch Camos. When You First Pack-A-Punch A Weapon In The Map, There's No Pack-A-Punch Camo On It.
  2. In The Match: Melee The Pack-A-Punch Machine. It Does A Green Effect, And When You Pack-A-Punch Your Weapon, There's No Camo On It.
  3. The Master Switch: Settings, Interface, Gameplay HUD, Then Scroll Down To The Gameplay Tab And Turn Off Your Pack-A-Punch Camo. This Is A Global Setting: No Matter What You Do In Game, You Will Never Have A Pack-A-Punch Camo.
  • If Your Camos Bug Out, Or You Have The Camo On One Gun And Not On Another, Turn The Master Switch Off And Then Back On Again.

Ballistic Knife Revives

  • You're Never Really Shooting At The Person You're Reviving. You're Kind Of Shooting Under Them At All Times.
  • Slightly Longer Range: Aim Down And To The Left Of Their Body.
  • Closer: Aim Literally Completely Below The Body.
  • I Seem To Be A Little More Accurate With This Revive From The Hip Than Aiming Down Sights.
