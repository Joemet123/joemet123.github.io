BO7 Zombies Augments Research And Equip Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-10-08

Every Method Here Is An Official Method, Not A Glitch.

Quick Answers

  • You Can't Even Begin To Think About Augments Until You Reach Level 11.
  • To Unlock Augments You Literally Just Get XP. The Item Doesn't Need To Be Equipped On You.
  • Black Ops 6 Research Carries Forward, But Every Item Has Three New Augments To Research.
  • Finish An Item's Research To Equip 1 Major And 2 Minor Augments On It, Three Total.

Unlock The Augments Menu

  1. Reach Level 11.
  2. Go To The Weapons Tab At The Top.
  3. The Previously Locked Augments Tab Is Now Available. Open It.
  4. You Now Have Two Sections: Equip Augments And Research Augments.

Research

BO6 Carry-Forward

  • In Black Ops 6 There Were Six Augments For Every Item. Black Ops 7 Adds An Additional Three For Every Item.
  • Your BO6 Progress Carries Forward. You Don't Have To Re-Unlock Anything.
  • The Final Augment Slot Is Not Actually An Ability. It's A Bonus Slot.
  • To Take Advantage Of The New Ones, Research Your Old Items Again.
  • Anything New, Like Wisp Tea (Perk), Fire Works (Ammo Mod) And Toxic Growth (Field Upgrade), Has All Nine Augments To Unlock Right From The Get-Go.
  • The Menu Shows A Percentage For Each Item. New Items Start At 0%.

Pick An Item

  1. Open Research Augments.
  2. Select The Perk, Ammo Mod Or Field Upgrade.
  3. As Long As The Check Is Next To It, That's The Item You're Researching.

Earn XP

  • You Just Get XP. It Doesn't Matter What Weapons Or Strategies You Use.
  • As You Go On, It Takes Progressively More XP To Unlock Each Augment.
  • Augments Are Not Time Based. They're XP Based, So You Want To Kill As Many Zombies As Quickly As Possible.
  • Do A Fast High-Round Strategy, Get To Say Round 50, Then Exfil.

Finishing An Item Mid-Match

  • You Can't Change The Research Item Mid Game.
  • If You Complete An Item's Augments In Your Game, It Randomly Selects The Next Item For You. You're Not Wasting XP.
  • Don't Want That Item? Change It When You Get Back From Your Game.
  • You Don't Need To Exfil Or Quit To Change It.

Equip Augments

  1. Open Equip Augments.
  2. Pick An Item.
  3. By Default You Have A Major And A Minor Augment Selected.
  4. Once That Item's Research Is At 100%, Select Two Different Minor Augments.

Result: 1 Major And 2 Minor Augments Per Item, Three Total.

Per Item

  • This Doesn't Work Like Camos. It's Per Item.
  • You Don't Need To Finish A Whole Class Like Perks, Ammo Mods Or Field Upgrades.
  • Items That Aren't 100% Complete Stay At 1 Major And 1 Minor For Now.

Recap

  1. Reach Level 11. Weapons Tab, Then Augments.
  2. Check An Item In Research Augments.
  3. Get XP. The Item Doesn't Need To Be Equipped.
  4. Change A Randomly Picked Item After The Match.
  5. At 100%, Equip 1 Major And 2 Minor In Equip Augments.
