MW3 Zombies Scorcher Wonder Weapon Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-11

If You Want A Wonder Weapon That Clears Out Huge Hordes Of Zombies, This Is The One For You. If You Want One That's Quick, It's Not Really The One For You.

How To Get It

  • Mystery Box.
  • Challenges Around The Map: A Mercenary Stronghold, An Aether Nest, Or Any Of The Places On Your Tac Map.

Controls

  • Fly: On Controller, Hold Down Your Left Trigger, Your Aim Down Sights Button.
  • Charge Shot: Hold Down The Trigger.
  • Single Shot: Spam The Trigger A Couple Of Times.
  • Ammo: Infinite. After Any Shot, Or After You Fly, It Regenerates Almost Instantly.
Advertisement

Fly

  1. Look In The Direction You Want To Go.
  2. Hold Down Aim Down Sights. The Wonder Weapon Points At The Ground And Shoots You Straight Into The Air.
  3. Deploy Your Parachute And Fly Wherever You Want To Go.
  4. You Can Do This Over And Over Again.
  • It Takes Five Bullets, But By The Time You're All The Way In The Air The Ammo Recharges.
  • At First I Was Turning Backwards And Spinning Around. Just Look Forward In The Direction You Want To Go.

Charge Shot

  • Hold Down The Trigger. It Takes Five Bullets, Same As Flying.
  • Really Great At Clearing Out Huge Hordes. Charge It Up, Shoot Into A Horde, And It Will Kill Every Single Zombie.

Single Shot

  • Spam The Trigger A Couple Of Times. It Shoots One Single Fire Bullet And Takes One Bullet From Your Stack.
  • Not As Much Damage As The Charge Shot, But If You Need To Get A Quick Shot Out, It Still Packs A Pretty Good Punch.
  • I Believe This Could Potentially Be A Glitch. If It Doesn't Work In The Future, That's Why.

Where It Shines

  • Clearing Out Large Hordes Of Zombies. That's What It Does Best.
  • Training Or Exfilling: The Ultimate Weapon.
  • Going Around The Map Doing Tons Of Contracts And Challenges: Your Bread And Butter.

Where It Struggles

  • Slow: A Bit Slow All Around, Whether You're Blasting Away To Another Area Or Shooting Zombies. Tier 3 Pack-A-Punched Or Not, Speed Cola Or Not. That's Its Main Drawback.
  • Close Quarters: Not Great. Have Another Weapon Out, Like A Shotgun, And Have This As Your Secondary.
  • Red Zone: Really Not Great There Unless It's Tier 3 Pack-A-Punched. With Super Sprinting Zombies That Hit For More Damage, An AR, A Ray Gun Or Another Wonder Weapon Will Probably Benefit You More, Because Those Have Much Better Mobility When Running And Shooting.
  • No Revives: I Tested It. It Does Not Revive Other People In Your Game.
