Necrofluid Gauntlet Unlock Guide, All Steps

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-09

How To Unlock It. What It Does Once You Have It: Necrofluid Gauntlet Abilities.

Power And Pack-A-Punch

The Game Very Clearly Guides You Through This, But The Order You Do Things In Could Help With Efficiency.

  1. Pro Tip: Add Stun Grenades To Your Loadout Before You Get Into The Game, For Use Later On.
  2. Grab T.E.D.D.'S Head In The Server Room At Spawn By Jumping Up And Opening The Door. Melee The Floor Panel And Pick Up The Head.
  3. Apply T.E.D.D. To Ol' Tessie.
  4. Leave Spawn Towards Blackwater Lake. Locate The Generators, Destroy The Cysts On Them, Then Flip The Power Switch Within Each Area.
  5. Blackwater Lake: The Switch Is Upstairs In The Cabins.
  6. Ashwood: The Generator Is Up The Zipline, In The Middle Of The Top Area, Through Two Doors. Its Switch Is Right There.
  7. After Flipping That Switch, Drive Tessie Into The Garage And Build The Pack-A-Punch Machine.
  8. Vandorn Farm: From The Point Of View Looking Into The Garage, Go To The Right And Up The Pathway. Do The Generator. The Switch Will Be Upstairs In The Barn.
  • There's A Tomahawk Easter Egg At Blackwater That Can Get You Random Rewards Of Salvage, Points Or Even Pack-A-Punch Crystals, So Going This Way First Is Nice. Steps Are In The Early Game Strategy Guide.

Stabilizer Chip From Uber Klaus

  1. After Flipping All Three Switches, Head To Spawn At Janus Towers Plaza.
  2. As Long As You're On Round 8 Or Above, Go Down The Road Toward Blackwater Lake And Near The Project Janus Storage Container Until A Very Beefy Uber Klaus Spawns Out Of It.
  3. To Kill Him More Easily, First Destroy His Shoulder Pads And Then Focus On His Core.
  4. Once Uber Klaus Is Gone, He'll Morph Into A Klaus Core. Defeat That Core Too.
  5. A Stabilizer Chip Will Drop For You To Pick Up.

Free Klaus

  1. Make Your Way Back To Ashwood, Up The Zipline, Into The Sheriff's Office, To The Left Of Vulture Aid. You'll Now Find Klaus In The Cell.
  2. Insert The Stabilizer Chip Into The Device On The Cell.
  3. Acquire 3 Stun Grenades, Typically Either Through The Ground Loot Or The Crafting Station.
  4. Throw Them Through The Cell, Making Sure They Ricochet To Detonate Practically Right On Top Of Klaus. It Takes 2 To 3.
  5. If Done Correctly, He'll Get Stunned, And After About 10 To 15 Seconds He's Going To Escape That Cell.
  • Pro Tip: Stand Where I'm Standing In The Video And Throw Where I'm Throwing, And It'll Work Every Time. You Should Never Miss.

Free Stun Grenades. If You Spawn In With One, You Can Find An Additional Two In The Map For Free, Meaning You Have To Buy Zero:

  • One At Spawn In The Server Room, Where You Got T.E.D.D.'S Head.
  • If You're Looking At Juggernog, Turn Around And It's Right There.

Aether Barrel At Zarya Cosmodrome

  1. Head To The Rocket Area Where The Wisp Tea Perk Is, At Zarya Cosmodrome, And Go To The Underground Support Systems.
  2. As Soon As You Open The Door, You're Going To See The Control Panel.
  3. Summon Klaus In Front Of It: Hold Down Your Tactical Grenade Button And A Circle Will Appear On The Floor. Let Go And He'll Land In That Exact Location.
  4. Klaus Will Power On The Control Panel, Indicated By The Green Light.
  5. Interact With It To Activate A Scanner. The Panel's Light Will Go Red And A Scanning Circle Will Spawn On The Floor.
  6. Stand In It For About 30 Seconds.
  7. If Completed, The Window In Front Of You Will Slide Down, Allowing You To Pick Up The Aether Barrel (The Canister).
  • You Cannot Leave The Circle Or You Will Fail, And The Same Thing Goes If You Go Down. If Either Happens, You Have To Go To The Next Round And Activate The Machine Again.

Carrying The Barrel

  • Grabbing It Prevents You From Sprinting, Meaning If You Attempt To Move Forward On Foot, You Will Die Very Quickly.
  • Any Jump Pad You Use With The Barrel On You Will Drop It On The Pad Before Launching You Away.
  • So You Need To Use Tessie.
  • The Barrel Can Be Voluntarily Dropped, Shown By The Button On The Bottom Of Your Screen. But Don't Lose It.

Charge It At All 3 Generators

  1. Insert The Barrel Into Each Of The 3 Power Generators, One At A Time. The Generator Charges It In A Self-Done 20-Second Process.
  2. Upon Charge Completion, You'll Be On A 1 Minute 45 Second Timer To Pick Up The Barrel And Deliver It To The Next Location.
  3. After Successfully Completing The Final Generator In Vandorn Farm, Quickly Grab The Barrel From The Generator And Place It Downstairs In The Cube Under Vandorn Farm.
  4. All The Sides Of The Cube Will Light Up Green, Now Showing Symbols.
  • Any Order Works, But It's Highly Recommended To Do Vandorn Farm Last, Since That's Where The Final Timed Step Takes Place.
  • If You Fail At Any Point, The Barrel Will Return To The Cosmodrome For Pickup, Letting You Resume From Whatever Generator You Were On.

Best Route From The Cosmodrome

  1. Ashwood: Back To The Central Point, Ashwood, For That Generator, And To Reheal Tessie In The Garage.
  2. Blackwater Lake: Looking From The Outside Into The Garage, Make A Right-Hand Turn And Go Up The Left Pathway Back Towards The Blackwater Cabins.
  3. Vandorn Farm: Either Head Back To Ashwood And, Before Its Entrance, Turn Around The Loop To Go To Vandorn Farm, Or Just Keep Going In The Direction You Were Going From Blackwater And It'll Bring You Through Spawn Right To Vandorn Farm. Whatever You Prefer.

Read The 3 Roof Symbols

  1. Go To The Jump Pad Upstairs, Look At The Right Monitor And Interact To Launch To Janus Towers.
  2. As You're Getting Launched, Look Back Down Towards Vandorn Farm While Up In The Air. You'll Notice Red Symbols On 3 Different Roofs.
  3. Launch Back To Vandorn Farm By Interacting With The Left Monitor At The Janus Towers Jump Pad, And You'll Get To See The Symbols Again On Your Way Back Down.
  4. Record Each Of The 3 Symbols.
  • The Symbols Are Always In The Same Locations Every Single Game, But What They Actually Are Changes Every Single Game.
  • Pro Tip: You Can Actually See The Barn Symbol From The Outskirts Where You Drive Tessie. Record That Separately So You Only Have To Note Down Two Symbols In The Air.
  • Another Pro Tip: You Can Pause Your Game While You're In The Air To Better See Your Symbols.

Enter The Symbols At The Cube

Tap Your Three Symbols Into The Necrofluid Gauntlet Solver And It Shows How To Insert Them. The Same Panel Is On The Full Map Cheat Sheet.

Each Roof Corresponds To One Side Of The Cube Downstairs. When Looking At The Front, Where The Eyeball Is, The Side With No Symbol:

  • Barn: The Back Set Of Symbols.
  • House: The Right Set Of Symbols.
  • Tower: The Left Set Of Symbols.
  1. Insert Your Symbols By Interacting With Each Side Of The Cube To Change The Symbol.
  2. When You've Inserted Your Correct Symbols, Interact With The Eye On The Front To Confirm The Code.

Keep The Gauntlet

Even Though The Gauntlet Is There, Do Not Grab It Until You're Ready.

  • Pro Tip: A Cymbal Monkey Could Be Helpful To Cover You, Just In Case. Although It Is Easy, You're Trapped In A Small Green Circle, And You Do Not Want To Die Doing This.
  • If You Fail This Step, You Have To Wait Till The Next Round To Try Again.
  1. Interact To Pick Up The Gauntlet.
  2. Wait For The Green Spores To Spawn In.
  3. As They're Spawning In, Just Shoot One Bullet Of The Gauntlet Into Each One.
  4. Hold Your Reload Button Down To Suck Them In.
  5. Once You've Cleared The Room Of All The Spores, You Can Leave The Circle With Your Free Necrofluid Gauntlet.
