Totenreich Side Easter Eggs Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-09

Also On This Map: Viking Helmets, Wunderbarrage And Free Power-Ups.

Glocke Drop

  • Reward: The Best Rewards, Possibly Getting You Wonder Weapons For Free. In The Video: A Free Ray Gun Mark II.
  • You Get One For Free Every Game By Just Turning On Power And Pack-A-Punch. Another One Costs 2,000 Salvage From The Crafting Station, And It Can Also Be Fished Up For Free.
  1. It's A Scorestreak, So Activate It Like Any Other Scorestreak To Pull Out The Controller.
  2. Find An Open Enough Area To Call It In.
  3. It Pulses Every 10 Seconds Or So And Shoots Zombies Up Into The Air.
  4. Kill All The Zombies In The Air. Enough During A Phase Brings Another Pulse, Throwing More Zombies Up.
  5. Your Goal Is To Not Only Kill The Floating Zombies, But To Reserve Extra Zombies On The Ground To Get Swooped Up Into The Next Pulse.
  • The Faster You Kill The Raised Up Zombies, The Better The Chance You Have To Get Three Pulses And The Best Rewards.

Free ARC-XD

  • Reward: A 100% Guaranteed Free ARC-XD, Very Quickly. It's Needed For One Of The Main Quest Steps.
  1. Make Your Way To Eidskallen Square.
  2. It's Just Sitting On Top Of The Boxes Next To The Trap.
  • You Could Also Fish One Up Or Buy One.

Fishing Rod Loot

  • Reward: Loot Like Grenades, Scorestreaks, Perks, Or Even The Secret Viking Helmet.
  1. Find An Olaf's Cod Cranker Fishing Pole. They're Around The Map Near Water, Either On The Decks Or On Buildings Close To Water.
  2. Once You Have One, Go Near Any Body Of Water. Around The Railings You'll Occasionally See Red Holsters.
  3. Interact To Place The Fishing Rod On One.
  4. Wait For It To Turn Blue, Meaning It Actually Caught A Fish.
  5. Interact With The Fishing Pole Again To Reel In Some Loot.
  • Poles And Spots: Fishing And Pole Locations Sheet.
  • The Helmet: Totenreich Viking Helmets Guide.

Flammenfalle Trap: Two Modes

  • Reward: Heat Mode Melts The Ice On You And Gives Infinite Wonder Weapon Charges.
  • Fire Mode: Activate The Trap Via The Panel. That's What Shoots Out The Fire. It's A Standard Trap And Kills Zombies As Expected.
  • Heat Mode: Literally Go Up And Interact With The Trap Itself. No Flames, And It's Not Supposed To Kill.
  • Both Modes Cost 1,000 Essence To Activate, With A 40-Second Up Time And A 60-Second Recharge Time.
  • If You're On A Max Ammo Round And You're Out In The Open, Heat Mode Automatically Melts The Ice Right Off You. It's One Of The Only Things In The Map That Does That.
  • The Other Fire Mode Doesn't Do That.
  • While You're Standing In The Heat Mode Circle, You Have Infinite Wonder Weapon Charges.

Stop Getting Frozen

  • Reward: Five Ways To Stop Yourself From Freezing, Including Inside The Boss Fight.

What Does Work:

  1. Wild Fire Field Upgrade: Just Activate It And You're Good For Its Duration.
  2. Flammenfalle Heat Mode: Only The Heat Version, Started By Interacting With The Trap Itself, Not The Console. It Doesn't Kill, But In It, It Melts The Ice Off You, Gives You A Napalm Burst Effect On Your Weapon And Negates All Freezing.
  3. Jotunn Star: Have It Out While It's Got A Charge.
  4. Icebane Helmet: Earn And Equip It. It Basically Does The Same Thing As The Jotunn Star, But Also Occasionally Shoots Out Some Ice At Zombies When Sliding. Unlock Steps: Totenreich Viking Helmets Guide.
  5. Just Stay Inside.

What Doesn't Work Is Almost Everything:

  • No Tacticals Or Lethals.
  • Double Jumping, Sliding And Dolphin Diving, With Or Without PhD Flopper.
  • The Fire Trap. It's Literally Fire, But It Does Not Melt The Ice On You.

T.E.D.D. Fish Trap

  • Reward: The Fishy Fish Bot Trap. He Sings, Lures In The Zombies And Then Turns Them Against Themselves.
  1. Make Your Way To The Lighthouse And Inside The Building Shown In The Video. On The Wall There's A Fish Inside A Bucket. Interact To Pick It Up.
  2. In The Meantime, Grab A Shock Charge From The Crafting Station. You'll Need One For The T.E.D.D. Task.
  3. Wait For A T.E.D.D. Task To Spawn In. Once It's Spawned, Start It.
  4. Place Down Your Fish On The Right Side, Shown In The Video.
  5. Throw The Shock Charge At It. You'll See T.E.D.D. Get Electrocuted.
  6. Complete The Given Task To A Legendary Reward, Which Is The Highest Tier.
  7. Two Parts Pop Out For You To Pick Up, Separate From The Crate.
  8. These Two Parts Are All You Need To Build Fishy Fish Bot Over At Eidskallen Square, Near The Ziplines.
  • He's Pretty Much Like Any Other Trap, Activatable Again After A Brief Downtime.

Richtofen Secret Room

  • Reward: Rewards From The Barrel At The End. The Video Does Not List Them.
  1. Make Sure You're Playing As Richtofen.
  2. Get To Round 15 Minimum And Make Sure You've Purchased Melee Macchiato.
  3. Melee The Barrel In Core Foundry Shown In The Video With Your Gun, So The Perk's Fist Ability Breaks It. No Wonder Or Melee Weapons.
  4. Hold Interact To Dig Up And Pick Up An Iron Cross. This Brings You To A Secret Area.
  5. In The Hallway, Pick Up The Intel To Clear Each Boundary.
  6. In The Room, One At A Time On Each Side, Pick Up A 115 Rock And A Soldier To Place And Balance Out The Scale On The Table.
  7. When Available, Grab The Sword Next To The Chair And Place It On The Knight Statue.
  8. I Believe You Must Interact With The Radio At Least Once To Finish This Quest And To Be Able To Actually Leave, So Do That.
  9. Once You Get Hazed And Hear Knocking On The Door, Interact To Open It Up And Let The Zombies Take You Down.
  10. Collect Your Rewards From The Barrel.
  • In The Secret Area You Must Repeatedly Wait For Dialogue To Finish And Do Things One At A Time Before You Can Progress Further.
  • After A Few Interactions You'll Be Able To Enter Codes Into The Radio. So Far These Only Give Dialogue.

Claw Machine Easy Method

  • Reward: An Easier And Quicker Solve For The Claw Machine Step Of The Main Quest.
  1. As Soon As You Get To The Claw Machine, Read The Board.
  2. See If Either Of These Two Combos Is Possible Right Away: Bunches Of 6 And 3, Or Bunches Of 7 And 2.
  3. Place Your Uranium To Make One Of Them.
  • 6 And 3 Example: A Uranium In Slots A2, B1 And D2. One Set Of Tubes Has Six Connected And The Other Has Three.
  • 7 And 2 Example: Uranium In A2, A3 And D4. It Works. 6 And 3 Was Also Possible On That Board.
  • All Solutions I've Ever Seen Include One Of These Two. I Can't 100% Say It's One Size Fits All.

Music Easter Egg

  • Reward: "No One There" By Vicky Psarakis And Kevin Sherwood Plays.
  1. Interact With The Three Mr. Peeks Headphones Around The Map, In Any Order.
  2. After Hitting The Third, The Song Plays.
  • Dry Dock: Near PhD Flopper, Down The Alleyway, On A Crate.
  • Core Foundry: Upstairs, On Top Of A Radio On The Floor.
  • Loven's Antiquities: Next To A Bowl.
