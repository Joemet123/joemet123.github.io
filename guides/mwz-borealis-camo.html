MW3 Zombies Borealis Mastery Camo Unlock Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-11

There's Actually Four Zombies Mastery Camos, With Borealis Last. Complete Everything To The Left And It Unlocks The Camo To The Right.

Find The Challenges

  1. Go To Any Weapon And Go To Your Gunsmith.
  2. Go To Customize, Then Camo.
  3. Tab Through To The Zombies Mastery Camos.
  • All Camos Is Separate (Example: In Zombies Get Five Mimic Kills). It's Not Needed For The Mastery Camos, But You'll Get Some While You're Doing Them.

The Order

  1. The Four Base Camos On A Weapon.
  2. Golden Enigma On That Weapon.
  3. Zircon Scale.
  4. Serpentinite.
  5. Borealis, Once You Have Serpentinite On 36 Weapons.

Base Camos

  • Get The Weapon To The Level, Then You Have Access To That Camo's Challenge.
  • You Can't Do Two At The Same Time. The Next Challenge Only Unlocks At Its Weapon Level.
  • Weapon Progression Is Shared Across The Game. You Can Rank Up Weapons In Multiplayer Too.
  • Already Leveled The Weapon To Max? You Can Probably Bang Out Almost All The Camo Challenges In One Full Game.
  • The Challenges May Be Slightly Different From Weapon To Weapon Or Category To Category (Pistols May Have Different Requirements), But The Way They Work Is Exactly The Same.

Example: SVA 545

  1. Level 2, Then 250 Kills.
  2. Level 13, Then Get 5 Kills Without Releasing The Trigger, 20 Times. You're Just Holding It Down.
  3. Level 20, Then 250 Kills In The Medium Or High Threat Zone (Sludge).
  4. Level 30, Then 250 Kills While Having At Least Four Perks Active (Chaos).

Golden Enigma

  1. Complete The Four Base Camos On The Weapon.
  2. Get 100 Kills And Successfully Exfil With That Weapon In A Single Deployment.

Zircon Scale

  1. Complete The Golden Enigma Camo On This Weapon.
  2. Complete Golden Enigma On Other Weapons. For The SVA 545: Six Golden Enigma Challenges With Assault Rifles, So Pick Five Other ARs. There's A Lot More Than Six, You Don't Have To Do Every Single One. Other Categories May Differ.
  3. Get 300 Kills With The Weapon While It's Pack-A-Punched.

Serpentinite

  1. Complete The Zircon Scale Camo On This Weapon.
  2. Complete 36 Zircon Scale Camo Challenges, Game Wide. That Ranges Across Assault Rifles, SMGs, Pistols And All The Other Weapon Categories.
  3. Get 10 Special Or Elite Zombie Kills With The Weapon.

Borealis

  • No Challenge Of Its Own. It Just Unlocks For You Immediately After You Complete 36 Serpentinite Camo Challenges.

Tips

  • Use The Weapon Regularly In Your Game And You're Going To Level It Up And Probably Just Complete Some Of These Naturally.
  • The Main Objective Really Is To Get The Weapon To Whatever Level You Need. On The SVA 545 That's 2, Then 13, Then 20, Then 30.
  • Pack-A-Punch Tiers And Threat Zones: Gun Rarity And Pack-A-Punch.
