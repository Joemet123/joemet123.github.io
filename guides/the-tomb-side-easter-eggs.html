The Tomb Side Easter Eggs Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-09

Free Ray Gun, Aether Tool, Pack-A-Punch Crystal, Self-Revive, Random Perk And Essence, Then The Song.

Free Ray Gun

  • Reward: A Free Guaranteed Ray Gun And 1,000 Essence, As Early As Round 1.
  1. Buy Death Perception. Yes, That's Right, It's A Requirement For This Easter Egg. The Perk Is In The Tombs Area Of The Map, Right Through The First Door At Spawn.
  2. Get A Shovel. Grab The One In The Same Room You're Already In For Death Perception (Shown In The Video). It Always Spawns There.
  3. Dig. Every Few Dig Spots You Get A Green Crystal. Pick It Up.
  4. Keep Digging To Collect Three Green Crystals In Total.
  5. Back At The Tombs Area, Interact With The Statue (Shown In The Video). Two Doppelghast Bosses Spawn Out Of It.
  6. Kill Them. At The Base Of The Statue You Get 1,000 Essence And A Free Ray Gun, Every Single Time.
  • You Get The Crystals Relatively Quickly, Possibly Within The First Three Digs. Sometimes It Can Take Quite A Few Dig Spots, So Just Keep Digging.
  • The Ray Gun Is Guaranteed Every Game. The Round You Do It On Depends On Your Essence And Digging Luck.

Free Aether Tool (Waterfalls)

  • Reward: A Free Purple Aether Tool.
  1. Equip Either Cryofreeze Or The Ice Staff. Staff Steps: The Tomb Ice Staff Guide.
  2. Head To The Dark Aether.
  3. Shoot The Two Waterfalls With Your Freeze Weapon To Turn Them Into Ice. Both Waterfalls Are Shown In The Video.
  4. When Done, A Purple Aether Tool Drops (Spot Shown In The Video).

Free Pack-A-Punch Crystal And Cryofreeze (Floating Rocks)

  • Reward: A Free Tier 1 Pack-A-Punch Crystal And A Cryofreeze Ammo Mod.
  1. Head To The Dark Aether.
  2. Shoot The Electrified Floating Rock In The Sky.
  3. A Couple Of Rocks Begin To Orbit The Arena. That Means The Easter Egg Is Activated.
  4. Shoot All Of The Moving Rocks As Quickly As You Can.
  5. You Hear A Noise. Your Free Crystal And The Cryofreeze Ammo Mod Are In The Center To Pick Up.
  • Don't See Your Electrified Rock: Just Shoot A Bunch Of The Rocks In The Sky Until A Couple Begin To Orbit.
  • Standing In The Center Does Make This A Little Bit Easier.

Free Self-Revive (10 Vases)

  • Reward: A Free Self-Revive And A Light Mend Ammo Mod.
  1. Shoot All 10 Vases Around The Map, In Any Order.
  2. Shoot The Last Vase To Claim Your Rewards.

The 10 Vases (Every Spot Is Shown In The Video)

  • Vase 1: Dig Site, The Spawn. Look Above Your Head.
  • Vase 2: Dig Site. Turn To Your Right And Look Near PhD Flopper, Above The Entrance Into The Next Area.
  • Vase 3: Dig Site. Right Above The Blue Dark Aether Portal.
  • Vase 4: Neolithic Catacombs. Outside The Barrier, By The Mystery Box.
  • Vase 5: Ossuary. Look Up And To The Left Of The Table.
  • Vase 6: Subterranean Temple. Look Up And To The Left Of The Dark Aether Teleporter.
  • Vase 7: While Staring At The Ceiling, In The Hole In The Ceiling.
  • Vase 8: Deep Excavation. A Little Bit Back, By The Stones, Looking Outside Of The Map By Quick Revive.
  • Vase 9: Shrine Of The Hierophants, By Stamin-Up. Look Up And To The Right.
  • Vase 10: The Tombs. On The Opposite Side Of The Painting Used For The Ice Staff.

Free Random Perk (Fire Bowls)

  • Reward: A Free Random Perk For Everyone In Your Game, Plus A Free Molotov From Each Bowl.
  1. Apply A Napalm Burst Ammo Mod To Your Weapon. You Can Do It At Any Of The Arsenal Machines Around The Map.
  2. Make Your Way To The Ossuary Area Of The Map, Outside The Green Portal (Shown In The Video). To The Right From There Is A Small Area With Unlit Fire Bowls Surrounding It.
  3. Shoot Each Fire Bowl With That Napalm Burst Weapon To Get A Free Molotov From Each.
  4. After Shooting The Sixth And Final Bowl, A Free Perk Drop Spawns In The Center Of That Room.

1,000 Essence And Full Meter (Generators)

  • Reward: 1,000 Essence, A Full Meter Power-Up And Armor Plates.
  1. Apply Dead Wire To Your Weapon.
  2. Head To The Dig Site.
  3. Shoot The Generator Next To The Armor Wall Buy.
  4. Then Shoot The Generator In The Dirt (Shown In The Video).
  5. Your Rewards Are On The Fallen Column Behind You.

Music Easter Egg (3 Headphones)

  • Reward: The Trivium Easter Egg Song, Titled Dig.
  1. Go To The Shrine Of The Hierophants. Looking At Stamin-Up, Turn To Your Left. Sitting By The Statue's Feet Are The First Mr. Peeks Bunny Ears Headphones. Interact With Them.
  2. Enter The Yellow Dark Aether Teleporter Right Behind You. As Soon As You Come Out Of The Portal, Make A Left Down The Path (Shown In The Video) To Your Second Pair Of Headphones. Interact With Them.
  3. Run Through The Green Dark Aether Portal Back To The Main Map. When You Come Through The Portal, Make A Left. The Third And Final Pair Is Right Next To A Pile Of Skulls. Interact With Them To Activate The Song.
