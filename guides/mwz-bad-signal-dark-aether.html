MW3 Zombies Bad Signal Dark Aether Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-11

Map Of This Dark Aether: Bad Signal Cheat Sheet.

Before You Start

  • Head To The Act Section Of The Main Zombies Menu And Change Your Act To Act 4.
  • No, You Do Not Need To Complete Acts 1 To 3 To Do Act 4.
  • Do What You Need To Do To Set Yourself Up For An Epic Boss Fight. If This Means Using Perks And Pack-A-Punch Schematics, So Be It.

Act 4 Mission 1: Bad Signal

  1. Once You're Fully Set Up, Make Your Way To The Portal Marked For You At F5.
  2. Interact With It. Everyone In Game Approves The Teleport By Opening Their Tac Map And Pressing The Respective Button.
  3. In The Dark Aether, Go To Each Of The Four Seal Totems Marked On The Map. Each One Is Shown By An Icon On Your Screen.
  4. Interact With A Totem To Break The Seal, Then Kill Zombies To Fill It Up With Souls. It Tells You When You're Done And A Mini Animation Plays.
  5. Once All Four Are Done, Make Your Way To The Beach Area, Where You Have To Fight A Big Worm.
  6. Once The Worm Is Dead, Collect The Loot (Shown In The Video) And The Reward Rift That Spawns, And Get The Locked Diary. You Get This Every Single Time.
  7. Interact With The Triangular Rift That Spawns To Exfil. You Get A Cutscene.

That Completes The Act 4 Story Mission, But It Is Only Step One Of The Main Quest.

Worm Fight

  • Only Shoot Him Where He's Glowing, Or Where You Get Double Damage Hit Markers.
  • When He Opens His Mouth To Laser You, Shoot Him.
  • He Will Try To Eat, Slam And Shock You. Try To Avoid That.
  • Occasionally Dark Aether Balls Get Spit Out At You. They Explode, But You Can Defuse Them By Shooting Them.
  • There's An Ammo Stash On The Beach, So You Can Refill Your Ammo And Grenades.

The Locked Diary

  • It's Used In The Next Section Of The Easter Egg Quest.
  • To Be Safe, So You Don't Lose It If You Die, Unequip It And Store It In Your Acquisition Stash.
  • You'll Need To Re-Equip It For Your Next Game. To Go To The Dark Aether, You Must Have The Diary Enabled.

Three Gold Items (Any Order)

In Your Next Game You Do A Series Of Mini Steps For Three More Easter Egg Items, In Any Order. Each Item Starts Purple. A Contract From An Aether Tear Turns It Gold. Doghouses, Aether Tears And Harvester Orbs Are Marked On The Urzikstan Cheat Sheet.

  • Set Up In The Menus With Whatever You May Need Or Want, Knowing You Won't Be Fighting Any Bosses Like The Worm This Game.

Aether Tear Contract, The Same For All Three

  1. Find One Of The Pink Aether Tears And Go Into It.
  2. Immediately Deploy Your Parachute.
  3. Fly Your Way Through The Floating Tear In Your Item's Color.
  4. Pay Close Attention To Where You Redeploy: Falling From The Sky Is An Item You Need To Find. It's Actually A Contract.
  5. Run Up To It And Interact With It To Start The Contract.
  6. Complete The Contract. Your Golden Item Is In The Reward Rift.

Dog Collar

  1. Find Your Way To A Doghouse. Interact With It And Place Down A Molotov Or Thermite Inside.
  2. A Dog Spawns From The Doghouse. Killing It Gives You A Purple Dog Collar. Pick It Up.
  3. Aether Tear: Fly Through The Red Tear.
  4. Contract: A Tier 3 Contract In The Red Zone That Requires You To Kill A Beefy Mangler.
  5. Reward Rift: The Golden Dog Collar.
  • I Placed Meat Inside In The Gameplay, But I Have Heard That It Is Not Needed.

Pill Bottle

  1. Make Your Way To An Aether Nest With A Brain Rot Ammo Mod Applied To Your Weapon. Nests Have Their Own Icon On The Map.
  2. Tap Fire One Of The Spores With Your Brain Rot Weapon. Only Shoot One Bullet.
  3. Interact With The Spore And Grab The Purple Pill Bottle From It.
  4. Aether Tear: Fly Through The Green Tear.
  5. Contract: A Mimic Bounty Contract.
  6. Reward Rift: The Golden Pill Bottle.

Camera

  1. Make Your Way To A Harvester Orb With The Dead Wire Ammo Mod Applied To Your Weapon.
  2. Shoot It With Your Dead Wire Weapon. You'll Notice It Turns Yellow.
  3. You Need To Deal The Finishing Blow With A Dead Wire Weapon. Other People Or Teammates Can Help Reduce Its Health, But The Final Blow Must Come From A Dead Wire Weapon.
  4. If Done Correctly, The Purple Camera Comes Out Of The Harvester Orb.
  5. Aether Tear: Fly Through The Yellow Tear.
  6. Contract: A Disciple Bounty Contract.
  7. Reward Rift: The Golden Camera.

Then Exfil. Your Game Is Probably Near Finished, So I'd Highly Recommend It. You Can Carry Over All The Easter Egg Quest Items From Game To Game So Long As You Successfully Exfil. Your Rucksack Should Now Have The Golden Camera, Pill Bottle And Dog Collar, And The Diary If You Carried It Into This Game.

The Pedestals

  1. Start Up Another Game. If You Have The Diary And The Other Three Items In This Game, You Can Also Go Into The Dark Aether In This Game.
  2. Prepare For Extreme Combat: Any Schematics, Perks And Tier 3 Pack-A-Punch Guns You Feel You Need. This Area Is Very Difficult To Survive In.
  3. Make Your Way To The Aether Storm At F5. It's Directly Across From The Rift You Went Into For The Worm Fight. Here There Are Four Pedestals.
  4. Place Each Of The Four Items Into The Correct Pedestal, The Same Way You Put Meat Into A Doghouse. From Left To Right:
  • Locked Diary: Cryo Freeze Pedestal
  • Pill Bottle: Brain Rot Pedestal
  • Camera: Dead Wire Pedestal
  • Dog Collar: Napalm Burst Pedestal

After All Four

  1. A Massive Triangular Dark Aether Rift Spawns With An Abomination Flying Out Of It. Kill It.
  2. Once It's Dead, A Reward Rift Gives You A Dark Aether Rift Sigil.
  3. Place The Sigil Into Its Slot On The Rift.
  4. Everyone Needs To Vote Yes. Then You're Teleported Into The Dark Aether.

Sigil Run: Three Bunny Contracts

Upon Spawning, You'll See Three Bunnies Scatter Themselves Throughout The Map. Each Bunny Is A Contract. Bunny Spots Are On The Bad Signal Cheat Sheet.

  • Aether Reactors: The Bunny That Spawns To Your Left At Spawn, On Top Of A Building. Interact With It To Start The Contract, Then Interact With Each Marked Aether Reactor After Taking Out Any Zombies Or Bosses Around It. You Must Disable All Three.
  • Escort: The Bunny On Top Of The Castle Looking Building. Take The Tank Through Its Three Checkpoints Without Zombies Destroying It.
  • Outlast: The Bunny Right At The Front Of The Bus. Interact With The Bunny, Head To The Marker On The Map And Survive The Area.
  • Loot And Stash Anything And Everything You May Want Or Need From Each Reward Rift, Including Sigils.
  • Elder Sigils: You'll Get These From One Or More Of These Reward Rifts. They're Used For Yet Another Layer Of This Easter Egg. If You See Them, Store Them In Your Rucksack. They Are Extremely Important.
  • Once You've Completed All Three Contracts, Exfil By Going To One Of The Triangular Rifts. There's One At The Top And One At The Bottom Of The Map.
  • If You Fail A Contract, You Can Still Exfil To Keep Your Stuff.

Elder Sigil Run: Classified Schematics

  1. Exfil With Your Elder Sigil In Your Rucksack.
  2. Spawn Into Your Next Game And Get Set Up First: Tier 3 Pack-A-Punch, Perks, Whatever You May Need.
  3. Make Your Way To The Triangular Dark Aether Rift By The Pedestals Again.
  4. This Time Go To The Opposite Side From The Side You Inserted The Sigil In Last Time, And Place Down The Elder Sigil. One Side Is For The Sigil And The Other Side Is For The Elder Sigil.
  5. Same Deal: Everyone Has To Approve The Teleport.
  6. Everything Is Much Harder In Here, The Contracts Are Much More Difficult, And You Only Have 15 Minutes.
  7. The Objective Is Still Exactly The Same: Complete All Three Contracts. Each One Gives A Reward Rift With Schematics And/Or Crates For The Classified Items.
  8. When You Complete Them Or Need To Exfil, Make Your Way To The Triangular Rifts To Exfil And Keep All Those Items In Your Rucksack.
  • The Classified Schematics From This Dark Aether: Aether Blade, Dog Bone And Golden Armor Plates.

Sigils And Elder Sigils

  • Sigil: You Get Regular Sigils From Tier 3 Contracts. They Get You Into The Regular Dark Aether.
  • Elder Sigil: You Get Them By Completing Contracts In The Dark Aether You Used Regular Sigils To Get Into. Place One On The Other Side To Get Into The Advanced Version, With The Classified Schematics.
