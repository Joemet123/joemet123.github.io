MW3 Zombies Free Perk Easter Eggs Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-11

The Urzikstan Cheat Sheet Shows Where Every Single Perk Easter Egg On The Map Takes Place.

How It Works

  • When Done Right, A Completion Noise Will Play And Inside The Spawn Portal Will Be Your Free Perk.
  • Do Each Free Perk Easter Egg For A Perk-A-Holic: Every Single Perk In The Game, For Free.
  • Every One Is Guaranteed Except Elemental Pop.

Guaranteed Perks

  • Death Perception (E1): Make Your Way To The Tower Shown In The Video. Climb Up The Main Ladder, Loop Around The Catwalk And Climb Up The Second Ladder To The Top. Directly Across You'll See Three Rings. Jump Off The Tower, Immediately Deploy Your Parachute And Fly Through All Three Rings. If Done Correctly, A Completion Noise Plays When You Land.
  • Speed Cola (D2): Make Your Way To D2 With A Vehicle. With This Vehicle, Go Up And Off The Top Of The Ramp Shown In The Video. When You Land, A Completion Noise Will Play.
  • Juggernog (C3): At C3 You'll Find A Campfire Next To Some Chairs. All You Have To Do Is Throw A Molotov Onto The Campfire.
  • Stamin-Up (D7): Go Into And Run Up The Stairs Of The Building Shown In The Video As Fast As Possible. It's Recommended To Drop Your Weapon First: This Way You Actually Do Run Faster. You Can Always Pick It Up Right After.
  • PhD Flopper (H7): Head To The Roof Of The Mansion Shown In The Video, Then Onto The Wood Scaffolding. Run And Jump Off The Wood Into The Pool. Tip: Drop Your Weapon Temporarily And Stand Between The Two Pieces Of Wood Shown In The Video. That's Where I've Had The Highest Success Rate. It May Take A Few Tries.
  • Quick Revive (I5): Bring Either The Two-Wheeler Motorcycle Or The Four-Wheeler ATV. No Other Vehicle Will Work. Go Into The Vehicle Under The Bunny On The Highway Sign, Lined Up Right Underneath The Post, And Wait For The Laugh. No Laugh: Get Out Of The Vehicle, Go Back In And Wait For It. Don't Let The Zombies Destroy The Vehicle. Once You Hear The Laugh, Hold Down The Drive Button And Race Directly To And Through The Other Sign With The Other Bunny. This Can Be Really Quick, Or Take You An Entire Game Because Of The Need For Near Perfection.
  • Deadshot Daiquiri (I3): You Need Either A Frag Or A Semtex Grenade. Throw It Through The Church Window, Timed As Shown In The Video. If You Fail, There's An Ammo Cache Right Next To You To Refill Your Grenades.
  • Tombstone (G7): Bring A Sniper. Spawning In With One Is What I Prefer And Recommend, Or You Can Loot One. Stand On The Roof Of The Building Shown In The Video And Look Across The Map Towards The Crane. On It You'll See The Blue Aether Bunny. Aim Down Sights With The Sniper For As Long As Possible, Until The Aether Bunny Jump Scares You.

Elemental Pop: 30% Chance

  • Elemental Pop (E4): Be Careful, It's In The Red Zone. You Will Not Survive Unless You Run With Only Your Fist Out. Go Up To The Grave Shown In The Video And Interact To Pay Respects. Then Check The Spawn Portal.
  • It Will Strip You Of Your Weapon Rarity, Ammo Mods And Pack-A-Punch. You'll Still Have Your Weapon, But None Of The Stuff On It.
  • It's A 30% Chance Of Your Perk, Not Guaranteed Like The Others, Because This One Is Tied To Another Easter Egg Which Allows You To Carry Over Weapons To Your Next Game.
  • If You Do Get A Free Perk, It Will Always Be Elemental Pop.
