BO7 Zombies Loadouts Beginners Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-10-08

Every Single Thing You Need To Know About BO7 Zombies Loadouts, In The Order You Use It.

Unlock Loadouts

  • You Need To Be Level 4.
  • Hit The Tabs On The Top, Then Select Loadouts.
  • At Level 4 You're Not Going To Have Many Weapons Unlocked, Or Any Attachments At All.

Field Upgrade, Lethal, Tactical

  • Pick Whatever One You Want From Each List.
  • Field Upgrade Example: Healing Aura.
Advertisement

Melee

  • Your Melee Slot. The Default Is The Knife.
  • Just Like With Weapons, You Can Select The Melee Weapon To Apply Attachments.
  • I Believe Every Single Melee Weapon Is Just A Camo.

GobbleGum Pack

  • Scroll Down. Some Gums Sit Below The Visible List, Legendary Ones Like Crate Power Included. That's Very Easy To Miss.
  • They Did Add A Couple Of Brand New Gums In BO7, Some Of Them Really Powerful.
  • The First Three Gums Are The Ones You Spawn In With Every Game.
  • The Last Two Will Always Be In The GobbleGum Machine In Game.

Move A Gum To Another Slot

  • You Can't Just Reorder Your Gums.
  • A Gum That's Already Checked In Your Pack Can't Be Moved To Another Slot.
  1. Select The Slot To Fill.
  2. If The Gum You Want Is In Another Slot, Swap That Slot Out For A Different Gum That's Not Currently In Your Loadout.
  3. Come Back To The Slot To Fill And Select The Gum.

Main Weapon And Gunsmith

  • To Change Your Weapon, Select It And Pick Whatever Other Weapon You Want.
  • To Modify It, Route 1: Hit The Gunsmith Keybind Shown At The Bottom Of The Loadout Screen.
  • Route 2: Select Your Loadout Weapon, Select The Weapon You Want To Modify, Then Equip Weapon And Open Gunsmith.
  • In The Gunsmith: Attachments And Camos.

Zombie Builds

  • You Can Have Multiple Different Variants Of A Single Weapon.
  • The Version In Your Loadout Slot Is Only What You Spawn In With.
  • Your Zombie Build Is The Version You Get Off The Wall Or Out Of The Box.
  1. Open The Weapon.
  2. Pick The Version You Want.
  3. Press The Button Shown At The Bottom Of The Screen (On My Setup It's 1).
  4. A Purple Z Next To It Means That's Now Your Zombie Build.
  • Off The Wall Or Out Of The Box, It's Always The Zombie Build Version, Even If You Spawned In With Another.
  • Once You Start To Unlock Weapons And Attachments, Go Into Every Single Weapon And Set Its Zombie Build. Keep Slowly Editing It Afterwards.

Weapon Build Codes

  • Every Weapon Build Has A Build Code.

Copy Your Code

  1. Open The Weapon In The Gunsmith.
  2. Find The Build Code.
  3. Hit The Button Next To It To Copy It To Your Clipboard.
  4. Paste It In Discord, On Twitter Or In A YouTube Description.

Missing Attachments

  • A Shared Build Has An Attachment You Haven't Unlocked Yet? The Game Asks You What To Do.
  • Equip The Current Build: Removes The Locked Attachment. Later You Have To Get Your Friend To Reshare That Build Code, Or Try To Remember The Attachment.
  • Duplicate The Build: Removes Whatever Attachments You Don't Have Unlocked, And You Go On With The Ones You Do Have.
  • In Almost All Cases, I Recommend You Duplicate It. As Far As I'm Aware, That Build Stays There Forever, So Once You Unlock The Attachments You Can Start Using It.

Import A Build

  1. Pick Literally Any Weapon In The Loadout Menu.
  2. Hit The Weapon Build Code Button.
  3. Type In The Code. The Attachments Show.
  4. It Asks If You Want To Save The Shared Build: Yes.
  • It's Added To Your List Of Builds For That Weapon.
  • All The Attachments Carry Over. The Camo Doesn't.

Share With A Friend

  1. Hover Over The Weapon In Your Loadouts.
  2. Hit Options, Then Share Weapon Build.
  3. Check Your Friends.
  • They Get A Notification In The Top Right-Hand Corner.
  • If They Click It, It Pops Up The Same Import Screen. They Just Hit Save Weapon Build.
  • Not Friends: Whoever Has The Build Goes To The Gunsmith, Where The Build Code Is Right There To Share. Or Hit Options On The Weapon And Just Copy The Build Code.

Multiplayer

  • This Weapon Build System And Sharing Builds Work Exactly The Same In Multiplayer.
  • You Can Share A Class With Each Other And Try It Out In A Different Game Mode.

Camos

  • Gunsmith, Customize, Then Camos.
  • It Appears That None Of The Customize Stuff, Camos And Skins, Comes Over When You Share A Weapon Build. It's Purely The Attachments.
