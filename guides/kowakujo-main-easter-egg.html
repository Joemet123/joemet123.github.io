Kowakujo Main Easter Egg Guide, All Steps

By Joemet123 · 2026-10-08

All Steps, No BS: Map Basics Like Pack-A-Punch And The Wonder Weapon, Your Flags And Scrolls Order, The Misunderstood Solve The Mystery Step And The Dragon Boss Fight. The Video Above Does It All In Under 10 Minutes.

Before You Start

  • For Max Understanding, Skipping Around Is Not Recommended.
  • If You Ever Fail A Step And Can't Proceed, Go To The Next Round To Try Again.
  • The Solvers And The Full Cheat Sheet For This Map Are Linked In This Guide And On The Kowakujo Tools Page.

Basic Map Setup

Pack-A-Punch

  1. Recapture The Wards By Interacting With The Dragon Flags And Staying Inside Their Spawn Circle Until Complete. One In Training Area, One In Kitchens.
  2. This Unlocks Central Courtyard's Doors.
  3. Head Up To The War Room And Eliminate The Oni To Get Shogun's Hanko.
  4. Open The Doors And Kill Zombies Near The World Seed To Spawn Pack-A-Punch.

Maneki-Neko

Made Up Of Three Parts. Each Part Has A Few Spawns.

  • Cat Statue: Gatehouse At The Crates. Kitchens By Double Tap, On The Barrel. Kitchens On The Barrel Under The Fish.
  • Furin Bell: Staging Area Up High. Stables On The Buried House. Training Area Up Next To The Wall Buy.
  • Karakuri Doll: Storage Rooms Across From PhD. Storage Rooms Down The Hall From PhD, On The Desk. Workshop, Left Of The Staircase.

Build It At The Station Near Vulture Aid, And Repurchase It For 500 Salvage If Needed. It Counts As A Tactical Lethal Equipment.

Nekomancer Wonder Weapon

For This, Have The Maneki-Neko, PhD Flopper And Death Perception.

  1. At The Tenshu Entrance, PhD Dive Underneath The Cage To Plunge It.
  2. Pick It Up, Carry It, Then Drop It In Any Active Lava Pool To Free The Cat.
  3. This Spawns A Volcanic Fissure That'll Consume One Of Four Areas Of The Map. Getting A Kill In That Area's Entranceway Spawns Pawprints. All Four Exact Locations: Stables Before Entering Staging Area, Flower Garden Before The Building, Kitchens Before Central Courtyard, Or Training Area Before Stables.
  4. Kill A Zombie About 7 Meters Away From Your Current Pawprints, In The Direction They're Facing, To Spawn Another Set. Repeat This Until You Get A Circle Of Pawprints.
  5. As Long As The Area Still Has Lava And Hasn't Molten Over, Throw A Maneki-Neko At The Circle To Have An Abomination Spawn That You Must Kill.
  6. Flip The Round. Orange Creaky Floorboards Appear In Training Area, In Kitchens Near The Novaline Wall Buy And In Kitchens Near The Mystery Box.
  7. At One Location A Cat Will Be Sleeping, So Do Not Shoot, Sprint Or Field Upgrade. Just Move Slowly Through The Non-Orange Floorboards And Pick Him Up.
  8. Deliver Him To The World Seed. It'll Emit A Damaging Pulse. Just Time It Between Each Pulse And Melee It Three Separate Times To Have The Wonder Weapon Drop To The Floor.

Fire The Nekomancer Like Any Other Weapon With Your Right Trigger: It Single Fires. A Charged Right Trigger Shoots A Pool To The Ground That Kills For A Few Seconds. All Abilities: Nekomancer And Music Easter Egg.

Board 1

Lanterns

  1. Around The Map, Activate The 11 Lanterns With The Nekomancer In Rapid Succession. Easily Done By Following The Pathway Shown In The Video.
  2. This Spawns A Ghost In War Room. Interact With It.

Fox Mask

  1. When The Ghosts Vanish, Ride The Kite To Tenshu Rooftops.
  2. Immediately Hold D (Or Your Thumbstick To The Right) To Glide On The Alternate Right Pathway, And Interact With The Fox Mask Sitting There.
  3. Place The Mask On The Wall In Storage Rooms To Start A Simon Says. Masks Flash One By One, Showing The Exact Order You Must Kill The Matching Newly Spawned Masked Zombies.
  4. First In Order Are 3, Then 4, Then 5. Interact With The Wall After Each Completed Sequence To Start The Next.
  5. After The Round Of 5, A Cutscene Plays And The Fox Mask Flies Away, To Now Be Shot For Parts In Three Locations: Workshop Gets Takeshi's Pipe, Collapsed Study Gets Takeo's Case And Onsen Baths Get Mitsuhide's Comb.
  6. Interact With The Evidence Shelf At Meditation Room To Spawn Board 1.

Board 2

Coin Purse

  1. Check The Bottom Corners Of The Mystery Box For A Rectangular Symbol Card. If It's Not There, Buy The Box Until It Moves, Which Puts It At The Correct Location.
  2. Throw A Maneki-Neko At The Box First, Then Hit The Box For A Guaranteed Gold Coin Purse.
  3. A Ghost Coin Purse Now Spawns At Outer Ward. Interact For A Cutscene.

Gardening Shears

  1. Get A Water Bucket. Some Spawns Are Tea Garden, Stables And Onsen Baths.
  2. Interact With The Water In The Baths To Fill The Bucket. It Holds Three Fill Levels, But Getting Hit, Sprinting Or Jumping Spills A Level. So Walk To The Flower Garden, Tiptoeing Around Any Lava Or Zombies.
  3. Here, Interact To Water These Three Plants: At The Base Of The Bridge, Down The Pathway To The Left Of The Bridge, And On The Other Side Of The Room Near The Bridge.
  4. The Area's Two Flower Pots Now Glow Yellow And An Immune Gardener Spawns. To Kill Him, First Deal Damage To The Glowing Flower Pots, Then Shoot Him.
  5. This Rewards The Gardener's Shears And Notes.

Mercantile Abacus

  1. At Kitchens, Melee The Basket To Knock Down A Tomato.
  2. Leave It Until The End Of The Round For A Zombie To Stuff It In Its Mouth.
  3. Kill That Zombie At Staging Area In Front Of The Barrier To Spawn The Merchant.
  4. He Regens Health, So Kill Him Fast To Claim His Abacus And Sales Note.

Noble's Hat

  1. At Staging Area, Throw One Decoy At Each Of The Three Windows Within About A 10 Second Time Frame To Break Them.
  2. The Noble Zombie Now Throws Grenades At You. Just Pick Them Up And Throw Them Back At Him Through The Windows.
  3. After Enough Hits, He Drops The Noble's Hat And Paper Evidence.

Place All Of This On The Shelf At Meditation Room To Spawn Board 2.

Board 3

Monkshood Flower

  1. At Flower Garden, Grab The Flower And Place It In The Pot At The Tenshu Entrance.
  2. At Training Area, Charge Shoot The Pouch With The Nekomancer. Pick It Up.
  3. When A Volcanic Fissure Consumes Any Area, Stay Inside It For About 50 Seconds To Fill The Pouch With Ash.
  4. This Ash Can Be Placed Into The Tenshu Plant.
  5. Find And Repeatedly Melee A Cherry Blossom Tree With Leaves Until You Get An Orange Glow. This Lets You Spawn A Tornado By Running In A Circle.
  6. Do This In Front Of The Plant To Get The Finished Flower.

Pufferfish

  1. Melee The Scrolls In Storage Rooms To Start The Puzzle Mini Game. A Maneki-Neko Spawns Next To You That Chases Away All Zombies.
  2. Shoot Or Melee A Scroll To Move It In Tandem With A Few Others. The Ultimate Goal Is To Have All Of Them Pushed Inward. Finish This Within 10 Seconds With Zero Thinking Using The Scroll Grid Solver: Inspect The Scrolls From The Side, Select All Facing Outward And Hit Solve.
  3. Completing This Gives Paper Evidence And A Pestle.
  4. Place The Pestle In The Bowl At Kitchens To Begin A Soul Collection. Only Brain Rot Ammo Modded Weapons Or Charged Nekomancer Shots Work For This.
  5. After Enough Kills, Interact With The Ghost Pestle For A Mini Cutscene. At Its End, Pick Up The Pufferfish To Your Left.

Plum Pit

  1. At Kitchens, Melee The Basket To Knock Over A Green Plum.
  2. Keep Jumping On It Until You Get The Pit.

Place All Evidence On The Shelf To Reveal Board 3.

Board 4

  1. Grab Two Shard Fragments: One Directly Off Board 3 And One On The Floor At War Room's Door. Also Grab The Evidence To The Left Of It.
  2. At Tea Garden, Assemble A Cup On The Table.
  3. Flip The Round, Then Interact With The Ghost Cup For A Cutscene.
  4. When It Ends, Grab The Cup And Place It In These Three Locations In Any Order To Start A Defend. If You Fail, Rebuild The Cup Next Round To Try Again. Completion Gives An Item And Your Cup Back.
  5. War Room Gets The Horse Silhouette. Collapsed Study Gets The Calligraphy Brush.
  6. Tea Garden Has A Dog Steal The Item And Run Away Very Fast In A Loop. Go In The Opposite Direction To Catch It, Most Easily From The Hillside Pathway, To Get The Tea Whisk.

Place All New Evidence On The Shelf To Reveal Board 4.

Board 5

Netsuke Of Brothers Statue

  1. At Stables, Wait For The Lava To Harden.
  2. Throw An Explosive At The Tiny Statue To Knock It Loose, Then Grab It.

Crest Medallion

  1. Interact With The Clock In Storage Rooms. A Repeatable Sequence Of Four Numbers Plays.
  2. Around The Map, Blue Symbols Appear At Stables, Outer Ward, Flower Garden And Central Courtyard. Each Symbol Matches A Position In Your Clock Sequence, 1, 2, 3 Or 4. As An Example, Symbol 3 Equals The Third Number In The Clock Sequence.
  3. Progress Rounds Until You Get A Defense Round, Which Spawns Six Flag Zombies. Killing Them Sends Their Flags To Staging Area.
  4. Flags Can Be Picked Up, Dropped And Swapped At Any Of The Holders In Front Of The Symbols Or Back At Staging Area. Flag Values Equal The Count Of Whatever Symbol Is On Them, Whether It Be Circles, Stars Or Anything Else.
  5. All Six Flags Must Be Used, With Either One Or Two Flags Placed Per Location, Adding Together To Equal The Clock Number For That Position.
  6. Example From My Game: Symbol 1, Tied To A First Clock Value Of 5, Had A Flag Combo Of 2 And 3. My Second Clock Number Was 11, So In Front Of Symbol 2 I Put 5 And 6. Since I'd Used Four Flags So Far, My Last Two Symbols Each Had Only One Flag, Still Matching The Clock Value For That Symbol.
  7. If Done Correctly, Interacting Back At The Clock Gets You A Medallion And Paper.

Place All Evidence On The Shelf To Reveal Board 5.

Solve The Mystery

Two Quick Tips: Activating The Left Incense Stops Zombies From Attacking For 60 Seconds (Just Don't Shoot), And You Can Quickly And Easily Solve Your Mystery With The Mystery Solver. Just Input Your Game's Info And It Returns Your Five Items Plus The Dial Position.

Slots 1 And 5

Portrait Slots 1 And 5 Are The Same Every Game. Slot 1 Is The Comb And Slot 5 Is The Crest Medallion. Place Them In Their Slots.

Slot 2

Each Of The Three Traps Requires 15 Kills To Spawn A Ghost Behind It, With One Of The Ghosts Pointing Out A Specific Accomplice. Take That Accomplice's Item From The Shelf And Place It In Slot 2.

  • Gardener: The Gardening Shears
  • Noble Or Courtier: The Noble's Hat
  • Merchant: The Mercantile Abacus

Slot 3

  1. Read The Doctor's Note (Note 1) To Determine The Cause Of Death And Symptoms.
  2. Read The Toxin Note (Note 5) To Match Note 1's Symptoms To It. Example: If You See Paralysis As A Symptom, That Rules Out Plum Pits, Since Paralysis Is Not A Symptom Of It.
  3. This Narrows It Down To Two Items, And Finding The Actual Item Is Down To Your Accomplice. If It Was The Noble, Your Item Can Never Be Plum Pits. If It Was The Merchant, It Can Never Be Flower. If It Was The Gardener, It Can Never Be Pufferfish.

Example: I Had Emesis, Which Reduced My Options To Plum Pits Or Pufferfish. My Accomplice Was The Gardener, And Since The Gardener Can Never Be Pufferfish, It Had To Be Plum Pits.

Slot 4

The Fourth Portrait Can Be Worked Out By Inspecting The Picture On It.

  • Fish: Tea Whisk
  • Mountains: Horse Silhouette
  • Bird: Calligraphy Brush

Animal Dial

  1. Re-Inspect Your Notes. In Note 5, Check The Amount Of Hours Your Toxin (The Item You Placed In Slot 3) Needs To Take Effect. In Note 1, Check The Time Of Death, Listed As "Hour Of The" And Then An Animal.
  2. On The Dial To The Left Of The Portraits, Find Your Animal, Then Place The Pointer Backwards The Amount Of Hours Note 5 Told You To.
  3. Interact With The Incense On The Right Side Of The Portraits To Lock It In. If Incorrect, Bosses Spawn And Force You To The Next Round.
  4. Success Spawns An Orange Orb That Floats Into The World Seed.

Lockdown

Interact With The World Seed To Enter A Lockdown. The Goal Is To Defeat The Heavy Oni Boss. If He Disappears, Shoot All The Red Floating Masks To Bring Him Back.

Boss Fight

To Enter The Nyxara Boss Fight, Interact With The New Kite Near Pack-A-Punch.

A Fairly Simple Three-Stage Fight With A New Mechanic: Flags. Just Take Out An Oni Boss, Grab His Flag, Then Stand In A Red Circle Until It's Charged. Once All Zones Are Captured In Any Phase, A Blue Aether Shroud Bubble Spawns That Protects Against All Incoming Attacks And Lets You Deal Way More Damage.

Blessings On Pedestals

  • Veytharion: Some Weaponry Upgrades
  • Dravakar: Instantly Captures All Flag Zones
  • Caltheris: Makes You Spew Out Fireballs

Your Primary Goals

  • Use The Flag Circles For Increased Damage And Protection.
  • Target Nyxara's Orange Weak Spots.
  • Use The Blessings.
  • Sometimes Use The Larger Boulders As Safe Zones.
  • Best Avoid Attacks By Constantly Watching Nyxara.

Attacks To Watch For

  • A Trio Of Fireballs And Raining Rocks
  • A Targeted Laser Attack That Explodes Along Its Seam
  • Fire On The Floor
  • Tornadoes, Which You Must Sprint Directly Away From

Phase 1 Only Has One Weak Spot And One Flag Location. With Each Phase After That, One More Flag And Weak Spot Are Revealed.
