Kowakujo Portraits Step, All 5 Correct Items

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-09

How To Solve The Mystery Step Of The Kowakujo Main Quest As Easy As Can Be: Five Slots, Then The Dial.

Before You Start

  • Fast Way: The Mystery Step Solver. Select All Your Info With The Buttons. It Gives You Back Your Items And The Dial Position.
  • Same Round: Do The Entire Mystery Solving Step On The Same Round You Do The Ghost Traps. Otherwise It Could Change Who Your Accomplice Is, Which Means Your Associated Item Changes, And The Item In Slot 3.
  • Left Incense: Activating The Incense On The Left Is Never Actually Necessary. But As Long As You Don't Shoot Any Zombies After Interacting With It, It Should Stop Zombies From Attacking You And Anyone Else In The Room For About 60 Seconds.
  • Order: Start With Slot 2.

Slot 2: The Accomplice

  1. Allow Each Of The Three Traps To Get About 10 To 20 Zombie Kills, Until A Ghost Shows Up Behind The Trap.
  2. One Of The Three Ghosts Tells You Which Of The Three Suspects They Believe Could Be Guilty. That's Your Accomplice.
  3. Take Your Accomplice's Item From The Shelf And Place It In Slot 2.

Traps And Their Ghosts

  • Flower Garden Trap: Ghost Appears Upstairs In The Kitchens.
  • Outer Ward Trap: Ghost Appears In The Staging Area.
  • Central Courtyard Trap: Ghost Appears In Tenshu Entrance.

Accomplice Items

  • Noble: Noble's Hat. If He Says The Courtier, That's Just Another Word For Noble.
  • Merchant: Mercantile Abacus
  • Gardener: Gardener's Shears

Slot 3: The Toxin

  1. Read The Doctor's Note, Note 1 (The Court Doctor's Record), To Determine The Cause Of Death And Symptoms.
  2. Read The Toxin Note, Note 5 (Known Toxins), To Match The Symptoms Of Note 1 To It.
  3. This Should Narrow It Down To Two Items. Finding The Actual Item Is Down To Your Accomplice.
  4. Place It In Slot 3.

What The Accomplice Rules Out

  • Noble: Never Plum Pit
  • Merchant: Never Monkshood Flower
  • Gardener: Never Pufferfish

Examples

  • If You See Paralysis As A Symptom, That Rules Out Plum Pit, Since Paralysis Is Not A Symptom Of Plum Pit.
  • I Had Emesis, Which Reduced My Options To The Plum Pit Or Pufferfish. But My Accomplice Was The Gardener, And Since The Gardener Can Never Be Pufferfish, It Has To Be Plum Pit.

Slot 4: The Picture

Inspect The Little Picture On The Fourth Portrait.

  • Fish: Tea Whisk
  • Mountains: Horse Statuette
  • Bird: Calligraphy Brush

Slots 1 And 5

They Never Change. Same Every Single Game.

  • Slot 1: Always The Comb
  • Slot 5: Always The Crest Medallion

Set The Animal Dial

  1. Re-Inspect Note 5 And Find Your Toxin, The Item You Placed In Front Of Portrait 3. Read The Amount Of Hours It Takes To Take Effect.
  2. Re-Inspect Note 1. It Displays The Time Of Death As "Hour Of The" And Then An Animal.
  3. Go To The Dial Right Next To The Portraits. Find That Animal.
  4. Count Backwards The Amount Of Hours Note 5 Told You To.
  5. Place The Pointer At That Time By Interacting Repeatedly Until It Gets To That Position.
  • Just To Be Clear: It Does Not Go On The Hour Of The Animal From Note 1. It Goes Back The Amount Of Hours Stated In Note 5 From That Animal.
  • The Animals Around The Dial Are On The Kowakujo Cheat Sheet.

Finish The Step

To Complete This Step, Interact With The Incense On The Right Side Of The Room.

  • Success: Spawns An Orange Orb To Proceed With The Easter Egg.
  • Failure: Spawns Bosses And Forces You To Wait Until The Next Round To Try Again.

The Rest Of The Quest Is In The Kowakujo Main Quest Guide.
