Reckoning Gorgofex Guide, All 3 Variants

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-09

Build The Base Gorgofex, Then Pick One Upgrade: Blue Gorgofex-C Or Yellow Gorgofex-N.

Before You Start

  • As With Everything Else On This Map, The First Thing You Must Do Is Turn On Power And Pack-A-Punch By Following The On-Screen Prompts.
  • You Can Only Choose One Gorgofex Upgrade Path. Once One Is Chosen, The Other One Is Completely Disabled.
  • Good News: After Building Any Variant, Variants Will Be Available From The Mystery Box For All Team Members.

Base Gorgofex

Step 1: Door Code

  1. Check Two Static Monitors In The T1 Mutant Research Lab For A Word: One To The Left Of Deadshot Daiquiri, One In The Periodic Room.
  2. Note The First Letter Of The Word On Each Monitor.
  3. Combine The Two Letters, Always Putting The Deadshot Letter First. That Gets You An Element On The Periodic Table.
  4. Check The Periodic Table In The Room And Match Your Element To It. The Atomic Number Is In The Top Left Hand Corner Of Its Square. Or Type Your Letters Into The Gorgofex Solver And Your Code Is There Instantly.
  5. The Code Is Three Digits. If Your Atomic Number Is One Or Two Digits, Simply Add One Or Two Zeros In Front To Fill In The Spaces.
  6. Input Your Final Code Into The Keypad Up The Stairs. Door Unlocked.
  • No Word On A Monitor: Don't Sweat, That's Sometimes Normal. Just Stare At It For A Good 20 Seconds Before You Draw That Conclusion. No Word Equals No First Letter. In The Solver, Leave That Field Blank.
  • Example: U Alone Is 92. Code: 092.
  • Example: P Alone Is 15. Throw A Zero In Front: 015.
  • Wrong Code Twice: If You Input The Wrong Number Two Times By Accident, Your Monitor Words, And So Your Final Code, May Change. Just Flip The Round To Confirm.

Step 2: Charge The Spore

  1. Head Into The Unlocked Room, The T1 Bioweapons Lab. Stand In Front Of The Yellow Spore, And I Mean Very Close To It.
  2. Slowly Kill A Set Of Three Spiders, Each One At A Time.
  3. Then Kill A Set Of Three Regular Zombies, Each One At A Time.
  4. After The Soul Hits The Top Of The Machine, Sucking It In Takes About Six Seconds. The Spore Does An Animation Ending With A Mini Poof That You'll Also Hear. After That, You Can Get The Next Kill.
  5. Hold Interact On The Spore. When It's Charged Completely, You're Able To Pick It Up.
  • Spiders: An Easier Way Of Getting Spiders To Spawn, I've Noticed, Is To Just Damage Zombies By Hip Fire Or Use The Napalm Burst Ammo Mod While Shooting Them. The Spiders Naturally Pop Out.

Step 3: Mushrooms

  1. Make Your Way To The T1 Quantum Computing Core With The Spore.
  2. There Are Three Distinct Mushrooms. Run Up To Each One And The Spore Pops Out, Sucking In Essence.
  3. Finish All Three.
  • About To Die: Simply Run Away And Come Back At Full Health. You Pick Up From Where You Left Off.
  • You May Even Cycle The Three Mushrooms With No Issue, Taking A Little Essence From Each One At A Time.

Step 4: Power Surge And Uber Klaus

  1. Go To T2 Dark Entity Containment.
  2. Initiate A Power Surge On Four Computers Within The Time Limit. They All Read "Initiate Power Surge" In Massive Green Text.
  3. Always Start With The Computer Shown In The Video. The Rest Can Be Hit In Any Order. It's Easiest To Just Circle The Arena.
  4. If You Somehow Fail, Start Over Again By Reactivating The First Computer.
  5. An Uber Klaus Spawns. Destroy Only His Shoulder Panels. Killing Him Forces You To Go To The Next Round To Try Again.
  6. Take Him To The Electrified Pad In Front Of The Forsaken, So The Forsaken Possesses Him.
  7. Survive All Other Enemies In The Area. Eliminate The Forsaken Uber Klaus, Then The Mini One That Pops Out Of Him.
  8. An Orb Falls Where It Died. Place Your Spore From Earlier Into It.
  9. After A Quick Animation, Your Base Gorgofex Is Ready For Pickup.

Gorgofex-C (Blue)

  1. Speak With Blanchard At The T1 Executive Suites, Next To Juggernog.
  2. In Spawn, At The T1 Project Janus Reception, Interact With The Red Button Next To The Portal.
  3. A Friendly Klaus Spawns And Works On A Computer. Defend Him From Attacking Zombies Long Enough And He Disappears.
  4. If You Fail, Start The Process Again By Hitting The Red Button Next Round.
  5. When He's Done, The Elevator To The T1 Quantum Computing Core Shuts Down.
  6. Take The Other Elevator Up To The T1 Mutant Research Lab. Jump Down Through The Hole In The Floor And You'll Be At The T1 Quantum Computing Core.
  7. Locate The Shut-Down Aether Elevator And Jump Into It, Deploying Your Parachute Immediately.
  8. On Your Way Down, Interact With Three Easy-To-Spot Red Buttons To Re-Enable The Elevator. Each One Turns Green And You're Sent Back Up.
  9. A Blue Orb Floats In Front Of You In The Air. Wait For It To Blast Away Into Multiple Orbs. Zombies Get Infected By The Orbs And Glow Blue.
  10. Kill Enough Of These Blue-Glowing Zombies, Specifically With The Gorgofex.
  11. Panos Spawns In The Room. Interact With Him. When The Animation Finishes, You Have The Gorgofex-C.

Gorgofex-N (Yellow)

Find The Vial

  1. Speak With Blanchard At The T1 Executive Suites, Next To Juggernog.
  2. In The T1 Quantum Computing Core, Grab A Straus Counter. Hold Your Tactical Button To Hold It Out.
  3. Use It To Find The Life Drain Essence Vial. You'll Never Physically See It, And It Can Be Practically Anywhere On The Map, Including Out-Of-Bounds, Unreachable Areas.
  4. Follow The Readings Below.
  5. Once You Think You Found The Location, Shoot A Charged Shot Of The Gorgofex At It. The Vial Pops Out For You To Pick Up.

Counter Readings

  • Out Of Range: Go To The Other Tower.
  • Low: You're In The Correct Tower, Just The Wrong Side Or Floor. Check The Other Side Of The Area You're In, Or Go Upward Or Downward In That Tower.
  • High, Around 9.5: You're In The Right Spot.
  • Even If It's Red, You Need To Be Looking In The Right Direction To Get The Highest Number Possible.

Build The Essence Extractor

Once, And Only Once, You Have The Vial, Pick Up Three Parts:

  • Clean Hood: T1 Bioweapons Lab, Where You Did The Spore.
  • PC Fan: T2 Dark Entity Containment, To The Left Of The Arsenal Machine.
  • Tubing: Outside The T1 Quantum Computing Lab.
  1. Return To Blanchard's Door At The T1 Executive Suites, Where You Interacted With Him. Assemble The Essence Extractor.
  2. Place The Life Drain Essence Vial On Top Of It.
  3. This Triggers A Lockdown With Faster Zombies. It's A Little Bit Of A Challenge, But Easier Than You May Think. Just Be Careful And You'll Be Good.
  4. Blanchard Steps Out Of The Room And Vanishes. Pick Up Blanchard's Life Essence Vial From The Extractor.
  5. At The Machine Next To Where You Charged The Spore, In The T1 Bioweapons Lab, Hold Interact With The Gorgofex Out To Build The Gorgofex-N.
