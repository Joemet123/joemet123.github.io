MW3 Zombies Bad Signal Keys & Secret Rooms

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-11

Also On This Map: Bad Signal Dark Aether Guide. Key And Door Spots: Bad Signal Cheat Sheet.

How It Works

  • Reward: In Every Door There's A Chance At A Wonder Weapon Case (Ray Gun, Wunderwaffe, Even V-R11) And A Chance At A Power-Up. Up To Five Wonder Weapon Cases If You Have The Best Luck In The Entire World. In My Games I Usually Get Two To Three.
  1. You Must Have Completed Act 4 And Its Main Easter Egg Quest To Enter The Dark Aether: Bad Signal Dark Aether Guide.
  2. Get Sigils. The Fastest Way Is Completing Red Zone Contracts.
  3. Place Them In The Dark Aether Teleporter At The Aether Storm On The Map.
  4. Collect All Keys. You'll Always Be Able To Get Every Single Key, But It Could Be The Wrong Key. Collecting All Of Them Gives You The Best Chance Of Avoiding Stuff Not Opening.
  5. For Each Secret Room, Go Up To It And Hold Interact To Use Your Keys To Unlock The Doors.

Keys

  • Al Bagra Officers Quarters (2 Doors Next To Each Other, So 2 Keys): On The Table In The Ticket Office; From The Doghouse; At The Top Of The Building Next To The One Behind Pack-A-Punch (Parkour Route Below).
  • Doghouse Key: Insert Three Pieces Of Meat Into The Doghouse. You Don't Need Four. The House Explodes, Gives You A Dog And Drops A Key.
  • Al Bagra Munitions Store Room (2 Doors Next To Each Other, So 2 Keys): On A Pillar On The Other Side Of The Parkour Building (Parkour Route Below); On The Desk At The Little Office.
  • ABF Antiquities Office (1 Door): The Key Is Marked On A Well. Drop Down The Well And Pick Up The Key Right From The Top Of The Crate.
  • South Bagra Top Floor Bedroom (1 Door): Make Your Way To The Pack-A-Punch Area And Drop Down Below The Surface. There's An Aether Nest: Shoot All The Spores In The Room. On The Final Spore, A Key Pops Out. If The Final Spore Was Over The Water, Your Key's Going To Be In The Water, So Go Fish It Out.
  • Al Bagra Barracks (1 Door): At The House Shown In The Video. Go Right Through The Window. The Key Is On The Table.

Parkour Route

  1. Climb Up The Ladder Of The Pillar By The Floating Vehicles, To The Top Of The Building Behind Pack-A-Punch.
  2. Wait For The Vehicle To Lower Enough For You To Jump Onto It.
  3. Wait Again For It To Tilt Enough For You To Run And Jump Onto The Center Of It.
  4. Leap Onto The Pillar Of The Next Building To Get The Key.
  5. Continue To Parkour Forward Onto The Only Other Vehicles, Again Waiting For Them To Lower Enough. You'll Be Led To A Pillar On The Other Side Of The Same Building With Another Key. This More Than Likely Will Be A Munitions Store Room Key.
  • Your Other Options For Both Keys: Use The Scorcher Or The Aether Tear (On The Sheet) To Fly There. There's Really No Need For That, Though.

Doors

  • Al Bagra Barracks: Bottom Floor Of The Building On The Sheet.
  • South Bagra Top Floor Bedroom: In The Building On The Sheet, On The Third Floor.
  • ABF Antiquities Office: In The Room Of The Outlast Contract, In The Hallways Up The Stairs. The Drop Rate For A Full Power Here Is Extremely High, If Not Guaranteed. It Seems To Spawn There Every Single Game.
  • Al Bagra Munitions Store Room: Bottom Floor Of The Parkour Building. Two Doors. In One Of Them You'll Find A Power-Up.
  • Al Bagra Officers Quarters: Up The Stairs, One Level From The Outlast Contract And Antiquities Room. Two Doors. It Seems Like A Max Ammo Is, If Not Guaranteed, Nearly Guaranteed To Spawn Here.

Tips

  • If You Have Death Perception, You Can Take A Sneak Peek Behind Some Of The Doors Before You Even Get The Keys, To See If There's A Wonder Weapon Case Sitting There. Power-Ups Don't Show.
  • With Some Doors The Power-Up Is Even Guaranteed.
  • I Do Not Recommend Doing This In The Elder Sigil Dark Aether. There Are More And Faster Zombies And You Have Less Time. It's Just Not Worth It.
  • I'd Highly Recommend The Grenade Launcher And Maybe The Scorcher.
