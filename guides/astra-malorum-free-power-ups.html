Astra Malorum Free Power-Ups, All 9 Locations

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-09

These Can Be Shot In Any Order, Except For The Random Perk And Fire Sale.

The 7 Standard Drops

  • Full Power: The Observatory Dome, Which Is The Pack-A-Punch Room. Go To The Side Of The Room Shown In The Video And You'll See It On The Machine Near The Top. Stand On The Crate There To More Easily See It.
  • Max Ammo: The Luminarium, In The Barrier, Up On The Ledge. You Can Actually See It From Either Side: If You Go Around The Bend And Look At The Barrier There, You'll See That It's The Same Opening As The Other Side.
  • Double Points: Machina Astralis, Upstairs, Mystery Box Side. Go Up The Stairs Towards The Mystery Box, Then Up The Next Flight Of Stairs Again. Right Away, On The Bottom Of The Cabinet Under The Shelf, You Should See It.
  • Nuke: Go To The Veilwalk, Which Runs From The Observatory Dome Toward The Archive Of Orbis. It's On A Ledge Before You Enter The Archive Of Orbis. Look Up And To The Right.
  • Bonus Points: In Spawn At The Crash Site, On The Rock Next To The Lamp Post. Stand On The Rock Shown In The Video To More Easily See It.
  • Insta-Kill: Go To The Scholar's Way Pathway Heading Out Of Museum Infinitum Towards Machina Astralis, But Don't Enter It. Move To The Right And Look Up To The Top Right Of The Building. It'll Be Sitting On A Pole.
  • Max Armor: Stargazer's Courtyard, After Turning On Power And Pack-A-Punch. Behind The Second Rock When Looking At The Wall Buy.

Hidden Rewards: Fire Sale And Random Perk

To Get Both The Free Fire Sale And Random Perk, You Need To Shoot All 7 Standard Drops First. Then These Two Drops Are Awarded To You At Their Locations At The Same Time.

  • Fire Sale: Museum Infinitum, On The Bookshelf Through The Barrier Shown In The Video.
  • Random Perk: Abyssal Rim, Next To The Steps, On The Pillar, Under The Ball Finial.
