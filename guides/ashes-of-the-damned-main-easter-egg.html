Ashes Of The Damned Main Easter Egg Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-10-08

Every Step Of The Ashes Of The Damned Main Quest, In Order: Tessie's Four Upgrade Parts, The Purple Clock Trials And The Boss Fight. Pure Steps, All In Under 10 Minutes In The Video Above.

The Tools For This Map Are On The Ashes Of The Damned Tools Page: The Full Map Cheat Sheet, The Blood Ritual Chalkboard Code, The Rocket Launch Code, The Projector Reels And The Necrofluid Gauntlet Solver.

Ol' Tessie: The 4 Upgrade Parts

Part 1: T.E.D.D.'S Head

  1. Melee This Panel In The Server Room At Spawn To Pick Up T.E.D.D.
  2. Build Him Directly On Tessie For Initial Access To Her.

Part 2: Pack-A-Punch

  1. Progress To Power Either The Farm Or The Blackwater Generator To Open Ashwood.
  2. Turn On Ashwood's Generator To Open The Garage Below.
  3. Drive Tessie Inside And Build Pack-A-Punch At The Crafting Station.

Part 3: Abomination Carcass

  1. Flip To A New Round Inside The Exit 115 Diner And Stand Somewhere Close To This Barrier. A Zombie Labeled "Cook" Spawns From It, Even On Ravager Rounds.
  2. The Cook Gives You A Key To Open This Freezer And Grab Your Abomination Carcass.
  3. Build It Onto Tessie At The Crafting Station In The Ashwood Garage.

Part 4: Wonderwaffe Turret

The Final Of Tessie's Four Parts. You Need The Necrofluid Gauntlet, The Serum Blood Ritual And The Three Keys.

The Necrofluid Gauntlet

Use The Necrofluid Gauntlet Solver For The Symbols Step.

  1. As Long As You're Above Round 8, Go Near This Storage Container At Spawn To Get And Defeat Uber Klaus, Plus His Inside Core, For A Stabilizer Chip.
  2. In Ashwood's Sheriff's Office, Insert It Into Klaus's Cell, Then Wait For The "Update Required" Restart Message To Show. Throw 2 Stun Grenades In Rapid Succession Into The Cell To Stun And Free Him.
  3. At Zarya Cosmodrome's Underground Support Systems, Hold Down Your Tactical Button To Place Klaus's Circle In Front Of This Control Panel And Summon Him To Activate It.
  4. Interact With The Panel To Activate A Scanning Circle. You Must Stand In It For A Total Of 30 Seconds. The Window In Front Of You Slides Down.
  5. Pick Up The Aether Barrel. Tessie Is Required For Transportation With This Equipped. If Lost, It Returns To Zarya.
  6. Make Sure All 3 Power Generators Are On, Then Insert The Barrel Into All Of Them, One At A Time, Where It Charges. With Each Completion You're On A Timer To Pick Up The Barrel And Deliver It To The Next Generator. After The Final Generator, Deliver The Barrel Into The Cube Under The Farm To Activate It.
  7. Use The Jump Pads. When In The Air, Look Back Down Toward The Farm To Get 3 Red Symbols On The Roof. Remember Them And Insert Them Into The Cube, Confirming Them By Interacting With The Eye.
  8. Grab The Gauntlet. To Keep It, Clear The Room Of All Green Spores By Shooting Them And Then Reloading To Suck Them In.

Serum Blood Ritual

Use The Blood Ritual Chalkboard Code For The Symbols Step.

Collect 6 Parts

  • Human Bones: Throw A Combat Axe At This Hanging Zombie's Ankle At Vandorn Farm, Then A Molotov At The Cut-Off Foot On The Ground. You Can Now Pick Up The Bones.
  • Hoard Hunk Chucks: While Driving Tessie In The Fog, Pan Around To Search For One Of The Randomly Spawning Purple Pods. Destroy It With The Abomination Beam For The Part.
  • Widow's Lantern: Pick Up The Jar Of Spores From Blackwater Cabin's Kitchen. Place It On This Horse At The Farm. After 3 Rounds The Spores Grow Enough For You To Pick Them Up.
  • Ravager Eyes: Defeat A Ravager With Any Trap On Any Round.
  • Mysterious Limb: Deal The Finishing Blow To A Bear With The Abomination Beam. Tip: To Spawn One In Before Round 16, Just Shoot The 3 Skulls Shown In The Video At Zarya With The Gauntlet. The Eyes Should Glow Green On Each And Spawn The Bear. Beam It To Death To Get The Part.
  • Powder Of The Forgotten: Buy Wisp Tea And Kill Until The Ghost Is Active. Stand Next To This Mirror In The Tailor Shop At Ashwood And The Ghost Enters The Mirror To Reveal The Seal Of Sorrow. Shoot The Mirror With The Wonder Weapon To Have It Spit Out The Powder.

Start The Serum

  1. Head To Yuri's Lab At Zarya Cosmodrome And Interact With The Powder To Start Your Serum.
  2. Symbols Spawn On The Board Above. Use The Top Part Of The Cheat Sheet To Match The First Symbol On Each Of The Board's 3 Lines, From Top To Bottom. Note The Item That Goes With That Symbol On The Sheet.
  3. Interact With Those Items In That Order In Your Game To Submit The Code. If Done Right, Interact With The Test Tube To Add Blood And Begin The Ritual.
  4. Flip A Round If You Do Anything Wrong.
  5. Tip: Stand In This Corner, Pop Toxic Growth And Use Your Wonder Weapon's Explode Ability. You'll Clear The Room With Ease.
  6. Finishing The Ritual Spawns In The Keys For The Next Step.

The 3 Keys

All Three Are In The Foggy Areas, Require Tessie For Transportation And Can Only Be Obtained By Shooting And Retracting A Gauntlet Shot Directly Next To Them. Each Must Be Delivered To The Seal At The Back Of Ashwood's Jug Side Before You Die.

Yellow Bruin Key: At The Crashed Rocket Going From Ashwood To Zarya.

  • Up On This Cliff
  • On This Crashed Pole
  • On This Building's Ledge To The Right, Before Entering Zarya

Until Delivered, This Key Takes Away Your Weapons, Leaving You Melee Only.

Red Nightbird Key: At The Orda Graveyard Going From Ashwood To Exit 115.

  • In This Dinosaur's Neck Bones, At The Spot Shown In The Video
  • In This Dinosaur's Mouth Between His Teeth
  • Behind This Tank

Until Delivered, This Key Knocks You Down To 50 HP And Standard Health Regeneration Is Disabled.

Green Terrapin Key: At The Grounded Ship Going From Ashwood To The Farm.

  • On The Ship, Through This Barrier
  • In The Corner Of This Crate
  • Under The Ship, Between These Two Poles

Until Delivered, This Key Slows Your Movement Speed To A Walk.

Once All Three Keys Are In The Seal, A Cutscene Plays. After That, Grab The Wonderwaffe Barrel And Build It Onto Tessie Inside The Garage To Fully Upgrade Her.

Purple Clock Trial Challenges

A Purple Clock Has Now Spawned Above The Garage. The First Three Challenges Can Be Done In Any Order, But Blackwater Lake Should Be Done Last.

Purple Charged Shots. Whenever A Step Says To Grab A Purple Or Charged Shot, This Is What's Meant:

  • Every Round, The Purple Clock Above Ashwood's Garage Can Be Activated By Shooting Tessie's Wonderwaffe At It.
  • That Lets You Gain A Purple Energy Charge Three Separate Times Per Round, Once Per Side Of The Clock, Except The Side Angled Down Toward The Garage. Shoot The Gauntlet At A Side Of The Clock Not Yet Used, Then Suck The Bullet Back In To Grab Its Charge.
  • You Can Switch Weapons And Shoot Those, But Shooting The Gauntlet Dissipates Its Charge.

Exit 115 Clock And Electricity

There Are 4 Parts To This Step. Pay Close Attention, Because The Order Matters.

  1. Wait For The Lightning To Be Actively Striking Exit 115. If There's No Active Lightning, You Can Cause It Yourself Once Every Round. There Are 3 Turned-Off Streetlights In The Area, And One Has A Small Electrical Spark. Shoot It With Tessie's Wonderwaffe And It'll Overload. Do This For All Three Turned-Off Streetlights And You'll Hear Lightning Strike Down.
  2. Shoot A Purple Charged Shot At The Clock On The Mechanic's Roof, Again Only After The Lightning Begins Striking. You Now Have 60 Seconds To Do As Much As Possible Of The Next Two Steps.
  3. Awaken Your Special Color-Coded Zombies. You Can, And Must, Repeat Steps 1 And 2 Every Round Until You Finish This Area's Challenges, But You Only Need To Activate These Zombies Once. After That, On The Next Round, You Can Walk Up To Them To Reawaken Them.
  4. Guide Each Zombie To Its Proper Color-Coded Light On Each Roof.

How To Awaken Each Zombie:

  • Mechanic: Activate Klaus Inside The Service Station, Then Summon Him Next To The Mechanic Lying On The Floor.
  • Trucker: Smash Tessie Into This Crate To Open It.
  • Waitress: Go Into The Diner And Melee This Cash Register To Open It, Not With The Gauntlet.

Where Each Zombie Goes:

  • Mechanic Goes Into The Yellow
  • Trucker Goes Into The Red
  • Waitress Goes Into The Blue

After All Three, The Lightning Stops, You Get A Quote And You See Red Wisps.

Zarya Rocket Launch

Use The Rocket Launch Code Sheet To Easily Decipher Your Launch Code.

  1. Get A Purple Charge And Blast Away To Zarya, Where You'll Notice Two Spinning Radar Dishes.
  2. Shoot The Charge At One Of Them To Slow It. Go Back To Ashwood, Grab A Second Charge, Then Slow And Shoot The Other Dish. This Activates The Computers In The Control Room.
  3. Look At These Two Monitors And Match Any Set Of The Two Flashed Symbols To The Sheet To Get Your Word. That Word And Its Symbols Go With A Launch Code, Seen On The Right Side Of Them On The Sheet.
  4. Insert The Launch Code One Number At A Time From Left To Right By Shooting The Red Button On Each Monitor. If You Mess Up, Shoot The Rest Of The Monitors To Reset It For The Next Round.
  5. A Correct Code Launches The Rocket After A Screen Flash.

Vandorn Farm Ritual Lockdown

  1. Get A Purple Charge And Hop Away To Vandorn Farm.
  2. Open The Farmhouse And Look At This Clock, Reading Either 9, 12 Or 3.
  3. Match The Clock To A Roman Numeral On The Floor Around The Table. Stand On It And Put The Gauntlet's Crosshair On The Farmer's Head. He Slowly Turns Toward You Until His Eyes Glow Red.
  4. Now Shoot His Head To Spawn In Some Artifacts: The Bear Is Upstairs On The Bed, The Mask Is In The Barn On The Zombie, The Chalice Is Sitting On This Ledge Next To The Tub In The Cellar And The Pigsticker Is Sitting Under The Workbench In The Shed.
  5. Place Each Item Down In The Red Circles Outside, Then Interact With The Final Item A Second Time To Begin A Lockdown. You Need To Protect All 4 Items From Attacking Zombies.
  6. When Done, Each Item Disappears Into Red Mist.
  7. If You Fail, Interact With The Red Circle, Then Collect Enough Souls To Retry Next Round.

Blackwater Lake Projector Reels

Use The Projector Reels Reference For This Step.

  1. Get A Charged Shot, Then Drive Tessie To Blackwater.
  2. Interact With The Smoke By The Mystery Box In The Back.
  3. In The Cabin, Shoot The Purple Shot At The Projector To Have A Sequence Play. It Shows The Order In Which You Must Shoot The Reels.
  4. Each Successful Reel Glows Purple When Shot, But The Wrong Reel Jump-Scares You And Forces A Round Flip.
  5. A Cheat Sheet To Make This Step Easier: Front Of The Cabin Is Upstairs, Back Of The Cabin Is Outside By The Ammo Box, Boat House Is The Pointy Roof And Tool Shed Is The Flat Roof.
  6. Once You've Done All The Reels, Call In Klaus In The Back, Then Summon Him Upstairs To This Box To Pick Up The Deathwhistle.

Veytharion Boss Fight

The Driving Sequence

  1. To Begin The Boss Fight, First Interact With Klaus, Then Give Him About 20 Seconds To Blow His Whistle And Summon All The Pyramid Souls Onto The Map. If You Don't Wait, You'll Fail The Step Due To Missing Souls.
  2. Hop Into Tessie To Start The Boss Fight. Drive The Exact, Never-Changing Path Shown In The Video To Pick Up All 12 Red Souls, Going Slow So You Don't Miss Anything.

Phase 1

Your Primary Goal At All Times Is To Keep Driving Tessie In Circles, Grabbing The Yellow Barnacles To Repair Her And Reduce The Amount Of Time She Breaks. Ram Tessie Into The Boss A Few Times To Reveal A Red Weak Spot. Keep Doing This, Aiming For The Red Spot Whenever It's Available For Increased Damage.

Phase 2

Phase 2 Is Very Similar. Do The Same As Before, But:

  • If At Any Point The Boss Is Doing A Bomb Attack, Don't Ram It Right Away. It'll Bomb Your Head As It Moves As Part Of The Attack.
  • Mostly Save Your Boost For Getting Out Of Bad Situations Like The Red Bomb Attack. For That One You Can Also Stay In Between The Marked Red Circles For Reduced Damage.

Just Take Your Time And Ram Tessie Into The Boss Whenever It's Somewhat Safe.

Phase 3

Phase 3 Adds Purple Mouths And A Laser Attack.

  • You Have About 10 Seconds From The Point A Mouth Starts A Purple Charge-Up To The Point It Aggressively Lasers You And Tessie.
  • Never Leave Tessie Unless Forced Out. If You Are, Use Her As A Shield.
  • The Boss May Pick Up And Throw Tessie At You Or To The Other Side Of The Arena, So Be Ready.
  • All That Has To Be Done Is Continually Smash Tessie Into The Boss's Purple Mouths To Do Damage And Negate The Laser.
  • Sometimes There Are Two Lit Mouths. Hit One, Then Make A Quick U-Turn And Smash Into The Other.
  • If You Know You're Not Going To Hit A Mouth, Try To Boost To The Other Side Of It. The Laser Still Targets You But May Take A Bit Longer To Reach You And Possibly Not Do As Much Damage.

If Tessie Breaks

  • Repair Her By Holding Interact For A Few Seconds, Using Your Best Tools Like Monkeys And Your Field Upgrade To Best Cloak You.
  • Reminder: There's A Free Max Ammo And Armor Plastered To The Wall That Can Be Sucked In With The Gauntlet.

Finishing Each Phase

Basically, Keep As Much Pressure On Veytharion As Possible By Doing Everything Above Throughout Each Stage. After Ramming Enough In Each Of The Three Stages:

  1. The Boss Glows Purple And Starts Spinning.
  2. Either Ram It Or Use The Wonderwaffe. This Spawns A Purple Light Somewhere In The Arena. The Light Despawns Very Quickly, So Boost To It.
  3. Driving Into The Light Charges Tessie.
  4. Ram The Charged Tessie Into The Boss. It Folds Up And Reveals A Yellow Weak Spot In The Middle Of The Arena.
  5. When This Happens, Use The Wonderwaffe To Spray That Spot.

Charging Tessie And Then Hitting The Boss With The Wonderwaffe Has To Be Done To Complete Each Of The 3 Boss Phases, 2 Times Per Phase. The Second Wave Usually Needs An Additional 1 To 3 Bullets, Max, From The Wonderwaffe. You'll Know A Phase Is Done When A Max Ammo And Full Armor Spawn On The Outskirts, Except At The End Of Phase 3, When You Get A Cutscene Instead.

After The Easter Egg

After Finishing The Easter Egg, Keep Playing And Interact With This Tablet At Blackwater. It Unlocks Cursed Mode Relic Research.
