Totenreich Main Easter Egg Guide, All Steps

By Joemet123 · 2026-10-08

All Steps, In Order: The Jotunn Star, Three Uranium, The Wunderbarrage, The Claw Machine, The Atomkraft Core, The Blodheim Hall Lockdown, The Compass Symbols And The Dravakar Boss Fight. The Video Above Covers It All In Under 8 Minutes.

The Tools For This Map Are On The Totenreich Tools Page: The Full Map Cheat Sheet, The Crate Paper Sheet And The Fishing And Pole Locations.

Power And Pack-A-Punch

As Always, The Guided Power And Pack-A-Punch Process Must Be Done. Here's A Quick Recap:

  1. Flip The Power Switch In Both Core Foundry And Skallen Market, Then Interact With The Center Console In Tyr's Head To Initiate Power.
  2. This Pops A Zombie Out Of One Of The Tubes. Go Through The Same One To Grab Its Dropped Keycard.
  3. In War Factory, Interact With The Console To Scan Your Card And Open The Door.
  4. Take Out The Zombies And Grab Your Free Glocke Drop.
  5. At The Center Of Fishery Island, Interact With The Pulsating Ground To Initiate A Link. Pack-A-Punch Drops After 45 Seconds.

Jotunn Star Wonder Weapon

  1. In Dry Dock, Interact With The Button At The Titan Crane Room To Lower A Board.
  2. Sprint Toward The Board, Jump On It Then Off It, And Quickly Turn Around To Pick Up The Chains Mid-Air Off The Ship. I Find Doing This From The Opposite Side Is Easiest.
  3. On Storm Bridge, Behind The Truck, Grab A Can Of Chili Chunks.
  4. Place It On The Table At Skallen Market, Right Across From Mule Kick.
  5. On The Next Max Ammo Round, After Killing A Few Frozen Zombies, A Very Beefy Zursa Bear Spawns. Killing It Gets You A Lantern.
  6. In Burial Grounds, Hold Interact On The Hole In The Building Next To The Church To Place Down Your Chains And Open The Door.
  7. Down The Stairs, Place The Lantern On The Table To Start The Constellation Step. Symbols Spawn On Each Side Of The Table And On The Walls. Look At The Table From The Stairs Toward The Back Wall.
  8. Interact With The Wall Symbols That Match The Table Symbols, In The Order Seen On The Table: Left Symbol First, Then Right, Then Back, Then Front. Interacting With The Symbols On The Table Resets All Progress.
  9. Once Done, A Ghost Appears. Follow Her Until She Stops And Asks For Souls. Get Those Kills, Then Follow Her Again: First By The Stairs, Then By The Water.
  10. Eventually She Spawns A Rocky Pathway Toward The Lighthouse. Follow The Path Laid Out For You, Jumping Rock To Rock, Through And Up The Lighthouse.
  11. At The Top, Interact With The Ladder To Finish The Process And Claim Your Dedicated Melee, The Jotunn Star.

Hold Melee To Pull It Out. Killing 3 To 5 Zombies With It Gives You A Charge. Up To 2 Charges Can Be Held At A Time, And You Use Them By Shooting (Right Trigger).

Collect 3 Uranium

You'll Now Have To Collect Three Uranium, In Any Order.

Uranium 1: ARC-XD Lock Picking

  1. Get An ARC-XD. There's A Free One On The Crate At Eidskallen Square.
  2. Destroy The Vent In Core Foundry.
  3. Drive The ARC-XD Through It And Boost Off The Ramp Onto The Boat And Into The Room.
  4. Detonate It At The Back To Blast Open A Door Next To Pack-A-Punch.
  5. At The Barrier, Take Note Of Which Of The Five Numbers Have The Nuke Symbol. Each Number Matches A Letter, Also Seen On The Head Jars Around The Room. Example: My Numbers Were 1 And 3, Which Means My Jars Were A And C.
  6. Place The Correct Jars On The Device To Get A Purple Water Jar. Place It On The Carcass To Get A Necrospike. If You Mix The Wrong Jars, Also Dispose Of The Non-Purple Mixture At The Carcass.
  7. Interact With The Cell To Begin A Lock-Picking Mini Game. Twist Around Until The Spike Lights Up White And Hold That Position To Open The Latch. Do This 3 Times To Get Into The Cell And Grab Your Uranium.

Uranium 2: Green Fish Ravager

  1. Use The Fishing And Pole Locations Sheet To Find A Fishing Pole First.
  2. Identify All 11 Fishing Locations. One Will Have A Green Fish Jumping Around In The Water.
  3. Place Your Rod At That Spot, Then Interact When It Turns Blue To Pull A Special Ravager Into The Map.
  4. Next Round, Locate The Ravager. He Has Buddies Around Him, But Killing The Special One Drops Your Uranium.

Uranium 3: Glocke Drop

  1. Get A Glocke Drop By Turning On Power, By Fishing Or From The Crafting Station.
  2. Gather Enough Zombies. In An Open Area, First Activate It Just Like Any Scorestreak, Then Call It Down With Your Right Trigger.
  3. It Pulses About Every 10 Seconds, Shooting Zombies Up Into The Air. Quickly Kill All The Orange Air Zombies, But Also Reserve Some Ground Zombies To Get Swooped Up Into The Next Pulse.
  4. Be Fast Enough To Get All 3 Pulse Rewards, And The Uranium.

Wunderbarrage And WW Signal

  1. Grab The Crowbar From The Shelf On The Lighthouse Second Floor.
  2. At Either Tyr's Foot Or The Titan Crane Room, Interact With The Paper On The Floor. It Indirectly Tells You Which One Of The Map's 4 Crates You Must Open. Match Your In-Game Paper's First Few Words To The Crate Paper Sheet To Find Its Crate. The Locations Stay The Same Every Game, But The Identifiers On The Bottom Right Do Change.
  3. The Four Crates: Upstairs In War Factory, In Core Foundry On The Bottom Floor Of The Building, In The Back Of Dry Dock Next To The Ship, Or On Fjord Road Behind The Truck.
  4. The Wrong Crate Attacks You. The Correct Crate Gives You A Bullet That Goes Into The Flak Cannon On The Right Side Of The Lighthouse. Melee It With The Jotunn Star To Shoot.
  5. Search The Robot In Spawn To Get A Transmitter. Build It Up The Ladder Inside Tyr's Head, Then Drop Down And Count How Many Times The Left And Right Lights Flash In Their 2 Sequences.
  6. Example: My Sequence 1 Was 1 And 4, Because Left And Right Both Blink Once But Right Blinks 3 More Times. My Sequence 2 Was 3 And 6, Because Left Blinks 3 Times And Right 6 Times In Total.
  7. At Antenna Tower, Melee The Rope With A Charged Jotunn Star To Ascend, Then Interact With The Device.
  8. Insert Your Sequence 1 Left And Right Flashes On Each Side. After A Strike To The Map And Killing Some Zombies, Do The Same With Your Sequence 2 Numbers. Another Strike Happens.
  9. Pick Up The Wunderbarrage From The Room Next To You.

Activate It Just Like The Glocke Drop, Calling It In At The Staircase In Dry Dock To Get Into The Machine Workshop.

Claw Machine

  1. In The Machine Workshop, Place Your 3 Uranium Into The Cylinder At The Front Of The Claw Machine To Start The Claw Puzzle Mini Game.
  2. Your Job Is To Move Uranium Tubes From The Left And Drop Them On The Right To Connect The Pre-Placed Tubes Horizontally And Vertically. This Completes Powered Circuits And Moves The Needle To The Green.
  3. If You Fail, Try Again Next Round.
  4. Make This Easy By Making Bunches Of 6 Tubes And 3 Tubes, Or 7 And 2.
  5. Example: I Made 6 And 3 By Placing Uranium In Slots A2, B1 And D2. I Made 7 And 2 By Placing Uranium In A2, A3 And D4, And It Worked.

Charge And Deliver The Canister

  1. A Successful Claw Machine Grants The Atomkraft Core. It Reduces Your Movement Speed But Can Be Dropped If Needed. It Must Be Delivered To The Pallets Next To Quick Revive.
  2. The Connected Generator In Lief Boat Repair Can Now Be Powered On.
  3. Keep Killing The Zombies Attacking The Core Until The Charging Gets Interrupted.
  4. Repower The Generator And Repeat The Process Until It's Fully Charged.
  5. Pick It Up And Bring It To Storm Bridge, Avoiding The Uplifting Glaciers On The Path. They Break Easily With The Wonder Weapon And Are Preceded By A Frost Effect On The Ground.
  6. Place The Core On The Barrel. After A Little Event Between The Robot And The Ice Monster, Pick Up Dravakar's Shard Next To The Robot Entrance Into Tyr's Foot.

Blodheim Hall Disciple Lockdown

  1. Place Dravakar's Shard In The Pit At Blodheim Hall. It Needs To Be Ignited With A Charged Wonder Weapon Or Blast.
  2. Grab A Disciple Injection. One Can Be Found For Free On The Ground Inside The Church.
  3. Activate The Disciple, Then Throw 5 Zombies At The Pit Until It Kicks You Out.
  4. This Starts A Mini Lockdown That Ends After You Kill An Extra Beefy Boss.
  5. A Sunstone Is Left On The Ground. Pick It Up And Place It On The Pedestal In The Church.

Compass Symbols

  1. Melee Or Blast The Sunstone With A Charged Jotunn Star. This Spawns A Symbol Wheel Above The Church, And Pylons Around The Map With Some Of Those Symbols.
  2. You'll Need To Find Your Three Symbols In The Correct Order. Your First Symbol Always Has The Arrow With A Single Line Pointing Toward It. Your Second Symbol Is Always Pointed Toward By The Double Arrow With Double Lines. Your Third Symbol Is Pointed At By The Arrow With The Triple Lines.
  3. Go Around The Entire Map, Finding And Shooting A Jotunn Star Blast At The Pylons Of Your Symbols, In The Correct Order.
  4. Success Gets Rid Of All The Pylons And Lets You Enter The Boss Fight By Interacting With The Center Console In Tyr's Head.

Boss Fight

Arena And Attacks

  • The Boss Arena Has Heat Traps On The Right, Middle And Left Sides. Activating One Negates Any Player Freezing.
  • A Charged Wonder Weapon Also Does This, Plus It Increases Your Movement Speed And Helps You Clear Zombies More Easily To Regain Armor.
  • If You Ever See A Red Circle On The Ground Or The Boss Conjuring Ice, Avoid The Outlined Area Or Duck For Cover Behind Rocks. An Ice Ball Is Inbound That Freezes Over The Area And Insta Downs You With A Direct Hit. Be Cautious Of The Ice Zombie Waves From Here, As They're Violent.
  • Throughout This Fight, Dravakar Unleashes Rune Rock Attacks On You, And Hiding Behind Rocks Does Not Always Prevent Them.

The Rune Attacks

  • H: Randomly Drops Rocks Over Your Surrounding Area. Focus On The Sky, Dodging And Weaving What's Coming Directly At You.
  • V: Connected V Waves Of Rocks. Move Left And Right Between Each V.
  • S: An S-Shaped Formation Aimed At Your Current Position. Move Left Or Right Of The S.
  • Line: A Horizontal Wave Of Rocks. Go Prone.
  • Arrow: The Rocks Directly Ambush You. Wait For The Rocks To Bunch Together Before Dodging.

Your Objective

Intermittently Shoot The Orange Weak Spots On Dravakar. Whenever He's Damaged Enough During A Phase, The Robot Calls In A Non-Harmful Strike To The Map, Killing All Zombies.

Ice Skulls (A Mini Phase In Phase 2). These Are Separate From Dravakar. Keep Your Distance, As They're Spinning And Immune, And Getting Too Close Wakes Them Up, Causing A Damaging Glacier Attack To Come Up From Under You If They're Not Knocked Fast Enough. To Knock One And Make It Vulnerable, Use A War Machine, Semtex, Frags Or Even A Charge Of The Jotunn Star To Get A Blast Into Its Mouth. Then Shoot It. If You Don't Finish It Off, You're Glacier Attacked Right Away. Just Repeat Until It's Dead, For Free Ammo And Armor.

The Phases

  1. Phase 1: You Get Super Sprinters And Just Need To Target The Weak Spot.
  2. Phase 2: Starts With, And Includes Throughout, A Few Rounds Of Ice Skulls. Taking Them Out Brings Dravakar Back To The Fight. He Has Moving Weak Spots And Does Rune Symbol Attacks.
  3. Phase 3: Confines You Tightly. Avoid The Rapid-Fire Rune Attacks While Spraying The Weak Spot In His Face. Lay Into Him Enough And The Easter Egg Is Complete.
