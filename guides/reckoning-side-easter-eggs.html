The Reckoning Side Easter Eggs Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-09

Side Easter Eggs On The Reckoning. Best Rewards First, Music Last.

Covered In Their Own Guides

  • Aether Blade: Reckoning Aether Blade Easter Egg Guide
  • Free Gold Tool And Pack-A-Punch Crystal: Reckoning Target Easter Egg Guide
  • Gorgofex: Reckoning Gorgofex Guide
  • Free Power-Ups: Reckoning Free Power-Ups Guide

Free Self-Revive

  • Reward: A Free Self-Revive.
  1. Make Your Way To The T1 Directors Office. It's The Room You're Required To Open To Activate The Teleporter To Pack-A-Punch.
  2. In Here, Turn Around. There's A Pool Table, And Behind That, There's A Wall Of Pool Balls.
  3. Shoot The Balls In The Order Shown In The Video: 1, 1, And Then The 5.
  4. The Red 5 Ball Is On The Very Bottom Rack. As Soon As You Shoot It, The Self-Revive Is On The Table.

Free Random Perk And 1,000 Essence

  • Reward: A Free Random Perk And 1,000 Essence.
  1. Open Up The Entire Map And Pack-A-Punch First. It Makes Stuff Just A Lot Easier On Yourself.
  2. Head To The T1 Executive Suites, Which Is Where Juggernog Is.
  3. Stand On The Ledge Shown In The Video And Look Down. You'll See Mister Peeks Sitting There.
  4. Shoot Mister Peeks. He Ascends Upward And Sits On The Railing.
  5. Interact With Him. Give It A Few Seconds.
  6. Jump Off The Railing Towards Him And Parachute Through All Of The Hoops Mister Peeks Leaves Behind.
  7. Follow His Path, As Seen In The Video, Through Every Single Hoop. At The Very End, You're Rewarded With A Free Random Perk.
  • Each Hoop Is A Free 100 Essence As Well.
  • Fail: In Solo, I Failed This Four Different Times, But I Was Still Able To Go Back To The Top And Try To Complete It Again On The Same Round.

Free Deadshot Daiquiri

  • Reward: A Free Deadshot Daiquiri, As Early As Round 1.
  1. Make Your Way To The T1 Mutant Research Lab And Run Through The Door Towards The Mystery Box Location (Shown In The Video).
  2. Make Sure You're Behind The Entrance Barrier. Shoot The Helmets On Both Dummies In The Distance.
  3. You'll Hear A Noise. That Means The Easter Egg Has Started.
  4. Behind You Are Some Deadshot Perks On A Pole. Shoot Them Without Missing And In Time.
  5. Done Correctly, You Drink A Free Deadshot Daiquiri.
  • You Don't Need Power Or Pack-A-Punch Open, Which Is Why It's Technically Possible On Round 1.
  • Use Any Weapon You Want. Shoot One Shot At A Time And Hit Every Shot, Or You Fail.
  • Fail: Going To The Next Round Is Required For The Chance To Try Again.
  • Everybody In Your Game Can Do It. Your Teammates Do The Same Thing You Did, Each One At A Time, On Every New Round.
  • Seemingly Only One Person Needs To Shoot The Helmets, At The Very Beginning, To Start It.

Unlimited Free Kazimirs

  • Reward: A Free Kazimir. Repeatable Once Per Round.
  1. Get A Kazimir, Either From Loot Or Through The Crafting Table.
  2. Make Your Way To The T1 Executive Suites. Stand Between The Two Benches Shown In The Video.
  3. Hold Down The Button You'd Use To Throw The Kazimir.
  4. Use Your Crosshair To Match The Same Angle And Position Shown In The Video.
  5. Throw It. The Goal Is To Land It On The Little Circle In The Middle Of The Logo.
  6. Hit The Circle And It Glows Purple.
  7. Get 10 Kills For Souls In Front Of The Circle. A Free Kazimir Drops.
  • Fall Short: The Kazimir Falls Out Of The Map. Sometimes It Gives You It Back.
  • Miss High: It May Fall In The Map. Then It's Used, And You Have To Buy Another.
  • Repeat: An Unlimited Number Of Times, Once Per Round. With Every Time You Do It, The Number Of Zombie Souls Needed Increases Substantially.

Gold Bins: Free Loot

  • Reward: Random Loot: Gold Tools, Pack-A-Punch Crystals, Essence And Literally Anything Else. The Final Gold Bin Guarantees A Scorestreak, Essence, Salvage And Equipment.
  1. Interact With All The Bins Listed Below. Four Of Them Give You Random But Potentially Amazing Rewards.
  2. Your Final Bin, At The Back Of Spawn, Is Glowing Gold. Interact With It For The Guaranteed Rewards.
  • Rewards Are Completely Random, So You Could Get Terrible Loot.
  • It Takes Less Than 90 Seconds Of In-Game Time. It's So Fast To Do, And The Best Rewards Are So Great, There's No Reason Not To Do It.

T1 Executive Suites: Six Bins. Looking At Juggernog From The Angle Shown In The Video:

  • One To Your Left, In The Corner.
  • One In The Doorway To The Right Of Juggernog.
  • One Off To The Left, In The Hallway.
  • One Next To The Ammo Crate, To The Right After Going Up The Stairs.
  • One On The Other Side Of The Top Area, Around The Bend.
  • One Through The Purchasable Door, In The Back Room Where The Portal And The Arsenal Machine Are.

T1 Project Janus Reception, In Spawn: Four Bins.

  • Next To The Zombie Barrier.
  • Next To The Plant Across From Quick Revive.
  • Next To The Plant Across From The Quantum Computing Aether Elevator.
  • Your Final Bin, At The Back Of Spawn, Glowing Gold.

Vending Machine: Free Loot

  • Reward: Random Loot Every Round: Wonder Weapons, Salvage, Essence, Pack-A-Punch Crystals And More.
  1. Go To The T2 Android Assembly. The Vending Machine Is Right Next To Where You Assemble The Friendly Klaus.
  2. Melee The Vending Machine At The Chute Where Stuff Would Usually Come Out. You Receive Random Loot.
  3. You Can Do It In Every Round That Passes By.
  • Melee Macchiato: Melee It With This Perk And It Breaks The Machine. It Gives You Quite A Bit Of Essence, And That's It For The Rest Of The Game.
  • Most Of The Loot You Get Is Insignificant, More Just Complimentary To Your Gameplay: A Single Piece Of Salvage, Maybe A Decoy. Occasionally You Get Something Great.
  • It's Free And Worth It To Take A Chance On Every Round.

Aetherella Laser Companion

  • Reward: A Friendly Aetherella With A Pretty Good Laser That Aims Exactly Where Your Weapon Crosshair Is Pointed. She Lasts Five Full Minutes.
  1. Make Your Way To The T1 Project Janus Reception, In Spawn. There Are Some Bathroom Stalls Through The Zombie Barrier (Shown In The Video).
  2. Pull Out Your Emote Wheel And Scroll Over To Sprays. Spray Anything You Want On The Stall Door To Reveal A Mini Statue On The Floor.
  3. Shoot A Charged Shot Of Any Gorgofex At The Statue To Make Aetherella Follow You.
  4. Go To The T2 Sublevel 10 Pack-A-Punch Room And Ride The Beam To Trap Her In It.
  5. Ride The Beam An Additional Time, But This Time Dodge Everything In Your Path So You Crash Into Aetherella.
  6. She Leaves The Beam In An Upgraded Form, With The Laser.
  • She Recharges, Ready To Be Crashed Into For Use Again, Every Three Rounds.
  • Need A Gorgofex First? See The Reckoning Gorgofex Guide.

Paintings: 1,500 Essence

  • Reward: 500 Essence For Each Painting Returned, 1,500 In Total.
  1. Open Pack-A-Punch.
  2. Deliver The Three Missing Paintings To The Wall In The T1 Directors Office. The Steps And The Three Pickup Spots Are In The Reckoning Aether Blade Easter Egg Guide, Under Return Three Paintings.
  • The T1 Directors Office Is The Next Room Right Over From Elemental Pop.
  • Each Painting Has Its Own Slot To Be Placed In.
  • You'll Sometimes Need To Go Prone To Pick Up A Painting, As The Hitboxes Are Really Bad.
  • The Red Portal In The Paintings Room Goes To The T1 Project Janus Reception, In Spawn, Where One Of The Paintings Is.

The "1 Million Points" Jumpscare

  • Reward: None. There Is No Free 1 Million Points. This Is The Jumpscare Easter Egg.
  1. Come To The T1 Executive Suites.
  2. You Don't Need A Really Zoomed In Scope, But An Attached Scope Of Some Sort Seems To Be Needed.
  3. Look Out Through The Barrier Right Next To The Jump Pad (Shown In The Video).
  4. Look Out Of The Map At The Table (Shown In The Video) For 5 Seconds.
  • Have Fun Trolling Your Friends.

Fried Chicken Bucket Helmet

  • Reward: A Chicken Bucket Hat. It Does Seem To Have No Use At All.
  1. In The T1 Executive Suites, Throw Some Kind Of Grenade At The Bucket On The Shelf Above The Bar. I Recommend A Semtex.
  2. Six Chicken Bones Spawn In, Scattered Around The Room. Pick Up All Six. These Locations Never Change.
  3. After You Get All Six Bones, The Bucket Hat Is Available For Pickup.
  • The Hat Is Visible To All Players In Game, Including Yourself In Third-Person Mode.

The Six Chicken Bones

  • One To The Left Of The Bucket, On The Same Counter.
  • One To The Right Of The Bucket, On The Same Counter, On The Dish.
  • On The Floor Near The Exit, At The Back Right Side Of The Bar.
  • Behind The Garbage Pail Next To The Sink, On The Floor Behind The Bar.
  • On The Chair Across From That Garbage Pail.
  • Under A Chair (Shown In The Video).

All Perk And Wunderfizz Locations

  • Quick Revive: T1 Project Janus Reception, Which Is Spawn.
  • Deadshot Daiquiri: T1 Mutant Research Lab. From Spawn, Go Up The Aether Elevator To The Right.
  • PhD Flopper: Around The Bend In The Same Area.
  • Melee Macchiato: Make Your Way Back Down The Elevator, And Instead Of Going To The Right, Go To The Left In The Spawn Area. It's Up That Elevator.
  • Speed Cola: Around The Bend From Melee Macchiato.
  • Double Tap: Moving Forward, Take The Jump Pad Over To The Next Area.
  • Wunderfizz: In The Same Area As Double Tap. It's Shown At The Very End Of The Video.
  • Stamin-Up: Take The Aether Elevator Up From There. It Leads You To Another Area Where Stamin-Up Is.
  • Juggernog: T1 Executive Suites. From The Stamin-Up Area, Take The Jump Pad Over To The Next Area.
  • Elemental Pop: Around The Bend From Juggernog.
  • Vulture Aid: The Final Perk Available On This Map. Its Location Is Shown In The Video.
  • Other Than The Wunderfizz, Death Perception Is The Only Perk That Does Not Appear To Be On This Map.
  • The Elevators And Jump Pads To Take Are Shown In The Video.

Pack-A-Punch In 30 Seconds

  1. Open Your Way To Richtofen's Office, The T1 Directors Office. It's Indicated By The Orange Markers On Your Screen And By Following The Purple Arrows Around The Map.
  2. Interact With The Device On The Corner Of The Desk. It Activates The Teleporter Behind It.
  3. Take The Teleporter.
  4. In This New Room, Restart The Particle Accelerator At The Point Indicated By The Orange Marker. Run Up To The Switch And Flip It.
  5. Around The Particle Accelerator Are Purple Aetherium Crystals That You Must Shoot To Break. You Need 12 Total.
  6. After Shooting All 12, Low Gravity Activates.

Music Easter Egg 1: Three Headphones

  • Reward: The Easter Egg Song, Remember Us, Featuring Elena Siegman.
  1. Have Pack-A-Punch Open.
  2. Interact With All Three Bunny Headphones.

Where The Headphones Are

  • T1 Directors Office: Starting At Spawn, Make Your Way Through The Red Teleporter. They're There Before You Go To The Next Red Teleporter, The One Into Pack-A-Punch (Shown In The Video).
  • T1 Mutant Research Lab: Make Your Way Around The Bend Towards Juggernog, But Jump Down The Railing And Parachute Your Way Down.
  • T2 Dark Entity Containment: From There, Go Around The Bend, Take The Launcher To The Next Area, Then Go Up The Aether Elevator. It Leads You To The Downstairs Area Of Where The Forsaken Is.

Music Easter Egg 2: Metal Detectors

  • Reward: The Song Samantha's Peace, Which Returns From Tag Der Toten In Black Ops 4.
  1. Go To Spawn And Stand In The Very Back Of It, So You See Quick Revive Through The Metal Detectors.
  2. Note The Number Of Each Metal Detector, Listed Below.
  3. Run Through Detector 1, Then Loop To 3, Then Through 2.
  4. Do It Four Complete Times In Succession, 1, 3, 2 Every Time, Without Breaking Anything Up.
  5. Your Screen Flashes When It's Done Correctly, And Samantha's Peace Begins.
  • As Easy As It Is, It Really Gets Confusing, Believe It Or Not. So Have Some Patience.

Detector Numbers

  • 1: On The Left, At Least From That View. It Will Always Be The One Closest To The Armor Wall Buy.
  • 2: Always The One In The Middle.
  • 3: On The Right. The One Not Attached To The Wall Of The Armor Wall Buy.
