BO6 Zombies Loadouts Explained

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-09

Everything Related To The Loadout Section In BO6 Zombies. Weapons Are Saved For Last Because There's A Ton Packed In There.

Open Your Loadout

  1. Go To Your Main Zombies Menu.
  2. Go To Weapons.
  3. Go To Loadouts.
  • You Can Select Your Weapon On The Top, Melee Weapon, Field Upgrade, Lethal, Tactical And GobbleGum Pack.

Lethal, Tactical, Field Upgrade

  • Lethal And Tactical Are Self-Explanatory: Select The Ones You Want.
  • If There's A Lock, You Must Get To The Appropriate Level. It Says The Level Under The Description.
  • Example: Combat Axe, Unlock At Level 53.
  • That Goes For Everything That Has A Lock Next To It. Just Get To The Level And It Unlocks For You To Use.
  • Field Upgrade: Just Pick Whatever One You Have Unlocked And Want To Use.

Melee

  • There's A Dedicated Melee Slot In This Game Now.
  • Just Pick What You Want.
  • You Can Pack-A-Punch Your Melee Weapon.
  • Hold Down Melee In Game And You'll Have It In Your Loadout.

Use A Variant

  1. Go Into The Melee Weapon (Shown In The Video).
  2. Select Knife.
  3. Select The Variant. Example: The Mind's Eye Variant From The Vault Edition.
  • Now You Have The Special Variant Applied As Your Dedicated Melee Weapon.

GobbleGum Pack

  • To Edit A Pack, Hover Over It And Hit Options. It Says It Right On The Bottom (The Triangle Button On PlayStation).
  • Delete: Permanently Deletes The GobbleGum Pack. You Have To Have One At Any Point In Time, So You Can Only Do It As Long As You Have More Than One.
  • Rename: You Can Name It Anything.

Create A Pack

  1. Scroll Down And Hit Create A New Pack.
  2. You Can Name It Right There.
  3. Select Each Slot, Through That Fifth Slot, And Select Whatever Gum You Want. Done, Your Pack Is Ready.

Equip A Pack

  1. Just Like You'd Select A Weapon, Click The GobbleGum Pack.
  2. Select Which Pack You Want.
  3. Now It's Equipped On The Bottom.

Starting Weapon

  1. Click On The Weapon Slot.
  2. Pick From Your Different Categories Of Weapons.
  3. Scroll Down To The Weapon And Select It. It's In Your Class At This Point.

Gunsmith

  • Right Under The Number Of Attachments, To The Right Of The Weapon, There's A Button That Tells You What To Press For The Gunsmith. Press That Button.
  • You Can Change The Attachments, So Long As You Have Them Unlocked.
  • Or Remove Them If You Just Want To Use The Base Weapon.
  • Camos: On The Top, Tab Over To The Customize Tab And Put On A Camo.

Weapon Builds

  • Select The Base Weapon And You Have A Standard Version Of It, No Problems.
  • Or Go In And Create A New Build Of The Weapon.
  1. Select The Weapon.
  2. Go In (Shown In The Video) And Pick Your Attachments. You Must Have Them Unlocked. Read Through What It Says To See How You Unlock Specific Attachments.
  3. Back Out. It's Created Another Build For That Weapon.
  • Do It Again And It Creates A Third One.
  • Your Blueprints All Show Up Here Too.
  • Editing A Blueprint Doesn't Create A Brand New Build. It Just Modifies That Build Because It's A Blueprint. In The Video, Removing One Attachment From A Vault Edition Blueprint Only Modified It.

Zombie Builds

  • A Zombie Build Is The Version Of A Weapon You Get When You Pick It Up In The Match.
  • Like Black Ops 3 Zombies, How You Had Weapon Kits. This Is Your Weapon Kit.
  1. Go Into The Weapon.
  2. On The Bottom Of Your Screen You See Set Zombie Build. Press That Button (The Left Trigger In The Video).
  3. That Red Icon Goes On The Build And It Says Zombie Build Set.
  • Buy The Weapon Off The Wall, Get It Out Of The Box Or Get It From Some Kind Of Challenge: It Will Always Be The Zombie Build You Have Set, With The Correct Attachments And Camos.
  • You Can Spawn In With Any Weapon You Want. When You Get That Weapon In The Match, It's Still Your Zombie Build.
  • You Can Do This For Every Single Weapon.
  • Creating A New Build Is Important Too, Because You Can Apply Custom Camos To The Weapon Even If It's A Vault Edition Weapon.
