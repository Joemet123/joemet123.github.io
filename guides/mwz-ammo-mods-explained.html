MW3 Zombies Ammo Mods Explained

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-11

Ammo Mods Are Just Simply Ammo Modifications That Let You Shoot Special Bullet Effects Every 20 To 30 Seconds.

Getting Ammo Mods

  • Unlike Previous Zombies Games, They're Not Available From The Pack-A-Punch Machine.
  • Complete Contracts.
  • Mini Objectives Like The Aether Nest. You Usually Get Them From There.
  • Simply Looting.
  • There Are Schematics For Each Ammo Mod. If You Have, Say, The Schematic For Brain Rot, Go Into Your Schematics Menu To Craft It And Bring It Into Your Game For Immediate Use. More In Acquisitions And Schematics.

Base Abilities

Each One Hits The Zombie You Shoot And It Activates On.

  • Cryo Freeze: Freezes And Slows The Zombie.
  • Shatter Blast: Creates A Mini Explosive Radius From The Zombie.
  • Napalm Burst: Burns The Zombie.
  • Dead Wire: Electrocutes The Zombie And Attempts To Create A Mini Wunderwaffe-Like Effect.
  • Brain Rot: Turns The Zombie, So It Then Kills Other Zombies.
  • Ammo Mods Are Actually Pretty Weak Against The Zombies.
  • But They Deal Additional Damage Against Bosses, So It's Very Important To Understand How They All Work.

Hidden Bonuses

These Are A Bit More Hidden, But Really Useful. You Get Them By Having The Ammo Mod Equipped On Your Weapon.

  • Brain Rot: Convert Zombies, But Also Manglers, Dogs, Disciples And Mimics, Into Friendlies That Kill Other Enemies For You. Additional Damage To Both Mimics And Apparently The Abomination Bosses.
  • Dead Wire: Simply More Damage To Disciples.
  • Napalm Burst: Simply More Damage To Manglers.
  • Cryo Freeze: Slows And Freezes Hellhounds Too, And Deals Additional Damage To Them. But They're Easy To Kill Anyway, So It Doesn't Even Matter.
  • Shatter Blast: No Obvious Benefits Off The Bat, None That We've Noticed. It Could Be Useful For Destroying Armor On Bosses Like The Mangler And Even Armored Zombies, If You Get It To Activate Within A Close Radius. Shoot An Armored Zombie And It Activates: It Helps Remove That Armor Faster. Shoot A Zombie Next To A Mangler And It Activates: It Helps Deal More Damage To That Mangler And Remove The Armor, So You Can Just Start Laying Bullets Into His Head.

Best To Worst

As For Which One Is The Best, I'd Say:

  1. Brain Rot: It's Guaranteed To Kill, And It Deals More Damage To The More Common Bosses You'll See Around The Map.
  2. Shatter Blast: At The Very Least It Knocks Down Zombies For You And Lets You Run Away From The Situation.
  3. Dead Wire: It Can Chain The Zombies Just A Small Bit. More Importantly, It Takes Out Disciples A Little Faster. They're The Most Terrorizing Boss: They Literally Deplete Your Health One HP At A Time And Make Your Screen Red.
  4. Napalm Burst: The Mangler Damage. He's A Common Boss, Just Not As Terrorizing As The Disciple.
  5. Cryo Freeze: Its Effect Is On Dogs, And They're Easy To Kill Anyway.
