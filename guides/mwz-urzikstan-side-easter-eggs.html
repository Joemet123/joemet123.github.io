MW3 Zombies Urzikstan Side Easter Eggs Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-11

Also On This Map: USB Red Worm Easter Egg, Free Perks. Map Spots: Urzikstan Cheat Sheet. Dark Aether Eggs: Bad Signal Keys And Secret Rooms, The 115 Song.

Friendly Dog

  • Reward: A Dog That Will Revive You When You're Down, Act As A Smaller-Scale Decoy Like A Monkey Bomb, And Even Kill Zombies For You.
  1. Find A Doghouse. Locations: Urzikstan Cheat Sheet.
  2. Kill Zombies To Get Meat.
  3. Interact With The Doghouse, Then Interact With The Meat Inside Your Rucksack To Place It Down. Same Mechanic As Placing Turret Cards Into Turrets Around The Map.
  4. Hand It Enough Meat For The Tier You Want And The Dog Spawns In.

Tiers (Minimum Meat)

  • Tier 1: 1 Meat, Gray Zone Doghouse.
  • Tier 2: 2 Meat, Orange Zone Doghouse.
  • Tier 3: 3 Meat, Red Zone Doghouse. Not Four, It's Three.
  • Dark Aether Dog: 3 Meat. A Kind Of Tier 3 Plus Dog, Only From The Dark Aether.

Getting Meat

  • Killing Zombies With Any Weapon Slowly Gets You Meat. The Shatter Blast Ammo Mod Should Speed This Up.
  • Explosive Weapons Like The Grenade Launcher Seem To Give A Higher Success Rate Of Zombies Dropping Meat.
  • Kazimir Grenades Seem To Be Some Kind Of Meat Generating Machine. One Costs 5,000 Essence At The Red Zone Buy Station.
  • Exfil Points: Call In The Chopper And Kill The Zombies That Spawn In As Quickly As Possible. You'll More Than Likely Get At Least Two Pieces Of Meat.
  • Don't Get On The Chopper. The Meat Doesn't Carry Over To The Next Game.

Tips

  • Each Doghouse Is A One-Time Use Per Location. There Are 24 Players In A Game, So If Somebody Uses It, It Disappears. If You Don't See It In Your Game, That's Why.
  • There's Only One Doghouse In The Red Zone.
  • Not Enough Meat May Just Break The Doghouse. If It Doesn't, Go Back And Hand It More.
  • I Do Think More Meat Equals More Health: A Tier 1 Dog Should Have More Health With Four Meat Than With One. I Can't Say It With 100% Certainty.
  • Each Tier Up Has More Health And Kills Zombies More Easily. Run Into The Red Zone With A Tier 1 Dog And He'll Be A Dead One Real Fast.
  • If He Already Took Some Hits And He Rests, He'll Regenerate Some Of His Health.
  • If A Teammate Goes Down, Even On The Opposite Side Of The Map, The Dog Comes To Them And Releases A Healing Aura That Immediately Revives Them. Not Once They're Completely Dead And Bled Out.

Dog Bone Schematic

  • Complete The Dark Aether Easter Egg, Then Do Elder Sigil Contracts. You Can't Get It From Tier 3 Red Zone Contracts. You Must Be In The Dark Aether.
  • Activate It In Game For A Tier 3 Dog In The Main Map, And A Dark Aether Tier Dog In The Dark Aether.

Rune Portals

  • Reward: Teleport Almost Anywhere From Anywhere On Urzikstan, For 1,000 Essence.
  • Over 41 Entrance And Exit Points Combined: 24-Plus Exits And 17-Plus Entrances. Every Exit Has Three Pink Symbols. Codes And Spots: Urzikstan Cheat Sheet.
  1. Come Within 4 Or 5 M Of A Portal Entrance. Wait For The Animation And The Noise, And It'll Pop Up.
  2. Shoot The Three Symbols Of The Code For The Exit You Want. Selected Symbols Turn Yellow.
  3. A Teleporter For That Code's Location Spawns In. Pay 1,000 Essence And You're At Your Destination.
  • The Codes Never Change. The Same Code Always Teleports You To The Same Spot, Every Game, From Every Entrance.
  • Your Teammates Don't Have To Pay If You Do. They Can Run In Right Behind You. The Portal Lasts 15-Plus Seconds, But I Wouldn't Push It. I'd Wait Until You're Together And Then Shoot It.
  • You Can Use The Same Entrances And Exits Multiple Times Per Game.
  • Wrong Symbol: With At Least Two Symbols Shot (Yellow), Melee A Third, Different Symbol With Just Your Fist. That Resets The Code.

Gray Zone Entrances

  • H4: Outside A Village, In The House Shown In The Video. Kind Of Hidden Off, But Right Through The Door.
  • C6, Ghalia Seaside: In The Last Room Of The Top Floor Of The Hotel Building.
  • C1, Kotovo Blocks: Inside The USB Worm Picture Room.
  • H2, Orlov Military Base: On The Second Floor Of The Broken Building.
  • H8, Raaha Island: On The Wall With The Mountain-Looking Painting.
  • C4, Seaport District: Inside The Train Car Shown In The Video.
  • H8, Shahin Manor: On The Outside Of The Containers Shown In The Video.
  • E1, Sorokin Industrial: Up The Stairs Of The Building Shown In The Video, Hiding Next To A Weapon Locker.
  • D1, Talanov Outpost / Kotovo Blocks: In The Underground Tunnel Area.
  • I2, Zlatyev Array: Inside The Building, Through One Of The Doorways Shown In The Video.
  • F8, Zaravan Suburbs: In A Room Next To The Boxing Ring.

Orange Zone Entrances

  • Low Town: Inside The Little Hut Shown In The Video.
  • F3, Popov Power: On The Wall Next To The Catwalk, In The Side Building Off The Main Ones You'll See On The Map.
  • C3, Rostova Shops: Up The Stairs, Inside The Conference Room.
  • F6, Shorok Opera House: Up The Stairs, On Some Doors.
  • D6, Zaravan City: On The Top Floor Office Room, In The Corner Next To The Whiteboard.

Red Zone Entrance

  • F5, Opal Palace: In The Underpass Area.

Dokkaebi Warlord Without A Keycard

  • Reward: A Reward Rift On The Roof With A Sniper Rifle Blueprint That Seems Pretty Solid, And Some Other Miscellaneous Rewards.
  1. Head To The Top Of Dokkaebi's Fortress, The Building In Zaravan City. The Elevators And Cranes Are Locked Off Or Functioning Weirdly: The Main Elevators Bring You Up To An Area, But The Rest Is Locked Off.
  2. Get Onto The Roof One Of The Three Ways Below.
  3. Kill Dokkaebi. She Flies Around On Drones And Occasionally Lands. You Can Still Shoot Her While She's Flying, But Once She Lands, Lay Bullets Into Her. She Should Die Fairly Quickly.
  4. If She Doesn't, Run Around And Use The Full Length Of The Roof. Don't Be Scared To Mantle To Different Places. Stay On Top Of The Roof And Take Out The Mercenaries And Equipment Coming At You.
  5. Once She's Dead, A Reward Rift Spawns On The Roof. It Seems To Be The Same Location Every Single Time (Shown In The Video).

Getting On The Roof

  • Rappel: On The Side Of The Building, Jump On The Flower Pot Shown In The Video And Mantle Onto The Boxes Leading To The Rappel. Once You're Up, Make A 180, Jump On Top Of The Rappel, Then Jump Over The Wall.
  • Building Across: Go To The Building Across From Dokkaebi's Fortress, Rappel To The Top From The Outside, Then Rappel Across To The Fortress Roof.
  • Scorcher: Take The Elevators Within The Main Fortress Building, Then Use The Scorcher To Fly Around The Roof To The Unlocked Areas. That's A Bit Easier.

Tips

  • Be Really Careful Up There.
  • Get A Dog, Through A Doghouse Or A Dog Bone.
  • The RGL Grenade Launcher Seems Fantastic At Taking Down The Enemies And Equipment Coming At You. I Two-Shotted Dokkaebi With It.
  • Maybe Have A Teammate. You May Die, Sure, But You Should Be All Right.

Secret Vault

  • Reward: Unlocks The Secret Vault At H7. The Video Does Not Say What Is Inside.

Take Out Four Different Bosses, In Any Order. After Each One, Interact With The Transmitter To Send A Signal To The Safe At H7.

  1. The Bishop: Make Your Way To The Tunnels At D1. You'll Get Smoked Out, And Here You Must Kill The Bishop. Then Interact With The Transmitter.
  2. Armor Truck: Check The Tac Map For A Single Armor Truck Circling Around The Perimeter Of The Orange Zone. Meet Up With The Truck And Kill The Knight Boss Once He Gets Out. Then Interact With The Transmitter.
  3. Hotel Room: Head To The Hotel At C5 And Up The Stairs To The Room Shown In The Video. Take Out The Rook Boss, Then Interact With The Transmitter.
  4. Hut: The Easiest One. Make Your Way To The Hut At I1 And Interact With The Transmitter On Top Of The TV.
  5. Head Back To The Safe At H7 And Interact With The Fire Alarm On The Wall To Unlock The Vault.
  • There Are Multiple Tunnels Around D1. If You Go To The Middle Of One And Don't Get Smoked Out, Somebody Has Either Killed The Bishop Or He Is In Another Tunnel.
