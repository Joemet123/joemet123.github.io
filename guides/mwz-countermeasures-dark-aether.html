MW3 Zombies Countermeasures Dark Aether Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-11

By Completing This Easter Egg You Access The Only Way Of Getting The Schematics For The Blood Burner Key, Mags Of Holding And V-R11. Map: Countermeasures Cheat Sheet.

Before You Start

  • Change Your Act Mission To Act 4 Mission 2, Countermeasures.
  • As Far As What To Run, Run What You Like. I've Been Rocking A Kastov-74u Variant That's Been More Than Solid. Decoys And Healing Aura Are Lifesavers.
  • In Game, Set Up With At Minimum A Purple Tier 3 Pack-A-Punch Weapon And Your Basic Good Perks: Quick Revive, Speed Cola, Stamin-Up, Juggernog And Deadshot.
  • Not Needed, But Death Perception Would Be A Fantastic Helper In A Few Future Steps. The Scorcher Is Also Not Needed, But It Could Help You Move Around The Dark Aether More Quickly.
  • You Really Don't Have To Go Too Crazy, Because The Act Mission Is Only Tier 2 Zombies.

Act 4 Mission 2: Countermeasures

  1. Enter The Countermeasures Rift At Around F4 On The Urzikstan Map And Follow The Prompts.
  2. Investigate The Distress Signal: Go To The Area It Directs You To. It Should Always Be At D7 On The Dark Aether Map, On The Top Floor, Right Up The Stairs.
  3. Escort The ACV Tank That's Right Outside The Building. It Does Seem To Be Invincible.
  4. When It Stops, Investigate The Source Of Signal Disruption By Interacting With The Console. It Should Always Be In The Same Spot As In My Game, In This Mini Amusement Park (Shown In The Video).
  5. After This We Had To Kill Two Different Mimics To Be Able To Escort The ACV Further.
  6. Shortly After, It Stops In Front Of A Little Red Mushroom Thing. Wait For It To Attach To The ACV To Continue Your Escort.
  7. Clear The Infected Spores From The Mall: Go Around The Mall And Shoot All Of The Spores, Like You Would In Any Other Infested Stronghold Or Aether Nest. Between The Bottom And Top Floors You Have To Shoot 15 Total.
  8. Wait A Couple Of Seconds For Your Crew To Return Back Outside To The ACV With You.
  9. You're Escorted To The Anomaly Site. Activate The PND To Complete The Mission.
  10. Before You're Able To Exfil, A Mangler Variant Spawns. Kill It, Interact With The Reward Portal And Pick Up The Golden Drum From The Rift.

Mall Tips

  • The Spores Can Be Tricky To Find, And You're Limited To As Long As Your Gas Mask Survives.
  • Keep Note Of The Two Ammo Crates In The Mall. They Refill Your Ammo And Your Gas Mask Health. There's One On The Bottom Floor And One On The Top Floor.
  • If Your Gas Mask Breaks, It's Not That Big Of A Deal. There Are A Ton Of Other Gas Masks Hanging Around The Mall To Pick Up.
  • Death Perception Shows The Spores Through The Walls.

Four Parts From This Dark Aether

You Must Get Four Different Parts In This Version Of The Dark Aether, While You're Doing The Story Mission Quest. You Can Do The Seals And The Story Mission At The Same Time, In Any Order. Everything Is Much Easier With A Team: As One Person Does The Story Mission Stuff, The Other Can Work On The Seals. If Done Efficiently, All Of This Can Be Done In One Run, In One Game.

  • Drum: The First Part. It's Already Gold, And You Get It From Completing The Mission. Hold On To It For The Rest Of Your Games.
  • After The Drum You Have The Option To Exfil And Come Back Into The Mission To Run Through A Second Time Faster, Or To Do The Rest Now.

MMA Gloves

  1. Activate The Seal Located On The Ship At Around D5.
  2. An Insta-Kill Spawns. Use It To Melee Kill As Many Zombies As You Can Within The Circle Around The Seal.
  3. Once You Melee Kill Enough Zombies, A Reward Portal Spawns With Your Purple MMA Gloves.

Perforated Target

  1. Activate The Seal At E4.
  2. Headshot Kill Zombies Within The Circle Around The Seal.
  3. Once Done, A Reward Portal Spawns With Your Purple Perforated Target.

Pristine Mirror

  1. Activate The Seal At Around I8. This One Is Tricky.
  2. You Have To Match The Ammo Mod You Use With The Color Of The Circle Surrounding The Seal. On Each Of The Four Corners Of The Seal Is An Ammo Mod You Can Interact With.
  3. When You Interact With One, It Drops Onto The Ground. Then You Can Apply It To Your Weapon.
  4. If The Circle Is Glowing Red Like In My Game, Interact With The Napalm Burst Option, Apply It To Your Weapon And Get Napalm Burst Kills Within The Red Fiery Circle.
  5. Eventually The Color And The Effect Around The Circle Change, And So Does The Ammo Mod You Have To Kill Zombies With. Keep Matching Until A Reward Rift Spawns With Your Purple Pristine Mirror.
  • Red, Fiery Circle: Napalm Burst
  • Smoke: Cryo Freeze
  • Electricity: Dead Wire
  • The Order Is Random, But Everything You Need Is Found Within The Circle. For Me, The Fire Circle Turned To Smoke, Then Electricity Filled The Circle, And That Was It.

Exfil: Use The Portal At The Soccer Field, E5.

Bring All Four Into One Game

  • In Your Rucksack: A Golden Drum, Purple MMA Gloves, A Purple Perforated Target And A Purple Pristine Mirror.
  • These Items Can Be Split Between Your Teammates, But All Items Are Needed Together In The Same Game At The Same Time. If You're Playing Two-Player, Your Friend Can Hold Two Items And You Can Hold Two To Save Rucksack Space, But You Must Both Go In The Same Game.

Gold Upgrades (Any Order)

Load Into A Standard Match Of Zombies. Bring In Some Good Stuff If You Have It, To Save Some Time. The Drum Is Already Gold And Ready To Go.

Gold MMA Gloves

  1. Make Your Way To The Boxing Gym At F8.
  2. Drop Your Weapon In The Ring Via The Rucksack.
  3. Interact With The Floor Of The Ring To Offer The Unattuned Relic.
  4. Right Outside The Ring Are Some Punching Bags. Use Your Fists To Punch Each Bag Two Times, From Left To Right. Each One Sets On Fire.
  5. After The Third And Final One, A Zombie Spawns In The Ring. Melee It To Death For The Gold MMA Gloves.

Gold Perforated Target

  1. Make Your Way To The Target Area At H8.
  2. At One Of The Shooting Stations, Interact With A Case On The Floor To Offer The Unattuned Relic.
  3. Eight Purple Glowing Targets Spawn In (Shown In The Video). Shoot All Of Them. You'll Know You've Shot A Target When The Glow Around It Turns From Purple To Red.
  4. After The Final Target, A Zombie Spawns From It. Killing It Drops Your Gold Perforated Target.

Gold Pristine Mirror

  1. Make Your Way To The Cemetery At I3, To The Gravestone With The Mirror On It.
  2. Offer The Unattuned Relic There.
  3. The Mini Building Right Next To You Begins To Glow. All Around Its Walls, Inside And Outside, Are Ammo Mods.
  4. A Few Zombies Of Different Types Spawn In Front Of The Gravestone. These Have To Be Killed By A Specific Ammo Mod. Grab The One You Need From That Building.
  5. Once You Interact With An Ammo Mod On The Wall, You Must Interact With It Again On The Floor To Apply It To Your Weapon.
  6. The Final Zombie Drops Your Gold Pristine Mirror.
  • Fire Zombie: Napalm Burst
  • Purple Zombie: Dead Wire
  • Smoky Zombie: Cryo Freeze
  • It's Either Random Or Brain Rot Just Isn't Used. If You Do See Something New, Here Or At The Seal In The Dark Aether, Brain Rot Would Be Your Only Option To Fill The Ammo Mod Gap.

The Pedestals

  1. Place All Your Gold Items On The Pedestals At G4, Where A New Dark Aether Rift Spawns.
  2. Each Pedestal Has An Exact Picture Of Its Item. Place The Gold Mirror In The Mirror Pedestal, The Gold Target In The Target Pedestal, The Gold Gloves In The Gloves Pedestal And The Gold Drum In The Drum Pedestal.
  3. A Rift Spawns And Out Of It Spawns A Boss, Which I Believe Will Always Be A Mimic.
  4. Kill This Boss. A Rift Spawns With A Guaranteed Sigil.
  • Once This Easter Egg Is Done, You Can Re-Enter This Dark Aether Through The Larger Rift At Around G4.

Sigil Or Elder Sigil

This Rift Works Exactly The Same As The Bad Signal One: On One Side You Insert A Regular Sigil, On The Other Side An Elder Sigil.

  • Sigil: From This Boss, And Earnable Via Tier 3 Contracts. 30 Minutes In The Dark Aether, And Stuff Is Slightly Easier. You Don't Have The Chance To Earn Any Of The New Schematics, Only One-Time Use Acquisition Cases. You Can Only Get A Case For The V-R11, Not The Schematic That Lets You Craft It Via The Menus.
  • Elder Sigil: If You Have Any From The Other Dark Aether. Only 15 Minutes, And Zombies Are A Bit More Rough. Definitely Hard, But Nowhere Near As Bad As The Bad Signal Elder Sigil Dark Aether.
  • By Completing Contracts In The Elder Sigil Dark Aether You Can Earn As Little As Zero, Or As Many As All Three New Schematics In Any Given Run: Mags Of Holding, Blood Burner Key And V-R11. These Are The Craftable Versions Via The Menu. They Are Not Guaranteed: It's Random What You Get From Reward Rifts.
  • You Can Also Earn Cases In The Elder Sigil Dark Aether, On Top Of Schematics. So If You Don't Get The Schematics, You'll At Least Get A Couple Of Weapon Cases.
  • The Only Way To Get These Schematics Is Via Elder Sigil Contracts In This Dark Aether.
  • Elder Sigils Are Only Earnable Via A Version Of The Dark Aether. You Cannot Earn Them On The Main Urzikstan Map.

Bunny Contracts (Any Order)

Pick Your Poison And Insert The Correct Sigil Into The Correct Side Of The Rift. Have Any Teammate Confirm The Teleport.

  • Eliminate Bounty: The Bunny At C5, In A Building. Interact With The Bunny To Start The Contract. Follow The Marker For The Boss On Your Screen And Defeat It. I've Heard The Boss Could Randomize, But In My Games I've Only Ever Gotten A Mega Abomination.
  • Outlast: The PND Bunny At E7, On The Roof Of A Building. Interact With The Bunny And Head To The Marked PND Location To Complete The Outlast Contract.
  • Aether Reactors: The Bunny At H6, At The Tippy Top Of The Mall Building. Interact With The Bunny, Then Make Your Way To Each Marked Aether Reactor And Interact With It To Disable It. Decoys And/Or Kazimirs Are Extremely Useful Here.
  • Each Contract Gives A Reward Rift And The Ability To Move On To The Next Contract.
  • Do Not Forget To Exfil.
