Necrofluid Gauntlet Abilities Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-10-08

All Necrofluid Gauntlet Abilities Fully Explained, With Tier 3 Pack-A-Punch Gameplay.

To Earn It: Necrofluid Gauntlet Unlock Guide And Necrofluid Gauntlet Solver.

Controls

  • Fire: Left Mouse.
  • Detonate: Right Mouse, Your Other Trigger.
  • Pull It In: Hold Your Reload Button (R On PC).

Bullets

  • You Have 8 Bullets In The Magazine.
  • The Bullets Stay Down On The Map, Whether You Shoot The Ground Or Zombies.
  • Even If You Kill A Zombie, It Doesn't Take The Ammo Out Of Your Magazine.
  • If You Run Too Far Away From The Bullet, You Lose It.
  • No Bullets Down And It Says Zero: Just Hit Reload And You Get Your Ammo Right Back.
  • It's Got Unlimited Ammo, So Long As You Don't Run Away From The Bullets.
Advertisement

Ability 1: Regular Shots

  • Shoot Zombies Regularly. It Does A Really Good Job At Taking Them Out.
  • The Bullets Still Stay Down On The Map For The Other Abilities.

Ability 2: Detonate

  • Use Your Alternate Firing Ability To Blow Up Every Bullet You've Shot Down.
  • It Literally Blows Up Everything. The Closer You Are To It, It Seems The More Effective It Is.
  • The Bullets Come Back Into The Weapon And Restore The Ammo Into The Magazine. Not Even A Need To Reload.
  • Since You Keep Your Ammo, You Can Just Rinse And Repeat.

Ability 3: Pull In Items

  1. Shoot The Item.
  2. Hold Your Reload Button. It Sucks The Item In Towards You.

Works On:

  • Armor Plates
  • Equipment
  • Drops

It's More Of A Tool. If You Get Trapped In A Corner And There's A Nuke, You Can Shoot A Shot Of This And Try To Pull It In.

Pulling Drops

  • It Seems To Be Very Finicky With Drops. You've Got To Be Within A Certain Radius Of The Drop.
  • It Has To Be Very Targeted: Shoot Under It.
  • Shoot It, Start Running In The General Direction Of It, Then Suck It Up And You Should Be Good.
  • You Really Do Have To Be Looking At That Area.

Overheal

  • If You Find A Stim Shot On The Floor, You Can Use It Even When You're At Full Health. For Me It Brought My Health From The Max Of Juggernog With Probiotic, 300, Up To 350.
  • Just By Using The Gauntlet And Getting Kills Regularly, I Got My Health Up To 400 HP.
  • It Doesn't Regenerate Back, But It Allows You To Overheal, So You Have Bonus Health.
  • Pay Attention To Your Health. If You're About To Die, Detonate. Not Only Will It Heal You, It'll Allow You To Overheal.

Best Strategy

Zursa:

  1. Shoot At The Zursa And Let The Bullets Land.
  2. Detonate. You Get The Initial Hit Damage And The Explosive Damage, So You're Doing Double Damage.

A Train Of Zombies:

  1. Shoot All Your Bullets Down.
  2. Get The Zombies Close To You.
  3. Use The Explosive Ability.
  4. Since You Keep Your Ammo, Rinse And Repeat.
