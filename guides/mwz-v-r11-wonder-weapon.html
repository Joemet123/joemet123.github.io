MW3 Zombies V-R11 Wonder Weapon Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-11

Not Good At Killing Zombies In The Traditional Sense, But One Of The Greatest Tools You Can Have In Your Arsenal.

How To Get It

  • Mystery Box.
  • Contracts: The Red Zone Gives You The Best Possible Chance. But The Truth Is, You Can Get It From Anywhere.
  • Dark Aether Contracts: In The Regular Sigil And Elder Sigil Dark Aether. Not Guaranteed At All, But A Much Higher Chance.
  • USB Red Worm Boss Fight: Beat It And Get A V-R11 Case To Drop. That's Where I've Seen The Highest Success Rate So Far. Steps: USB Red Worm Guide.
  • Dark Aether Secret Keys Easter Egg. Steps: Bad Signal Keys And Secret Rooms.
  • It's Still Very Hard To Get. There Are Other Wonder Weapons You Can Get Instead, And With Some Of The Easter Eggs You're Not Even Guaranteed One.

Ammo

  • Not Fantastic When It Comes To Ammo.
  • It Truly Shines With Three Bullets. Three Is The Magic Number: Shoot Two To Three Bullets At A Time For Maximum Results.
  • Refill It Every 60 Seconds From Ammo Crates, Scattered Literally Everywhere Around The Map. You Can See Them On Your Mini Map And Tac Map.
  • Don't Be Scared To Move Around And Use Your Ammo.

Turn Enemies Into Friendly Soldiers

  • Shoot Regular Zombies, Armored Zombies Or Mercenaries To Turn Them Into Friendly Soldiers.
  • I Don't Believe There's Any Rhyme Or Reason To What They Become: Sometimes An RPG, Sometimes An AR, Sometimes A Sniper. That Could Be Incorrect.
  • The Type Is Kind Of Irrelevant. They All Act As Mini Decoys: Zombies Within A Small Radius Hit Them And Not You.
  • They Shoot At The Zombies Too, But Don't Do A Very Great Job At Killing.
  • Sometimes They'll Block Off Certain Areas. They Definitely Come In Clutch.
  • Eventually They Convert Back Into A Zombie For A Split Second And Explode. Zombies Attracted To The Decoy Get The Explosion.
  • It's Not Good At Straight Up Shooting Zombies And Killing Them. It Literally Won't Do That At All.

Bosses

  • Amazing At Killing Bosses. Sometimes It'll Literally Kill Them In One Shot, All The Way Up To Three.
  • The Stormcaller, The Mega Abomination In The Red Zone, The Mangler, The Mimic, The Disciple, Or Even A Dog.
  • Very Few Bosses Won't Die In Three Shots, As Long As You're Shooting Their Critical Areas:
  • Mega Abomination: When His Mouth Is Open.
  • Disciple: The Chest.
  • Mimic: The Mouth.
  • Worm Boss Fights: A No-Go As Your Primary Weapon. Even If It Did Somehow Do Good Damage, Which I Have Not Been Able To Confirm, It Would Still Require Aimbot Levels Of Precision.

Shoot A Teammate

  • They Get 5 Seconds Of Invisibility, A Zombie Blood, And 5 Seconds Of Bonus Damage.
  • They Glow Green While Both Are Active.
  • Multiple Bullets Do Not Seem To Stack It.
  • The Bonus Damage Is Not An Insta-Kill. From My Testing It Seems To Be Along The Lines Of 1.25x To 2x, Closer To A Double Tap. I Can't Confirm This With Numbers.

Repair Vehicles

  • Shoot A Vehicle To Regenerate Its Health: A Car Or Bike You're Driving, Or An ACV You're Escorting.
  • Works In Any Zone Or The Dark Aether.

ACV On Escort Contracts

  • It Seems You Can Only Heal It When It's Stopped.
  • Taking Damage While Moving: Move Away From It So It Stops, Then Shoot It.
  • A Single Bullet May Heal It To 100%, But I Just Shoot Two To Make Sure.
  • Escort Is The Hardest Contract In The Dark Aether, And The V-R11 Is A Huge Difference Maker. Steps: MWZ Contracts Guide.
