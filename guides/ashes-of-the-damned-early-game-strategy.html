Ashes Of The Damned Early Game Strategy

By Joemet123 · 2026-10-08

The Easiest, Most Basic Strategy I Use Every Game. Set Up Before Round 13, No GobbleGums.

Plan In Order

  1. Start: Rampage Inducer On, Spray The Green Pods.
  2. Rounds 1 To 7: Melee Headshots, Quick Revive, Speed Cola, Melee Macchiato, Tomahawk Challenge.
  3. Rounds 7 To 10: Ashwood, Vulture Aid, Pack-A-Punch Build, Juggernog.
  4. Rounds 10 To 12: Vandorn Farm, Stamin-Up, Pack-A-Punch Your Gun.
  5. Round 13 And Up: Wisp Tea, Gun Headshots, Side Objectives.

Spawn Setup

  • Start The Game Up With The Rampage Inducer.
  • Zombies Spawn Quickly: More Zombies, More Kills.
  • Turn It Off Before The Ravager Round.

Green Pods

  1. Pick Up The Fumigator At Spawn (The Gas Tank Or Watering Can).
  2. Spray The Green Pods. There Are Two In Spawn, And They're All Over The Map.
  3. Defend It Long Enough. Don't Let The Zombies Destroy It.
  4. Finished: Salvage, Points, Maybe Even A Pack-A-Punch Crystal.
  5. Water It Again For More. Repeat As Long As You Have A Green Pod.
  • Very Little Risk: The Zombies Run Toward The Pod.

Melee Headshots

  • A Melee Headshot (Zombie Melee Critical Kill) Pays More Than A Normal Melee Kill Or A Gun Headshot.
  • Line Up The White Marker In The Middle Of Your Screen To Their Head.
  • Stragglers, The One-Off Zombies, Are The Best. Aim Higher Than Their Head And Melee.
  • Charge Your Melee: Hold Your Aiming Button, Then Melee. More Damage Per Swing. Matters From Round 5 Or 6.
  • Using Your Bullet Weapon: Get As Many Headshots As You Can.
  • Ravager Rounds: I Really Don't Recommend Meleeing.
  • Pick Up Every Drop, Or The Ravagers Steal Them. Collect All Your Salvage.

Small Bonuses

  • Spores: Melee Them. One Hit Each, Saves Ammo.
  • Green Pods: Run Over Them With Ol' Tessie To Heal Her. Sometimes Salvage, Sometimes Points, Sometimes Both.

Rounds 4 To 7

Buy While You Melee:

  • Quick Revive: Spawn, Server Room.
  • Speed Cola And Melee Macchiato: Heading Toward Blackwater.
  • Get The Free Points From Every Perk Machine.

Melee Macchiato:

  • Even Smoother, Faster Melees.
  • Raises The Round In Which Your Melee Becomes A Two-Hit.
  • Augments In The Video: Espresso, Plus The One That Lets You One-Hit Zombies For Longer.

Tomahawk Target Challenge

  1. In The Blackwater Area, Hit The Trophy.
  2. Run Back Outside And Grab The Tomahawk.
  3. Throw It At The Glowing Target Outside The Map. The Targets Eventually Start To Move.
  4. You Get Tomahawks Back.
  5. Try Not To Miss. Hit One Of The First Two And You Should Be Good. Missing Others Is Fine.
  6. Hit Enough Targets And Mr. Peeks Spawns In, Does A Little Dance And Drops Random Loot.
  • In The Video: 500 Points, A Pack-A-Punch Crystal And A Second Weapon.
  • The Crystal Went On A Bullet Weapon. Keep The Melee Weapon Clean.
  • By Round 7 In The Video: Three Perks, 5,000 Points To Spare.

Rounds 7 To 10: Ashwood

  1. Open The Door Toward Ashwood.
  2. Open The Next Door, Power It Up, Flip The Switch.
  3. Using The Melee Weapon, Buy Vulture Aid Rather Than Double Tap: More Salvage, More Points.
  4. Put Ol' Tessie In Here And Build Pack-A-Punch On Her.
  5. Keep Meleeing, Upstairs Or Downstairs.
  6. Buy Juggernog, The Final Perk In This Area.
  • Video By Round 10: Practically 9,000 Points, Four Perks, Juggernog Fifth.

Tips

  • Grab The Nuke At The End Of A Round.
  • Hold Crouch When You Jump Down And You Combat Roll: No Fall Damage.
  • Ravager Round: You Can Still Spray A Pod. Don't Melee Ravagers: The Reward Is Zero And You're Very Likely To Die.
  • Grab Tier 2 Armor. Always Plate Up If You're Meleeing.
  • Weapon Becomes A Two-Hit: Upgrade Its Rarity To Green. Good For Even Longer.
  • Overheal: A Spare Stim Shot At Full Health Gave An Extra 50 HP In The Video.
  • Take Double Points When It Drops.

Rounds 10 To 12: Vandorn Farm

  1. Go To Vandorn Farm For The Final Generator And Hit The Spores. Then Open Up The Farm.
  2. Grab The Free Points From Death Perception, Hiding In A Corner, Then Flip The Power Switch.
  3. Use The Double Jump To Purchase Stamin-Up.
  4. Probably Pack-A-Punch Your Weapon Now. 7,000 Points To Spare In The Video. Upgraded To Blue For The Wonder Weapon Fight.
  • Result In The Video: Six Perks And Pack-A-Punch On Round 12.
  • Within 20 Minutes You Can Be Set Up Very Well.
  • A Bullet Weapon Gets You Through The First Rounds A Little Quicker, But On Round 13 You Have No Points.
  • Keep Running Ol' Tessie Over Green Pods: She Repairs, You Get Salvage And/Or Points.

Side Objectives

  • Uber Klaus: Round 8 Or Above, Near The Project Janus Crate On The Road Toward Blackwater. Take Him Out, Then Destroy His Core. For The Wonder Weapon, Not Points.
  • Chompy: A Couple Around The Map. Melee Him Open, Then Throw A Tomahawk, A Grenade, Anything Like That Into His Little Black Hole. Free Points Or Salvage.
  • Waking Klaus: Install The Stabilizer Chip First, Then Throw The Stuns Into His Cell.
  • Wisp Tea: Zarya Cosmodrome. Seventh Perk In The Video, On Round 13.

Rounds 13 And Up

  • Armored Zombies Start. Use Your Bullet Weapon, But Still Get Headshots For The Extra Points.
  • Charge Your Melee. With The Melee Macchiato Augments, A Charged Headshot Is Still A One-Hit.
  • Back Away From The Zombies Now. They Do Hit.
  • Round 14 Or 15: Pretty Much Done With The Melee Weapon. To Keep It Going, Pack-A-Punch It And/Or Upgrade Its Rarity.
  • Easter Egg Steps: Just Get Your Headshots. Don't Try For Melee Kills. It Will Cost You.

T.E.D.D. Tasks

  1. Find T.E.D.D. On The Map And Start A Task.
  2. Kill Zombies To Finish It.
  3. Rewards: Pack-A-Punch Crystals, Wonder Weapons, Points, Perks And More.
  • Really Good On The Earlier Rounds. Optional, But A Nice Cherry On Top.

For The Full Quest After You Are Set Up, See The Ashes Of The Damned Main Easter Egg Guide.
