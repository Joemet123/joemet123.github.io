MW3 Zombies Rucksack Explained

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-11

Rucksacks Are Where You Store The Items You've Obtained By Playing The Mode.

What Carries Over

  • If You Successfully Exfil, All The Equippable Items In Your Rucksack Come With You Into The Next Match.
  • That Means Ammo Mods, Aetherium Crystals, Aether Tools, Wonder Weapon Cases, Perk Cans, Self-Revives, Scorestreaks, Anything Marked By An Arrow In Your Inventory.
  • The Ray Gun You Have On You And The Perks You Have Equipped Won't Carry Over. Any Cases And Cans That Are Unused Will.
  • They're Also Stashable In Your Acquisitions Tab.
  • To Open The Rucksack In A Match, Press Down On The D-Pad.
  • Tombstone Lets You Keep The Items In Your Rucksack Even If You Die. Steps In MWZ Tips And Settings.

Schematics

  • A Schematic You Obtain In A Match Goes To Your Rucksack.
  • To Unlock It, You Must Keep It In Your Rucksack And Successfully Exfil. Then You Can Craft It In Your Schematics Menu.
  • Do Not Drop It Or Trade It Out Before Exfilling, Or It Disappears After The Match Ends And Won't Unlock For You To Use.
  • More In Acquisitions And Schematics.

Sizes

  • Small: 5 Slots. What You Spawn In With By Default.
  • Medium: 7 Slots.
  • Large: 9 Slots.

Getting A Bigger Rucksack

Loot

  • You Can Get All Three Sizes By Just Looting.
  • You'll More Than Likely Have To Get The Large One By Doing Contracts And Other Objectives Closer To The Red Zone, Not Necessarily In The Red Zone, Like A Mercenary Stronghold Contract Or A Fortress. If Not, Do Contracts In The Medium Tier Zone.
  • It's Pretty Random. The Closer To The Harder Part Of The Map You Are, The Better The Loot Will Be.

Buy Station

  • There Are Tons Of Buy Stations Around The Map. Look For The Buy Station Symbol On A Slightly Zoomed In Tac Map.
  • Gray Zone: Nothing In Terms Of Rucksacks.
  • Orange Zone: A Medium Rucksack For Only 2,000 Essence.
  • Red Zone: A Large Rucksack For A Large 10,000 Essence.
  • More In Buy Stations Explained.

Which One To Get

  • I'd Recommend Just Going For The Medium Rucksack, Then Try To Run Into Contracts And Objectives That Give You The Large One In The Slightly Higher Threat Zones.
  • It Really All Comes Down To Skill. The Better You Are, The More You Should Focus On Stashing.
  • The Worse You Are, The More I'd Worry About The Now: Perks And Pack-A-Punch, Stuff That Keeps You Alive In Your Current Game.
  • The Price Jump For The Two Extra Slots, From 7 To 9, Is A Bit Much If You Don't Think You're Going To Exfil At Least Five Games In A Row. 10,000 Essence Is No Joke.

Rucksack In The Gear Tab

  • You Keep Stuff When You Exfil, On That Operator. Exfil With A Medium Rucksack, A Two-Plate Armor Vest And A Gas Mask, And As Long As You're That Operator, You Get All Of It Immediately When You Spawn In Next Game.
  • You Can't Carry That Gear Over To Another Operator, But You Can Carry Over Your Acquisitions.
  • Dismissing An Operator Resets All The Gear On That Soldier. Clear Out The Rucksack First, And Make Sure You Don't Need Or Want Anything From It.

Add Or Remove Items

  1. In The Gear Section, Select A Rucksack Slot.
  2. Pick An Item From Acquisitions Or Schematics.
  3. To Take An Item Out, Hover Over It And Hit Unequip. It Goes Back Into Your Acquisitions.
  • I Believe Acquisitions Has A Finite Number Of Spaces (10 In The Video).
  • Anything You Add, You Spawn In With. Once You Use It In A Match, It's Gone.
