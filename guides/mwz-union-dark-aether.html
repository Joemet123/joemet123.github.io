MW3 Zombies Union Dark Aether Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-11

Map, Contracts And Spore Eggs: Union Cheat Sheet. Triangles And Dog Houses: Urzikstan Cheat Sheet.

Before You Start

  • My Class In The Video: WSP Swarm Dual Wielded, Healing Aura, Decoys.
  • The Aether Blade And Gold Armor Plates Are The Best Possible Schematics To Run In Any And All Games Of This Quest. They Make Everything Significantly Easier.
  • Get A Dog, Especially For This Quest. The Dog Is Actually Required For One Of The Steps.

Union Story Mission

  1. On Your Zombies Main Menu, Select Story Missions And Change Your Mission To Act 4 Mission 3: Union.
  2. Set Up In Your Game With At Minimum A Tier 2 Pack-A-Punch, Purple Weapon.
  3. Enter The Dark Aether Rift At E4.
  4. Follow All Of The Prompts. First, Follow Ravenov.

Two Crystals

  1. Two Crystals Spawn, Marked In Game For You. Interact With The Crystal To Inspect It.
  2. Listen Around The Area For Runestones With Symbols Making Very Loud Noises. All Of The Runestones Are Within The Yellow Circle On Your Minimap, In The Top Left-Hand Corner Of Your Screen.
  3. Three Symbols That Match The Runestones On The Walls Appear In Front Of The Crystal.
  4. Shoot The Runestones On The Walls In The Order Shown In Front Of The Crystal, From Left To Right.
  5. Where The Code Sequence Was, There's A Fully Formed Red Crystal. Shoot It To Shatter It.
  6. The Other Crystal Is Marked On The Other Side Of The Map. Same Exact Process: Interact, Shoot The Three Correct Symbols In The Correct Order, Then Shatter The Red Crystal.
  7. Return To Ravenov At The Marked Point On The Map And Watch Jansen Do An Animation.

Four Seals

  1. Three Seals Spawn, Each Marked In The Sky With A Yellow Glowing Light.
  2. To Get To Them You May Have To Parkour And Climb Up Different, Kind Of Weird Angles.
  3. At Each Seal: Interact With It, Then Collect Some Souls. When Finished, The Relic Item Tied To That Seal Flies Up Out Of The Dark Aether (Not Really Important For Anything).
  4. The Fourth And Final Seal Is Only Accessible Once The First Three Are Done. It's Marked Separately With Another Yellow Light In The Sky, Under The Entity.
  5. Activate This Special Seal. A Boss Named Taoxia Spawns.

Taoxia Boss

  1. Shooting Taoxia Damages Her, But At Some Point Her Health Bar Goes Gray, New Marked Mini Bosses Spawn, And She Tries To Regenerate Her Health.
  2. Kill The Mini Bosses. Only Then Can You Damage And Kill Taoxia Again. She Only Takes Damage When The Health Bar Is Red.
  3. Killing Taoxia Spawns A Reward Rift With A Guaranteed Gold Giraffe Toy. Pick It Up.
  4. Interact With Jansen To Have Her Escort You To A Rift Leading Out Of The Dark Aether.

Purple Relics

In The Next Game Or Games You Find The Remaining Relics And Upgrade Them.

  • They Can Be Obtained And Upgraded In Any Order. You Can Get One In One Game, Upgrade It, Then Go Into Another Game. The Goal Is To Get Them All At Once And Upgrade Them All At Once, Hopefully With No Issues.
  • If Somebody In Your Party Has Already Opened This Rift By Doing This Quest, They Can't Re-Obtain The Items. Have The Person Who Has Not Unlocked The Rift Get The Purple Relics By Doing The Steps Themselves. It Can Be In The Same Game.

Purple Science Journal

  1. Locate The Aether Storm On Your Tac Map (Icon Shown In The Video).
  2. It's Recommended To Use A Gas Mask. You'll Be Fighting Extremely Tough And Fast Zombies, On Top Of Being Suffocated To Death By Storm Gas.
  3. Go Into The Storm And Kill Storm Infected Zombies, As Safely And Effectively As You Can, Until A Reward Rift Spawns With Your Purple Science Journal.
  • You Do Not Have To Shoot Or Kill The Stormcaller At All.

Purple Imaginary Friend Drawing

  1. Locate The House At I7.
  2. Go Upstairs Into The Bedroom Shown In The Video.
  3. Go Prone On The Side Of The Bed Shown In The Video And Interact With The Item Under The Bed.
  4. Wait A Few Seconds For A Reward Rift To Spawn With Your Purple Imaginary Friend Drawing.
  • The Interaction Could Be A Little Weird. If You Don't Get A Quote From The Entity, Go Back And Interact With It Again.

Purple Laptop With Stickers

  1. Get A Friendly Dog, Either Through The Dog Bone Schematic Or For Free Via The Dog Houses Around The Map.
  2. Locate Any Of The Mercenary Camps Around The Map (Icon Shown In The Video).
  3. Lead Your Dog To The Mercenary Labeled Sergeant At This Camp. The Dog Has To Kill The Sergeant.
  4. Run Around Not Shooting Anything. That's The Easiest Way. Let The Dog Do His Job.
  5. After The Dog Takes Out The Sergeant, A Reward Rift Spawns With Your Purple Laptop With Stickers.
  • There's A Chance You'll Die, But The Dog Revives You.

Gold Upgrades: Triangle Trio

You Get The First Gold Item, The Giraffe Toy, From The Story Mission. The Remaining Relics Are All Upgraded Via Practically The Same Process: The New Red Zone Easter Egg I'm Calling The Triangle Trio, With One Additional Step.

  1. Locate The Triangle In The Red Zone For Your Relic. Each Side Points Toward A Runestone.
  2. Look In The Three Directions The Triangle Is Pointing In And Shoot The Three Runestones Tied To It.
  3. Go To That Triangle's Alignment Location. Stand In The Spot Where The Triangular Patterns Across The Buildings Align To Form A Triangle. This Starts A Soul Collection.
  4. Before You Place The Relic Down, Confirm It's In Your Rucksack. Place It Down Within The Circle Where Prompted. After You Place It Down It's Gone, And All Teammates Lose The Item.
  5. Let Zombies Enter The Circle For A Pink Glow To Appear On Them Before Killing Them.
  6. If Successful, A Special Enemy Spawns. Killing It Gives A Reward Rift With Your Gold Relic.

Each Relic

  • Gold Imaginary Friend Drawing: Triangle At F5. Alignment Also At F5, On The Bridge. Special Mangler Variant.
  • Gold Science Journal: Triangle At E4. Alignment At E4, On Top Of The Pathway. Special Dog Variant.
  • Gold Laptop With Stickers: Triangle At F5. Alignment At F5, On Top Of The Building. Special Mimic Variant.

Each Location Only Takes Its Own Relic. Exact Spots Are Shown In The Video And On The Urzikstan Cheat Sheet.

Avoid Losing A Relic

  • Other People In Your Game Can Interfere With The Soul Collection Step By Killing Your Zombies. Their Kills Are Wasted: Only Teammate Kills Count.
  • If You Fail Any Of The Upgrades, You Lose The Item And Have To Go Into Another Game To Get It Again.
  • The First Step Could Be Bugged For A Lot Of Different Reasons: Teammates Who Already Completed The Rift Quest, Teammates Not Having The Relics In Their Rucksack, Or Other People On The Map Trying To Do The Same Thing At The Same Time. The Chance Is Extremely Small, But It's Not Impossible. Wait A Few Minutes. If The Runes Or The Patterns On The Buildings Don't Spawn, Exfil And Do It Again In A Fresh Game.
  • I Recommend All Teammates Pick Up All The Relic Items And Do Everything Together If Possible. It's Not Necessary, But It Reduces The Risk Of Any Issues.
  • Each Triangle Is A One-Time Use Easter Egg Per Game. If You Shoot The Runes And Collect Souls Without Placing A Relic Down, You Get The Traditional Rewards, But You Can't Access Those Runes A Second Time In That Game For Your Upgrade.
  • I Do Believe That After You Complete A Relic Upgrade, The Runes Should Respawn, So You Can Do The Regular Version For Its Rewards. It Is Really Finicky Though, So It's Hard To Say With 100% Certainty.

Open The Rift

  1. Make Your Way To E4.
  2. Interact With A Pedestal, Then Interact With The Matching Gold Item In Your Rucksack When Prompted. Each Of The Four Items Has Its Own Pedestal, Shown In The Video.
  3. The Rift Forms, Spawning An Abomination And A Disciple.
  4. Kill The Disciple For A Guaranteed Sigil.

Sigils And Elder Sigils

  • Sigil: Max 30 Minutes. One-Time Use Acquisitions Of This Dark Aether's Schematics Via Contracts. Slightly Fewer And Weaker Zombies. Earned Via Red Zone Sigil Or Elder Sigil Contracts.
  • Elder Sigil: Max 15 Minutes. Both One-Time Use Acquisitions And The Actual Craftable Schematics Via The Same Contracts. The Most Challenging And Fastest Zombies In The Game. Earned Via Any Dark Aether Contract.
  • The Elder Sigil Contracts Here Do Not Guarantee You The New Schematics. It's RNG: You Could Get Them All In One Run, But More Than Likely You Won't, And You Could Get None.
  1. When Ready, Insert Your Sigil Into The Correct Side Of The Rift.
  2. All Players Open Their Tac Map To Accept The Teleport.

Dark Aether Contracts

  • The Contracts Can Be Done In Any Order. Each One Is A Yellow Light In The Sky And Is Also Marked On The Cheat Sheet.
  • Yes, There Is An Escort Contract, So It's Highly Recommended To Have One Person Use A Pack-A-Punched V-R11 As A Secondary To Continually Repair The Escort Tank.
  • Also Buy Some Additional Self-Revives And Kazimir Grenades Before Coming In, As Stuff Gets Very Hectic.
  • Each Contract Gives You A Perma-Perk Item That Greatly Helps Your Survival, At The Expense Of A Rucksack Space. They're Stackable, But They Don't Exfil With You Back To Urzikstan.

Bounty: Clock Tower

  • At E2. Mega Abomination Bounty: Kill The Marked Boss For A Reward Rift.
  • Perma-Perk (Skull Item): Whoever Has This Skull Looking Item In Their Rucksack Has Bosses And Enemies Outlined, And Deals Significantly More Headshot Damage.

Outlast: Water Tower

  • At I3. Activate The PND And Survive Within The Small Area Long Enough To Get A Reward Rift.
  • Perma-Perk (Foot Item): Whoever Has This Foot Item In Their Rucksack Runs Faster, And All Enemies They Look At Slowly Freeze.

Escort

  • Starts At F6. Escort The ACV Tank Around The Map To A Few Different Points Until It Finishes On The Bridge.
  • You Can Repair The Health Of The Tank By Shooting It With A Pack-A-Punched V-R11.
  • Some Mini Bosses Glow Green. They're Guaranteed To Give You A Nuke, So Definitely Kill Them.
  • Perma-Perk (Thumbs-Up Hand Item): Whoever Has This Item In Their Rucksack Has A Chance For Enemies They Kill To Explode. It Does Seem That It Has To Be Headshot Kills.

If You Wish, You Can Exfil From The Dark Aether At G4.

Spore Eggs: Smoke Signals Blueprint

  • Reward: The First Time, The Smoke Signals RAM-7 Blueprint. It Has Limited Ammo, Plus An Alternate Firing Mode With No Ammo Counter, A Chainsaw, That Destroys Enemies Pretty Quickly, Including Mini Bosses, Manglers And Mimics.
  • In The Elder Sigil Dark Aether You Also Get Rewards Equal To The Elder Sigil Contracts, So It's A Fourth Way To Earn The New Schematics. In The Regular Sigil Version You Get Additional Rewards.
  • If You Have Bonus Time In The Dark Aether, Take On This Additional Challenge. It Works With Either Sigil, But I Would Do This One In The Regular Sigil Dark Aether.
  1. This Easter Egg Doesn't Unlock Until You Complete All Three Contracts.
  2. Head To The Central Island.
  3. Break The Three Mini Eggs At F3, D4 And F4.
  4. Shoot The Larger Main Egg At F4. It Bursts Open, Revealing A Mini Boss Fight.
  5. This Boss Only Takes Damage When The Health Bar Is Red. Eliminate Any Bosses It Marks And Spawns In, Then Take It Out.
  6. You Automatically Get Your Smoke Signals Blueprint. Collect Your Additional Rewards From The Reward Rift.

Skip The Contracts With PhD Flopper

  • If You Don't Complete Any Of The Contracts, The Eggs Are White And Unshootable.
  • With PhD Flopper, Dolphin Dive Directly At The Eggs A Few Times. It Reveals The Egg.
  • The Hitboxes On Them Are Very Finicky.
  • Useful If You Fail A Contract Or Just Want To Fight This Boss.
