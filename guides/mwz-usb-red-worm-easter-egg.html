MW3 Zombies Red Worm USB Easter Egg Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-11

The Urzikstan Cheat Sheet Has All The Locations And Coordinates Of Everything You Need.

Reward

This Easter Egg Awards You At Minimum One But Up To All Of These Schematics, In One Game:

  • Tier 3 Flawless Pack-A-Punch Crystal Schematic.
  • Scorcher Wonder Weapon Case Schematic.
  • Gold Legendary Aether Tool Schematic.
  • This Is The Only Way To Get These Schematics.

Before You Start

  • Intended For The Hardest Of The Hardcore Players, Or Those Of You Looking For An Extreme Challenge.
  • These Steps Are Centered Around A Team. Everything Said Goes For The Entire Team.
  • Quite Simple In Terms Of Steps, But Very Challenging To Execute In Game Because Of The Timer.
  • I'd Recommend That Only One Player Goes To Do Contracts While The Others Set This Easter Egg Up.

Step 1: Read A Picture Room

The Four Picture Rooms

  • Urzikstan University: E8
  • Ah-Dahr Estates: G8
  • Kotovo Blocks: C1
  • Orlov Military Base: H2
  1. Make Your Way To One Of The Picture Rooms.
  2. You'll See The Same Pictures As On The Cheat Sheet. Each One Is A Coordinate Where A Console Lies.
  3. Out Of All The Possible Spawn Points There Will Be Only Four Consoles, Hence Only Four Pictures.
  4. Locate The Exact Console Each Picture Represents. The Cheat Sheet Has Every Console Picture Under USB Consoles.
  • You May Use The Cheat Sheet To Skip This Step And Brute Force The Consoles By Checking Every Location Manually. The Consoles Are Scattered All Over The Map And You're Extremely Limited On Time, So This Is Not Advised.

The 12 Console Locations

There Seem To Be 12.

  • Hadiqa Farms: I5
  • Zlatyev Array: I2
  • Kotovo Blocks: C1
  • Zaravan City: D6
  • Shahin Manor: H8
  • Popov Power: F3
  • Opal Palace: F5
  • Urzikstan Community Center: D8
  • Al-Abboud Condos: C4
  • Nahr Bathhouse: F4
  • Shorok Opera House: F7
  • Opal Palace: G4

Step 2: Collect The Four USBs

  1. Run Up To Each Console And Interact With It For A Mini Animation. Out Will Pop Your USB Flash Drive.
  2. Each Console Has A Specific USB Flash Drive: Alpha, Beta, Charlie Or Delta.
  3. Collect All Four. You Can View Which Flash Drives You Have In Your Rucksack.

Step 3: Get Set Up Before The Storm

Now Wait For The Storm To Roll In. This Is Your Time To Get Set Up. You're Limited On Time To Kill The Boss, So Being Properly Set Up Is No Joke.

  • Tier 3 Pack-A-Punch With At Minimum A Purple Weapon. Gold Is Highly Recommended.
  • The MCW And Holger Are Common Purple And Gold Wall Weapons In The Red Zone. They Will More Than Likely Be Your Best Option.
  • Do Contracts To Earn Essence: Tier 2 Or Tier 3 Raid Weapon Stash, Deliver Cargo And Possibly Even Outlast. Do Whatever Is The Fastest. If You Can Do An Elimination Fast, Do It.

I'd Recommend At Minimum:

  • Tier 3 Pack-A-Punch On Your Gun: 30,000 Essence.
  • Your Six Basic Perks: Stamin-Up, Quick Revive, Speed Cola, Juggernog, Deadshot Daiquiri And PhD Flopper. Maybe Tombstone, Just In Case You Die, So You Can Carry Over Any Leftover Essence. 12,000 To 14,000 Essence.
  • Self-Revives: Three Plus Your Equipped One. 20,000 Essence.
  • Sentry Turrets: Three Plus Your Equipped One. 8,000 Essence.
  • Durable Gas Mask: 4,000 Essence.
  • Roughly 74,000 Essence Total, So You Need To Be Doing A Lot Of Contracts.

Before Your Match Starts:

  • Run Healing Aura And Bring In Gold Armor Plates And The Aether Blade, So You Can Regen Health Fast And Kill Any Zombies And Dogs On Your Backside While You're Shooting At The Boss.
  • Spawn In With Stuff To Make The Contract Burden Less: Pack-A-Punch Crystals, Perks, Schematics And Gas Grenades. Gas Grenades Actually Deal A Decent Amount Of Damage To The Boss.
  • This Is The Time To Use All Of Your Good Stuff.

Step 4: Find The Boss Arena

  1. Check The Tac Map For One Of The Boss Fight Locations: Two Ammo Crates Together.
  2. It Will Always Be Close To The Storm. If The Storm Spawns In The Top Left Of The Map, The Boss Fight Probably Won't Be In The Lower Right-Hand Corner.
  3. The Cheat Sheet Shows The Four I Believe Are All The Possible Locations, Under Worm Fight.
  4. Around Your Boss Arena You'll Notice Four Refractors.
  • Make A Good Mental Note Of Where The Ammo Crates Are. You Need To Visit Them Constantly.

Step 5: Start The Fight

  1. As Soon As The Storm Rolls In And Covers A Refractor In The Arena, It Becomes Active.
  2. Override It By Inserting One Of Your USB Flash Drives. You And Your Teammates Check Your Rucksacks And Match Each Drive Up To The Correct Refractor.
  3. Insert All Four And The Boss Fight Will Start.
  4. Equip And Place Down All Your Sentry Turrets. They'll Help Kill The Zombies While You Focus On The Boss.

The Boss Fight

Timer

  • Yes, You Are Fighting This Boss In The Storm.
  • Your Timer Is The One On The Left-Hand Side, Until The Storm Completely Rolls Over The Whole Map And That Hits Zero.
  • It Varies. It Could Be 8 Minutes, It Could Be 10.

Ammo Crates And Gas Mask

  • Your Durable Gas Mask Only Lasts About 60 Seconds Or So. Keep Running To The Ammo Crate To Refill It On Top Of Your Ammo.
  • Each Ammo Crate Is Individually Timed. If One Still Has, Say, A 20-Second Timer And You Need Your Grenades Or Gas Mask, Go To The Other One.

Doing Damage

  • Shoot His Glowing Body Spots, His Mouth When He Lasers You, And Anywhere You Get Double Hit Markers.
  • No Double Hit Markers Means You're Doing No Damage.

His Attacks

  • He May Slam Down On You, Laser You, Eat You Or Tunnel Under The Ground By You.
  • Keep Moving Around. Shoot And Focus On Those Weak Spots, But Keep Moving. Downing From Him Should Then Be Minimal.
  • Getting Eaten: Spam Your Deploy Parachute Button While You're In His Mouth (X In The Video). A Lot Of The Time, When He Chews You Up And Spits You Out, You Instantly Redeploy Instead Of Hitting The Ground Hard And Dying.
  • You'll Still Have A Lot Of Zombies To Kill. The Sentry Turrets Reduce The Burden On You A Little Bit.

The Reward Rift

  1. Kill The Boss Quickly, Because You're On A Timer.
  2. Once He's Dead, A Rift Spawns With High-Quality Rewards And Your Only Chance At The Three Schematics.
