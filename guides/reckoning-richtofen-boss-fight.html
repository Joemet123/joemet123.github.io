The Reckoning Richtofen Boss Fight Solo Strategy

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-11

My Solo Richtofen Boss Fight With No GobbleGums. The Video Is A Full Game With Commentary: It Shows The Main Quest Steps But Doesn't Explain Them, So This Page Only Covers My Setup And The Boss Fight.

Get Set Up First

  • I Slow Played Everything Just To Ensure That I Got Set Up. It Took Me One Hour Exactly To Get To The Richtofen Fight. To Be Honest With You, Sometimes It's More Worth It To Just Get The Easter Egg Done Than To Set Up.
  • Take Advantage Of All The Free Stuff On This Map. They Give You A Lot: Free Perks, Free Essence From The Gold Bins And The Paintings, And More In The Reckoning Side Easter Eggs Guide.
  • I Grab Quick Revive First. By The Boss Fight I Had Seven Perks, Pretty Much All The Main Ones. I Didn't Get Death Perception.
  • Go For The Aether Blade. No Reason Not To. If You're Not Pack-A-Punched, The Disciple From The Safe Is Beefy. It Cost Me My Quick Revive. He Does Not Seem To Leave That Room.
  • Once You're Done With Mister Peeks, Switch Over To Aether Shroud.
  • For The Gorgofex Door Code I Use The Gorgofex Solver.
  • You Need The Gorgofex Even If You're Not Going To Use It. It's Required For The Easter Egg. Reckoning Gorgofex Guide.
  • The Free Deadshot Easter Egg Is Very Hard With Some Weapons. It's Doable, But Harder. I Shot More Than One Shot And Failed It. I Should Have Done It With The Hand Cannon.
  • Targets: The First Wave Just Gives You An Ammo Mod, But If It's Cryofreeze, That's A Free Ammo Mod. After Round 11 The Second Wave Gives You A Purple Aether Tool. Reckoning Target Easter Egg Guide.
  • I Held Off On Pack-A-Punch, Because On Round 16 The Target Easter Egg Gives You The Tier 2 Crystal. But Not Pack-A-Punching May End Up Costing You More Than 5,000 Essence.

Before You Go In

  • Tier 3 Armor, Tier 3 Pack-A-Punch, A Gold Weapon And Three More Plates Ready To Go.
  • I Wanted 6,000 Essence Before Going Into The Boss Fight.
  • Get Your Max Ammo Before You Go Anywhere.

Loadout

  • GPR: Really Something Special. No Recoil, High Damage.
  • Death Machine: Many People Have One. It's A Little Useful And Gets Off A Little More Health, But At The End Of The Day It's Really Not The Be-All And End-All. I Didn't Have One.
  • Aether Blade: Really Underrated, Especially If You Pair Good Augments With It. If You Get Overrun, Just Throw It.
  • Field Upgrade: Aether Shroud.

The Boss Fight

  1. You're Helping S.A.M. Fight Richtofen.
  2. First, Kill Zombies. On Solo It Just Takes 10, Then One More Round Of 10.
  3. Then Shoot Down The Crystals (Shown In The Video).
  4. If You Are Below Round 25, A Wunderfizz Does Not Spawn. On Round 25 Or Above, One Does.
  5. Then The Richtofen Fight Itself. The Spot I Hold For Most Of The Second Half Of The Fight Is Shown In The Video.

Tips

  • If You Go Down At All During The Boss Fight, Save And Quit Your Game. This Way You Can Come Back In Again And Do The Boss Fight Again. That's An Amazing Feature, Especially With A Boss Fight This Difficult. More On Saving: BO6 Zombies Save And Quit Explained.
  • The Uber Klaus And More Klaus Spawn In. Get Rid Of Them, They Shred You.
  • Make Sure You're Armored Up As Much As Possible.
  • You've Got To Hit Your Shots.
  • I Threw Smokes Down Through The Fight, And Picked Up A Free One I Saw On The Floor. When I Couldn't Buy Smokes, I Had To Do It Legit.
  • Don't Forget Your Aether Shroud. I Forgot I Had It.
  • Ammo And Essence Run Out. I Ran Out Of Both, And After The First Wave I Really Just Had To Kill For Essence To Get Smokes Again.

More On This Map

  • Gorgofex, All 3 Variants
  • Aether Blade
  • Target Easter Egg
  • Free Power-Ups, All 8 Locations
  • Side Easter Eggs, Free Perks And Loot
