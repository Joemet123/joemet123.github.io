Rex Infernus Main Easter Egg Guide, All Steps

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-07

All The Map Puzzles, All The Hidden Parts, Aligning Those Pesky Pillars, Then Wrecking Our Beloved Warden In The Boss Fight.

Before You Start

  • If You Ever Fail A Step And Can't Proceed, Go To The Next Round To Try Again.
  • Every Solver Tool For This Map: Rex Infernus Tools.

Power, Pack-A-Punch And The Void Claw

  1. Hold Out In The House Until Round 3.
  2. From Then On, At The Beginning Of Each Round Until You Leave The House, The Downstairs Phone Rings For A Few Seconds. Answer It And The Front Door Blows Open, Revealing A Portal You Can Take To The Nexus Forge.
  3. Jump Up At The Center And Place The World Seed In The Pedestal To Spawn Flying Skulls.
  4. Keep Shooting A Skull Until It Goes Transparent And Flies Away Toward One Of The Temples. Follow It And Shoot It Again. Keep Doing That Until It Reaches One Of The Braziers And Fires Up An Usurped Flame.
  5. Interact To Pick It Up. Deliver It Back To One Of The Two Braziers Next To The World Seed With Your Fire Button.
  6. Repeat This On The Other Skull And Fire Up The Other Brazier With Its Flame.
  7. Crank Both Wheels In The Center Of The Nexus To Unlock Power, Pack-A-Punch In The Center Blue Portal, And Grapple Guns At The Green Stations Around The Map.

Void Claw

  • The Grapple Gun Is Called The Void Claw And Is On A 30 Second Timer.
  • You Can Get It Up To Three Times Per Round.
  • With It, You Can Drag Purple Orbs.

Astral Flame

  1. Grapple And Drag One Of The Purple Orbs To Pack-A-Punch In The Rotunda. It'll Spaz Around It.
  2. Crank One Of The Wheels. Both Upper Braziers Light Up With A Purple Astral Flame.
  • Pack-A-Punching A Weapon Also Makes An Astral Flame And Skips The Crank Cooldown (About 90 Seconds).

Caltheris: Pressure Plate Lights

  1. At Caltheris Main Chamber, Stand On The Four Stone Squares One At A Time.
  2. On Each One, The Goal Is To Shoot The Yellow Lights That Spawn Around You.
  3. After All Four, The Right Wall Opens With A Lantern. Interacting With It Spawns Purple Orbs Onto The Map.
  • You Can Interact With The Lantern Again Every Round For 3 To 4 Fresh Orbs.

Veytharion: Cube Puzzle

  • In The Veytharion Sanctuary There Are Four Cubes In The Same Locations Every Single Game. Only Their Symbol Changes. For Some Of Them You'll Have To Wall Jump.
  • Interact To Pick One Up, And Hold Interact To Keep Carrying It. They Can Be Dropped If Needed, Just Don't Do It While Jumping Or They Break.
  • They All Get Delivered To The Left Holder, Then Moved Across: Veytharion Blocks Solver. It's The Guaranteed Way To Beat This Every Single Time, Whatever Your Starting Pattern.
  • Never Have Fire Touching Water Or Plant. The Hand Is Your Buffer And Your Main Transporter: Whenever The Hand Is In The Transporter, Melee Or Shoot The Dial To Transport It To The Other Side.
  • Puzzle Done: The Wall Opens With A Water Well Plus A Free Perk.
  • Remember The Well. Interacting With It And Then Flipping The Round Makes It Rain For The Entire Next Round. Needed Later.

Dravakar: Riddle Wall And Handles

  1. In The Back Of Dravakar Sanctuary There's Four Handles And A Blue Riddle Wall. Interact With The Blue Riddle To Have One Of Four Riddles Read Out To You. Subtitles Do Help.
  2. Turn The Correct Handles For Your Riddle: Dravakar Riddle Solver.
  3. Confirm With The Central Handle. A New Room Unlocks Above You, Reachable Only With The Grapple Gun. Used Later.

Nyxara: Crystals And Secret Room

  1. At Dravakar's Entrance Doorway, Wall Jump Or Grapple To The Broken Balcony.
  2. Blast Open The Right Side Of The Wall With Either A PhD Dive Or An Explosive To Get The Fracture Of Nyxara.
  3. In The Nyxara Area, Double Jump Or Grapple Up To The Empty Holster To Place The Crystal.
  4. Grapple At The Surrounding Holsters To Change The Direction Of The Little Arrow Under Them: Nyxara Crystals Order. They Must Be Set To The Same Directions Every Game.
  5. Get And Throw An Astral Flame At The Back Of The Area To Charge The Eye And Have It Pulse Out Purple Balls. They'll Bounce Crystal To Crystal.
  6. If The Arrows Were Set Right, A Secret Area Is Revealed At The Left Side Of The Chamber.

Warden's Blight Wonder Weapon

  1. At The Nyxara Secret Room, Interact With The Orb To Get Full Access To The Underground Secret Area.
  2. Just Survive, Letting The Vine Drain Your Life.
  3. When Done, Interact With The Fruit Inside The Vine To Eat It. You Must Be On Round 8 Or Above.
  4. This Brings You To A Forest Where You Can Grab All Three Wonder Weapon Parts.

Forest Parts

Each Part Is By A Body. Go Near It, A Mini Animation Plays, Then Grab The Part.

  • Part 1: Over To The Left, In The Cabin Where You Spawn.
  • Part 2: Veering Off To The Right, In The Middle, By The Charred Body Laying Against The Rocks.
  • Part 3: Over By The Cave On The Left Side. It's Finicky.
  • You May Not Get All Three Parts In One Run, And That's Fine. Come Back Next Round. You Can Eat The Fruit Again One Time Per Round For The Rest Of The Game.

Build It

  • Grapple Into The Ceiling Room You Unlocked Earlier At The Back Of Dravakar's Chamber (Dravakar Inner Sanctum). Forge Your Warden's Blight At The Table.

Cleanse Items

Each Temple Has Its Own Item To Cleanse It Later On, But Getting Them Now Makes Sense.

Dravakar: Blacksmith's Hammer

  1. Get A Weapon With The Shadow Blast Ammo Mod.
  2. In Dravakar's Wonder Weapon Forge Room (Dravakar Inner Sanctum) You'll Notice Four Shields. Get A Shadow Blast Activation Very, Very Close To Each Shield To Reveal A Secret Compartment.
  3. One Will Be The Blacksmith's Hammer. One Will Be Shadow Rift, Which You Can Use For The Next Item.

Nyxara: Ancient Scroll

  1. Get A Weapon With Shadow Rift And Go To The Forest.
  2. Have Shadow Rift Activate On A Horde Of Zombies. It Takes Enough Kills Specifically From The Shadow Rift Ammo Mod.
  3. One Of The Zombies That Falls To The Ground From The Sky Drops The Scroll. You'll Also Hear It.

Caltheris: Shimmering Thread

  1. Drag Purple Orbs Into The Map And Interact For Rewards.
  2. One Eventually Gives The Shimmering Thread.
  • Spawn More Orbs At The Caltheris Lantern You Unlocked Earlier.

Veytharion: Woven Sash

  • Only Possible On A Rain Round. Force One By Interacting With The Veytharion Well And Then Flipping The Round.
  • It's Nearly Impossible To See. Just Spam Interact As You're Following.
  • Ruinas Insula: Near PhD Flopper.
  • Aranea Insula: That Middle Island, Left Of Widow's Wine.
  • Spira Insula: On The Rock Near Juggernog.
  • You Can Jump Or Grapple To All Of Them.

The House: Symbols And The Eye

  1. Shoot The Basketball On The Top Of The House. It Won't Appear If Anyone Is In The House.
  2. Every Round After That, A New Symbol Appears On The House. Note Only Its Position.
  3. After Four Rounds, Four Symbols Will Have Spawned And The Children Will Be In The Window. No More Symbols Are Coming.
  4. Wait For An Exfil Round. Only Once You Hear The Exfil Bells, Shoot All Four Symbols With Any Weapon In The Order They Spawned.
  5. If Done Right, The Kids Start Dancing. Quickly Start An Exfil, Kill The HVT And Teleport. It'll Say Her House. If Not, You're Going To Exfil To The Main Menu.
  6. Upstairs, Shoot The Toilet With A Charged Warden's Blight Shot. Grab The Eye (Eye Of The Forge) That Drops On The Floor.
  7. After Some Surviving, Answer The Phone To Return To The Map.
  • For This Step You Need A Pack-A-Punched Warden's Blight. If You Forget, The Children Give You A Tier 1 PaP Crystal As Soon As You Get There.

Void Talon Grapple Upgrade

  1. The Eye Can Be Built On Only One Single Grapple Pedestal Around The Map. I Recommend The One By The Nexus, Because That's Where A Lot Of Stuff Goes On.
  2. That Grapple Gun Upgrades To The Red Void Talon. Each Activation Lasts 60 Seconds, And It Seems To Have Slightly Better Range As Well.

Rebuild The Nexus

  1. Around The Nexus There Are Three Pillars With Eyeball Slabs On Them: One Outer Ring, One Middle, One Inside. Use The Red Grapple Gun To Rip Them Off.
  2. Head Down Into The Nexus Core Under Pack-A-Punch. There Are Six More Eye Slabs On Opposite Sides To Rip Off. 12 Total.
  3. They Reveal Webbed Gears. Use Explosives Or A Flame To Clear The Webs.
  4. Crank The Wheel At Pack-A-Punch, Then Very Quickly Sprint Down The Stairs On The Wall-Buy Side And Flip The Floor Handle Closest To The Perk Machine. One Interact, About A 10 Second Window.
  5. This Unlocks The Three Monolith Pillar Handles Upstairs.

Titan Trap Symbols

  • At Caltheris Entrance And Veytharion Passage, Giant Titan Traps Can Be Found.
  • When Activated, They Display Symbols On The Handcuffs Of Both Hands.
  • Note Which Titan Has Which Symbol On The Handcuff Of Which Hand For Later. There's Only Four Symbols.

Nexus Pillar Alignment

  • Don't Randomly Spin The Pillars. Read Their Positions First.
  • There's Three Pillars With Handles: An Inner One (Closest To Pack-A-Punch), A Middle One And The Outer One (Closest To The Shadowsmith Temples).
  • Every Pillar Is Facing Something: One Of The Shadowsmith Temples, The House, Or None Of The Above. Stand Directly Behind The Pillar, Facing The Opposite Direction Of Pack-A-Punch, To See What It's Facing.
  1. The Goal Is To Align All Three Pillars Facing The Shadowsmith Temple You Want To Work On. Open The Nexus Pillar Handles Solver. Set The Floor Handle Under Pack-A-Punch, The Temple You Want, And What Each Pillar Is Facing.
  2. Turn Each Pillar The Amount Of Turns It States. Only Total Turns Per Pillar Matter, Not The Order You Do Them In.
  3. When Done Right, A Blue Laser Fires At The Temple.

Cleansing A Temple

Each Temple Is The Same Process, Just In That Area, With One Exception: The Slab Challenge.

  1. Align The Pillars On The Shadowsmith Temple You're Working On.
  2. Put That Temple's Item In Its Main Chamber Brazier. Place It Before You Pick Up A Flame: Interact Doesn't Work While Holding One.
  3. Spam The Brazier With An Astral Flame To Reveal A Symbol With Little Symbols Surrounding It That Match The Titan Handcuffs.
  4. Guide A Zombie To The Flame And It'll Be Imbued With That Symbol. You'll See It Kind Of Puffing Around It. Decoys Help.
  5. Guide The Zombie To The Titan Trap, To The Hand With The Same Symbol. The Matching Hand Crushes The Zombie, Breaking Its Handcuff. At The Right Titan The Zombie Walks Under The Right Hand On Its Own.
  6. This Unlocks A Floor Slab Right In The Temple's Entranceway. Interact With It For A Quote, Then Get On It And Complete A Mini Challenge (Below). The Slab Moves When Done.
  7. Shoot A Charged Warden's Blight Shot To Have The Disc On The Floor Raise Up And Fire A Beam. The Closer To The Middle You Shoot The Disc, The Less Aggressively It Repositions The Beam. Aim It (Below).

Slab Challenges

The Community's Best Knowledge Right Now. You're Only Doing The Challenge For The Temple The Blue Beams Are Pointing At.

  • Caltheris: Aim Down Sights At The Mystery Box For 30 Seconds.
  • Dravakar: Replate (3 To 5 Armor Plates) While On It. Taking Damage Is Not The Trigger.
  • Nyxara: Go Prone For About 30 To 60 Seconds.
  • Veytharion: Spam Jump For About 30 To 60 Seconds.

Beam Aim

  • Caltheris: Near The Corner.
  • Dravakar: Up On The Wall.
  • Nyxara: Far Below The Map On The Left, Right Below The White Outline.
  • Veytharion: Through The Left Zombie Spawn.

Break The Wall And Finish

  1. If Aligned Right, Getting Some Kills Charges The Wall Orange And Blasts It Open.
  2. Shoot The Face, Then Destroy All The Purple Shadow Zombies (Some Floating, Some Below The Map, Also Visible On The Mini-Map). Then Shoot The Shadowsmith And The Wall Again Until The Screen Flashes White.
  3. Directly Under The Shadowsmith's Face Outside, Grapple An Orb, Then Shoot A Charged Warden's Blight Shot To Have The Orb Infuse Up Into The Shadowsmith. For Nyxara, It Has To Be On The Floating Rock.
  4. Interact With The Veytharion Well To Make It Rain Next Round. Otherwise The Next Step Doesn't Work.
  5. In The Rain, Grapple An Orb Into The PaP Rotunda, Then Crank For Some Astral Flames. Pick Up A Flame And Throw It Between The Eyes, At The Very Small Glowing Marker. For Nyxara, Grapple To The Nose And Throw At About The Same Time. It's The Easiest Way.
  6. If Done Right, Lightning Strikes, Cleansing That Shadowsmith's Area. Finish Each Temple Before You Move The Pillars To The Next. Repeat For The Other Three.

The Warden Boss Fight

After Cleansing All Four Shadowsmiths, Interact With The Portals On The Bottom Floor Of The Nexus Core To Start.

  • He Moves Fast And Can Knock You Off The Map, So Never Stop Moving.
  • All Phases Are Practically The Same. He Just Gets More Aggressive As He Gets Angrier.
  • Always Be Shooting The Stingers That Spawn, To Avoid A Potential Wipe Or Getting Nicked By Them.
  • The Skulls Circling Him Are Massive. Take Those Down To Kind Of Stun Him, Then Deal Damage To The Tip Of His Tail. Otherwise He Takes Minimal Damage.
  • Watch Out For The Purple Shadow Souls On The Outskirts. Take Those Out Over All Things When You See Them. From Phase 2 On They're His Armor: No Stun While Any Are Alive.
  • About Two-Thirds Of The Way Through His Meter, A Special Trial Happens. Spray And Take Out Anything And Everything In That Room. Reward: +350 HP For 60 Seconds.
  • The Grapple Gun, Used Right, Can Be Huge In Avoiding Attacks. Use It Wisely.
  • He Fakes His Death Before The Last Phase, Then Gets Ultra Aggressive. Stick To The Game Plan.
  • You Get Shadowsmith Blessings Throughout The Fight. They Seem Focused On Damaging Those Skulls, And Some Give You A Speed Boost. If You See Them, Pick Them Up. They're Not Nerfs.
