Astra Malorum Main Easter Egg Guide, All Steps

By Joemet123 · 2026-10-08

The Full Astra Malorum Main Quest Easter Egg, Every Step In Order: Power And Oscar, The LGM-1 Wonder Weapon, Oscar's Planets, The Brain, The Statues, Mars, The Mars Bird, The Piano Pillars And The Caltheris Boss Fight. The Video Above Covers It In Under 5 Minutes.

The Tools For This Map Are On The Astra Malorum Tools Page: The Full Map Cheat Sheet, The Planet Order Solver, The Statues Solver And The Pillar Piano Solver.

Power, Pack-A-Punch And Oscar

First, Turn On Power And Pack-A-Punch As Guided By The Game. Quick Recap:

  1. Activate The Piano In The Observatory Dome To Ping A Component Next To A Zombie In Both The Luminarium And Machina Astralis.
  2. Retrieve Both Parts And Place Them Into The Piano, Then Hold Interact To Repair It.
  3. Interact Again For A Defend That, When Completed, Spawns An Oscar For The First Time.

Oscar Tip: If You're In Standard Mode, You Can Spot And Even Ping Oscar On Your Tac-Map.

LGM-1 Wonder Weapon

Collect 4 Parts (Any Order)

  • Car Battery: Shoot A Pack-A-Punched Weapon At Tessie In Spawn.
  • Aberrant Wires: Locate And Shoot One Of The Blinking Lamp Posts Anywhere In The Outside Areas Of The Map.
  • Damaged Drone Part: Shoot Down One Of Oscar's Drones.
  • 3 Absolute Zero Fragments: Use A Weapon With The Cryofreeze Ammo Mod To Shoot Any 3 Purple Crystals Around The Map, To Get 3 Fragments Total, One From Each Crystal.

Defeat Oscar In 3 Traps (Any Order)

After Oscar's Third Trap Kill, Your Wonder Weapon Drops.

The Luminarium Laser Trap

  1. Get Oscar, Then Interact With The Trap Twice: First To Place Down Parts, Then To Activate It.
  2. Run Oscar Around The Trap Room Until Dead.

Museum Infinitum Rocket Trap

  1. Get Oscar And Interact With The Speaker Side Of The Rocket Display To Place Down Your 3 Fragments.
  2. Keep Oscar Within The Trap's Smoke Blast As The Countdown Reaches Zero To Have Him Killed.

Observatory Dome Beam

  1. Listen Closely And Look At The 9 Steaming Valves: 2 In The Upper Catwalk, 4 Around The Main Walls And Pillars, 2 On The Bottom And 1 Out Next To The Teleporter.
  2. Only One Will Be Active At A Time, Transferring Steam To The Next After Being Shot. Do This For 5 Total Steaming Valves In Quick Succession To Watch The Telescope Above Pack-A-Punch Align.
  3. Get A New Oscar To The Area And Interact With The Left Side Of The Piano To Go Into The Telescope. Locate And Hover Over The Sun To Shine A Beam.
  4. Get And Keep Oscar In The Beam And He'll Blow Up.

Oscar's Planets

  1. Get Above Round 10 And Secretly Creep Behind A New Oscar Without Irritating Him.
  2. After Going Through Eliminations 18, 7 And 12, He'll Say Elimination 20, Followed By Thurston Speaking Out 3 Planets.
  3. Convert Them To Numbers In The Exact Order Stated, Using The Planet Order Solver Or The Cheat Sheet.
  4. Insert Them Into The Machine In The Pack-A-Punch Room.
  5. Jump On The Lift To Grab The Key.

Transfer And Defend The Brain

  1. Head To Museum Infinitum And Shoot The Glass To Grab The Saw.
  2. In The Next Room At Machina Astralis, Interact With The Chamber To Unlock It, Exposing A Head. Hold Interact To Cut Out Its Brain.
  3. Go To The Luminarium And Interact 2 Times With The Jar To Swap Brains With It And Start A Defend.
  4. When Done, Pick Up The Brain And Deliver It To Machina Astralis, Next To The Teleporter.
  5. Then Hold Interact To Complete The Connection.

Statues

Use The Statues Solver For This Step.

  1. In The Next Room, The Archive Of Orbis, 3 Different Books Can Be Found On 3 Bookshelves Next To 3 Different Statues: Next To The Banister, Next To The Gramophone And Next To The Painting By The Exit.
  2. Each Statue Is Always Next To The Same 3 Books Every Game.
  3. Read The Monitor To The Left Of The Brain You Just Placed Down And Count How Many Books Appear For Each Statue's Bookcase.
  4. Then Turn Each Statue That Amount Of Times.
  5. Thurston's Reading List Is Not A Book, But Rather Indicates The Beginning Of Your Sequence.
  6. Example: If You Get Witchlight Codex, The Black Veil And The Unknowable Void, That Would Be 1 Book From Each Bookcase, So Turn Each Bookcase's Statue 1 Time.
  7. If You Fail, You Have To Try Again Next Round.
  8. Then Climb The Ladder And Grab Neptune. It Can Be Placed On The Pole Up The Stairs At Machina Astralis.

Unlocking Mars

  1. Head To The Observatory Dome And Into The Telescope By Interacting With The Left Side Of The Piano.
  2. Locate Mars And Note Its DEC Number As 2 Separate Numbers Between The Special Characters. If One Number Is 1 Digit, Add A Zero In Front Of It. Example: If You Get 46, Then 1, Your Code Is 4601.
  3. At Machina Astralis, Insert This Code Into The Machine Up The Stairs From The Armor Wall Buy.
  4. Find 3 Pieces Of Paper, Always Spawned On The Same Tables: The Luminarium Trap Room, The Archive Of Orbis And Machina Astralis.
  5. The Directions On The Papers Indicate How You Need To Align Neptune, Saturn And Mars On The Ceiling Of Machina Astralis. Do It By Shooting The Planets, Getting As Close As Possible To The Listed Direction.
  6. When Done, Interact With The Brain To Start A UFO Lockdown.
  7. To Complete It, Destroy Enough Of The Special Beeping UFOs That Oscar Disperses Before They Hit The Portal On Top.
  8. If Done Right, The Teleporter Will Activate And Bring You To Mars.

Mars Bird

  1. At Mars, The Brain Canister Will Fall To The Center. Pick It Up And Build It On The Machine Upstairs. You'll Hear A Type Of Noise Coming From It.
  2. Shoot The Coils To Spark Them Blue, Waiting About A Second Between Shots For A Bird To Travel To Each, In This Order: Back Right, Back Left, Front Right, Front Left.
  3. Then Shoot The One Behind And Above You Every 10 Seconds Until You're Able To Capture The Bird Through Wall Jumping And Low Gravity Jumping.
  4. Place It Where The Video Shows.

Mars Piano Code Pillars

Use The Pillar Piano Solver Or The Cheat Sheet For This Step.

  1. Shoot The Coils Atop The 5 Pillars Around Mars With The Wonder Weapon And Have Its UFOs Activate Them: 1 On The Back Right, 2 In The Back Left And 2 Upstairs.
  2. Head To The Observatory Dome And Interact With The Right Side Of The Piano For It To Play A 5-Sequence Code. 4 Flashes Will Be Symbols And 1 Flash Will Be Static.
  3. Note The Full 5-Symbol Sequence In Its Exact Order, Including When The Static Appeared.
  4. Back At Mars, Interact With The Pillars In That Exact Order, Filling In The Static With Your Only Missing Symbol In The Static Position Of The Sequence.

Caltheris Boss Fight

The Boss Fight Can Now Be Started By Interacting With The Brain At Mars.

Phase 1: The Caltheris Skull

The First Main Boss Phase Is The Caltheris Skull, Which Has 3 Mini Phases. All Require You To Collect Souls While Avoiding The Boss's Attacks. When Each Mini Phase Is Complete, You'll Get A White Flash, And At That Point You Must Hold Interact On The Brain To Fire At The Skull.

  1. Mini Phase 1: Collect Souls On The Right, Avoiding Zombies, Mimics And Lasers.
  2. Mini Phase 2: Collect Souls On The Left, With The Addition Of Fire Rocks Indicated By Red Circles On The Ground Beforehand.
  3. Mini Phase 3: Collect Souls On Both Sides, With A Further Addition Of Toxic Zombies That Can Insta-Kill If They Explode On You, And Gas That Impairs Vision Plus Limits Health To A Max Of 175 HP When In It.

The Boss Will Drop To The Ground After Mini Phase 3.

Phases 2, 3 And 4

Phases 2, 3 And 4 Are All Very Similar.

  • The First One Is Caltheris And Is Straightforward.
  • Then Caltheris Ascendent, Who's A Bit Faster But Again Manageable.
  • The Final Phase Is Caltheris The Needle. In Addition To Rocks, She'll Throw Long-Lasting, Heavily Damaging Aether Pools.
  • Before This Phase Starts, A Drop Called Veytharion's Blessing Can Be Found Upstairs. Pick It Up, Because It Gives You Infinite Reserve Ammo And Lethals, As Well As A One-Time Refill Of Tacticals.
  • Always Use Low Gravity And Create As Much Distance As Possible To Avoid Getting Slapped Or Slammed, Which Could Instantly Down You If You Don't Jump.
  • Continually Dodge The Different Rocks Throughout All Phases.
  • The Main Objective Is To First Destroy The Boss's Armor, Best Done With The Wonder Weapon.
