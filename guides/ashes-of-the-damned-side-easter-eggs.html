Ashes Of The Damned Side Easter Eggs Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-09

Free Ray Gun Mark II, Free Pack-A-Punch Crystals And Gold Tools, Mr. Peeks Loot And The Claw Talisman.

Free Ray Gun Mark II (Jump Pads)

  • Reward: A Ton Of Free Loot At Ashwood, Including A Free Ray Gun Mark II.
  1. Turn On All Three Generators And Build Pack-A-Punch On Ol' Tessie.
  2. Take Each Of The Seven Jump Pads On The Map At Least Once.
  3. Progress Three To Four Rounds. This Light At Janus Towers Plaza (Shown In The Video) Begins To Flash.
  4. Be Patient And Wait For A Fresh Sequence, Then Count The Number Of Times The Light Flashes In Each Set Of Flashes.
  5. Each Set Is Between One And Seven Flashes, For A Seven-Set Sequence.
  6. Each Number Ties To A Location's Jump Pad. The Jump Pad Map Is In The Free RG-MK2 Solver, And It Spits Out Your Exact Path If You're Confused.
  7. Drive Tessie To The First Location And Take The Jump Pads In The Order The Light Sequence Flashed.
  8. After Your Final Jump Pad, Launch Back To Ashwood, Regardless Of Where You Finished. A Purple Portal Spawns Somewhere In The Sky.
  9. Once You Locate It With Tessie, Note The Jump Pads The Portal Is Between And Go To One.
  10. If The Portal Is Off Center With The Arrow On The Jump Pad, Take That Jump Pad From The Opposite Side, Even If The Jump Pad Is Going In The Direction Of The Portal. Otherwise You'll Miss The Portal By An Inch And Have To Do Everything All Over Again.
  11. Do This Right And You'll Fly Through The Portal And Respawn At Ashwood Alongside The Loot.

Free Pack-A-Punch Crystals And Gold Tools (Toxic Pods)

  • Reward: Free Pack-A-Punch Crystals And Aether Tools, Up To A Tier 2 Crystal And A Gold Tool, As Early As Round 1. The Rewards Are Not Guaranteed.
  1. Get One Of The Four Fumigators Around The Map (Listed Below). Just One Is Enough.
  2. Drop The Toxic Growth Field Upgrade On Any Green Pod Around The Map. The Video Shows What They Look Like.
  3. Spray The Pod With The Fumigator.
  4. After About 10 Seconds It Gives You The Rewards.
  • Toxic Growth Is The Key Here. You Can Do It Without Toxic Growth, But The Rewards Are Miserable.
  • Repeatable At Every Pod On The Map, Every Round.
  • Do This In Spawn Right Away And You Can Usually Pull At Minimum A Tier 1 Pack-A-Punch Crystal And A Blue Tool With Ease.
  • Sometimes You Get A Tier 2 Crystal And A Gold Tool From A Single Pod.
  • Keep Repeating Until You Get Your Desired Result.
  • This Is An Extension Of Another Mini Easter Egg On The Map. The Version Without Toxic Growth Is In The Early Game Strategy Guide.

The Four Fumigators

  • Spawn: Shown In The Video.
  • Vandorn Farm: Next To The Power Switch.
  • Ashwood: Judgment Square, Across From The Arsenal Machine.
  • Blackwater Lake: In The Tool Shed.

Combat Axe Targets (Mr. Peeks Trophy)

  • Reward: Free Pack-A-Punch Crystals, Essence, Salvage, Weapons And More, As Early As Round 1.
  1. Head To Blackwater Lake And Interact With The Trophy Over To The Side In The Shed.
  2. A Purple Target Spawns In The Air, Down Off The Road (Shown In The Video).
  3. A Combat Axe Also Spawns On The Tree Trunk Right Outside Where You Got The Trophy. Take It.
  4. Throw The Combat Axes At The Glowing Purple Moving Targets.
  5. After Hitting 11 Of Them, Mr. Peeks Spawns In, Raves Out And Bursts Open With Rewards.
  • You Can Also Spawn In With Combat Axes Or Buy Them, But They're Given To You For Free Repeatedly Throughout This, So No Need To Stress.
  • I Believe You're Guaranteed Essence, And You Occasionally Get Pack-A-Punch Crystals, Weapons And Stuff Like That.
  • Same Easter Egg As The Tomahawk Target Challenge In The Early Game Strategy Guide.

Claw Talisman (Secret Boss Fight)

  • Reward: The Claw Talisman. Permanent Melee Double Points, Which Means Knifing Only For The Remainder Of The Game.
  1. Open Up The Map And Buy Yourself The Death Perception Perk.
  2. In The Foggy Areas Where You'd Typically Drive Tessie Through, You'll Eventually Spot A Glowing Orange Bear Footprint.
  3. Look In The Direction The Bear Print Is Facing. When You're Standing On It, You'll See The Next Orange Bear Print Show Up.
  4. Keep Following The Orange Bear Prints Until An HVT Boss Fight Begins.
  5. Kill The Zursa With Your Melee.
  6. Pick Up The Talisman.
  • It's A Very Challenging Boss Fight. You Need To Be Set Up Before You Do It.
  • There's Some Weaponry In The Area. I Highly Recommend Picking It Up.
