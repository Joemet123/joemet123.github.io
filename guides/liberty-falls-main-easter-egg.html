Liberty Falls Main Easter Egg Guide, All Steps

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-07

Every Step, Simply Put: Start The Quest, Build The Jet Gun For Free, Build The LTG Device, Charge Two Canisters, Set The Projectors And Win The Final Lockdown.

Before You Start

  • Some Steps Can Be Done In Tandem Once You Know The Map. For Clear Explanations, Here Is Everything Separate.
  • If A Step Fails, Retry Next Round.
  • Projector Colors: Projector & Straus Counter Solver.

Start The Quest

  1. Follow The Prompts On The Map To The Church Area, Where Pack-A-Punch Is.
  2. In The Pack-A-Punch Area, Interact With The Big Black Machine To Enable The Easter Egg Quest.

Jet Gun Wonder Weapon

  • You Need The Thrustodyne M23, Aka The Jet Gun. It Also Has Its Own Short Guide: Jet Gun Build Guide.
  • You Can Get It Out Of The Box, But Don't Waste Time Or Money Doing So. Build It For Free, Quickly.
  • Once The Next Step Is Done, You Can Get Rid Of It If You Wish.

Part 1: Handbrake

  1. Go To The Cemetery Next To The Church. Kill Some Zombies There And Wait For The Groundskeeper Zombie To Spawn In. And Yes, He Can Spawn On A Vermin Round.
  2. Kill Him And Pick Up His Key. Fail To Kill Him In Time And He Teleports Away, Then Returns A Couple Of Minutes Later In The Same Area.
  3. Use The Key To Open The Shed In The Groundskeeper's Yard. Grab The Handbrake Off The Table.

Part 2: Water Pressure Gauge

  1. On Washington Avenue, Next To The Plants, Pick Up The Water Valve From The Hose.
  2. In The Bowling Alley, Melee The Panel Next To The Bowling Lanes.
  3. Interact With The Water Gauge To Place The Valve, Then Hold Interact To Start A Mini Sequence.
  4. Keep Holding Interact. You Can Progress It A Little, Kill Some Zombies, Then Progress It Some More. Decoys, Teammates Or Smoke Grenades Help Protect You.
  5. Fail To Finish It In A Certain Amount Of Time And You Fail The Step. No Issue: Retry Next Round.
  6. Once Done, The Water Pressure Gauge Falls On The Floor. Pick It Up.

Part 3: Electrical Wires

  1. The Last Part Is In A Secret Room. Blast The Gate To The Building In The Riverside Area, Just Right Of Spawn, With The Mangler Cannon Scorestreak Or A Mangler's Direct Shot. The Hitbox Is Very Small, So Be Accurate.
  2. Inside, Interact With The Piles Of Junk On The Floor (Small Rewards). Dig Through All Of Them Until You Get The Electrical Wires.

Build It

  • Upstairs In The Motor Lodge, Hold Interact At Its Crafting Station. Done.

LTG Device

Three Parts, Any Order. Use The Jet Gun's Left Trigger Suck Ability To Pull Each One In.

  • Pack-A-Punch Area (Dark Aether): On The Railing Up High.
  • Washington Avenue: In The Window Above The Speed Cola Perk. Lean Right Over The Edge From The Roof, Or Jump On The White Truck In The Middle Of The Street And Suck It Up From There.
  • Olly's Comics: Go Behind The Counter, Look Up And Suck.
  • Build: The Crafting Station On The Roof Of The Bank, At The Back, In The Area Named The Alamo.

First Canister

  1. In The Dark Aether Church, Grab The Canister From The Machine That Started The Quest. The Hitbox Seems A Little Inconsistent, So Try A Different Angle.
  2. Take It To The Trap On Hill Street, Just Down The Path From The Cemetery. You Run Slower With It. Drop It (Button Shown At The Bottom Of Your Screen) Close To The Trap's Pulsator Pad.
  3. Place The LTG Device On The Pad At The Cemetery. A Mini Lockdown Starts: Defend A Rift.
  4. Too Many Zombies Enter The Rift And You Fail. Next Round, Interact With The LTG Device On The Floor Again.
  5. If Successful, A Red Mangler Or Abomination HVT Boss Spawns In.
  6. Shoot It How You Normally Would, Just More Slowly. The Goal Is To Make It Weaker, Not Kill It. It's Good To Go When It Starts Glowing Purple.
  7. Activate The Dark Aether Field Generator Trap And Train The Weakened Boss Through The Pad By The Canister. The Trap Kills It And Sucks The Soul Into The Canister.
  8. Pick Up The Canister (You'll Notice A Purple Glow Around Your Screen) And Insert It Into The Machine In The Dark Aether Church.

Projectors And The Straus Counter

  1. Pick Up The Straus Counter From The Left-Hand Side Of The Machine. It's A Tactical.
  2. Pick Your LTG Device Back Up From The Cemetery For Later.
  3. Find The Three Projectors: The Groundskeeper's Yard, The Patch Of Grass On The Hilltop Stairs, And Off The Side Of The Alamo When You Fall Onto The Yummy Freeze Rooftop.
  4. A Projector's Color Is The Light On Its Top And/Or Front.
  5. Use The Straus Counter In Front Of Each Projector. Whatever Color You See, Do The Opposite. The Goal Is Balance:
  • Red Screen: Turn The Projector Green.
  • Green Screen: Turn The Projector Red.
  • Yellow Screen: Keep The Projector Yellow. Yellow Is The Balance Point.
  • Many Guides Say To Match The Projector To The Screen Color. That Is Not Correct.

Second Canister And Final Lockdown

  1. Get Your Canister From The Device On East Main Street, In The Back Of Spawn. Repeat What You Did Earlier One More Time.
  2. Drop It Next To The Aether Field Generator Trap Pulsator Pad, About 5 Ft Away, Right There In Spawn.
  3. Place The LTG Device On The Pad In The Riverside Area, Just Right Of Spawn. Defend A Rift Again.
  4. If Successful, You Get Whichever Red HVT Boss (Mangler Or Abomination) You Didn't Get The First Time.
  5. Shoot It More Slowly Until It's Glowing Purple. Then Activate The Trap And Train It Through The Pad By The Canister.
  6. Pick Up The Charged Canister And Place It Into The Machine In The Dark Aether Church.
  7. When You're Prepared For The Final Lockdown, Technically This Map's Boss Fight, Interact With The Console On The Left Of The Machine. The Whole Team Must Approve On Screen.
  8. Kill Everything You See In Three Separate Waves. Each Wave Gives A Power-Up. I Believe The First Gives A Max Armor, The Second A Max Ammo.
  9. At The End, A Cutscene Plays With An Option To Quit Or Continue Playing.
  • Continue For Some Awesome Rewards: A Free Non-Permanent Perkaholic, Some Pack-A-Punch Crystals, Maybe Even A Wonder Weapon.
