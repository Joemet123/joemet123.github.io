MW3 Zombies Tips And Settings

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-11

Quick MW3 Zombies Tips, One Video Each.

Pro Tips

Moving Around

  • Slide Cancel: The Best Way To Move Around As Fast As Possible And Avoid All Enemies. Sprint, Slide, Then Hit The Jump Button. There Isn't Infinite Sliding Like In Black Ops 3 Zombies, But If You Properly Combine Sprint Timing With Sliding, You Can Work Out A Nice Rhythm To Move Much Faster. Repeat This Loop If You're Ever Forced To Run Around The Map.
  • Follow The Compass: If You Ping Something, You Don't Need To Keep Pulling Up Your Tac Map. Just Use The Marker On The Compass On The Top Of Your Screen. In A Place Like The Dark Aether This Is So Helpful, Because Tac Map Markers Quickly Disappear.
  • Blood Burner Bike: An Extremely Rare Vehicle With Incredible Health And Damage To Zombies Plus Bosses. It Drives Pretty Fast And Over Water With No Issue, And Doesn't Require Any Fuel. Only One Spawns Per Game, And From What I Hear, It Can Spawn Anywhere.

Essence And Loot

  • Double Points On Contracts: Have Double Points Active When Completing A Contract And You Get Double The Money For It. A Contract Paying 5,000 Essence Pays 10,000.
  • Purple Aetherium Chests: The Chests With Purple Aetherium Sticking Out Of Them Seem To Always Have Much Higher Quality Loot. Never Pass Up On Them.
  • Gold Weapon: You Can Get A Guaranteed Gold Weapon Every Single Game From The Red Zone. Useful When You're Out Of Aether Tools But Really Need To Do Something Challenging, Like The Worm Boss Fights.
  • Three-Plate Armor Vest: Always Guaranteed From A Mercenary Stronghold.
  • Free Perks: You Can Get All Nine Perks In Game For Free Via The Various Free Perk Easter Eggs. Cheat Sheet And Free Perks Video On The Urzikstan Page.
  • Death Perception: Lets You See Loot And Enemies Through Walls. Doing Something Very Meticulous, Like The Key Easter Egg In The Dark Aether? It Could Be Huge When Deciding Whether You Want To Waste Time Getting A Key For A Certain Door. If There's No Wonder Weapon Case, Why Bother?

Doghouses

  • Getting Meat: An Easy Way Is Kazimir Grenades. They Suck Up Everything Around Them And Drop Tons Of Meat. Explosive Weapons Like The Grenade Launcher Work Too.
  • How Much Meat: You Don't Need To Give The Doghouse Four Pieces Of Meat For A Dark Aether Or Tier 3 Dog. Three Pieces At Minimum Is Just Fine.
  • Despite The Minimums (One Piece Of Meat In The Gray Zone Doghouse, For Example), Four Pieces In Any Zone Gives The Dog More Health.

Bosses

  • Worm Boss: The Fights Adjust To The Amount Of Players You Have. Solo, It's Not As Hard To Kill The Boss, At Least In Terms Of Health, As In A Six-Player Game.
  • Mangler: Melee And/Or Shoot His Arm To Kill Him Much More Quickly.
  • Ammo Mods: They Can Actually Help You Kill Bosses Faster. Brain Rot: Bonus Damage To Mimics And Certain Versions Of The Abomination. Dead Wire: Disciples. Napalm Burst: Manglers. Cryo Freeze: Dogs.
  • With Brain Rot You Can Also Turn Dogs, Manglers, Disciples And Mimics, And Shatter Blast Helps Destroy Armor On All Types Of Enemies. Full List In Ammo Mods Explained.
  • Farm Kills At Exfil: Exfils Are Fantastic For Farming Mini Boss And Zombie Kills, Whether You Need Them For Missions, Dog Meat Or Just A Challenge.

Gear And Weapons

  • Share A Mystery Box Weapon: Knife A Weapon That's Yours Sitting In The Mystery Box And Anyone Else Can Pick It Up.
  • V-R11: Repairs The Health Of Your Vehicles, And Yes, This Includes Escort Vehicles.
  • Ammo Crates: They're Not Just For Ammo. You Can Repair The Health Of Your Gas Mask And Refill Your Grenades From Them. They're Always On A Pretty Generous 60-Second Timer, So Use Them As Much As Possible.
  • Rarity And Pack-A-Punch: Both Increase The Damage Output Of Your Weapon. A Chart Showing Roughly How Each One Scales The Damage Is In The Video. More In Gun Rarity And Pack-A-Punch.

Save Guns For Your Next Game

This Is Not A Glitch Or Cheat. It Uses The Elemental Pop Free Perk Easter Egg To Indirectly Carry Your Weapon Over: You Get The Upgrades Back As Items And Apply Them To The Same Gun Next Game.

  1. When You're Done With Your Game And About To Exfil, Make Your Way To The E4 Coordinate Of The Map. Be Careful: It's In The Red Zone And Extremely Difficult To Survive. Really Only Come With One Weapon So You Can Switch To Your Fists And Run Much Faster.
  2. Go Up To The Grave (Shown In The Video) And Interact To Pay Respects.
  3. It Strips Your Weapon Rarity, Ammo Mods And Pack-A-Punch.
  4. A Portal Spawns Where You Sacrificed Your Weapon. Inside You Get Something Of Equal Value Or Less Than The Weapon You Traded In, And A 30% Chance Of Elemental Pop.
  5. Stash The Items From The Portal Into Your Rucksack.
  6. Successfully Exfil. The Items Come With You To Your Next Game.
  7. Next Game, Select Your Insured Weapon If That Was The Gun You Took In And Buffed Up, Or The Weapon You Exfilled With From Your Contraband Stash.
  8. Apply All Of The Stuff You Got From That Portal.
  • Best Case: Sacrifice A Double Pack-A-Punch Purple Weapon With Brain Rot And You Can Get Back, At Max, A Purple Aether Tool, A Brain Rot Ammo Mod And A Gold Tier 2 Pack-A-Punch Aetherium Crystal.
  • No Floor: What You Get Back Is Random. Your Ceiling Is Your Current Weapon Loadout, And The Floor Could Literally Be Just A Green Aether Tool. I've Seen People Get Back Just A Can Of Elemental Pop, But That Was When They Traded In Less Rare Items.
  • Trade In Something You Spent A Lot Of Time To Get (A Double Or Triple Pack-A-Punch Weapon, A Purple Or Gold Weapon) And You'll Probably Get Something A Lot Better Than A Green Aether Tool.
  • This Cannot Be Done With Any Wonder Weapons.
  • What Carries Over: Rucksack Guide.

Keep Your Camo After Pack-A-Punch

  1. Before You Pack-A-Punch, Simply Melee The Pack-A-Punch Machine. It Turns Green.
  2. Then Pack-A-Punch Your Weapon, Or Use The Crystal You Want To Use.
  • You Keep Your Camo Or Mastercraft.
  • Yes, This Even Works With Pack-A-Punch Crystals.
  • A Feature, Not A Glitch.
  • You Must Do It Before Pack-A-Punch. If You Don't, You're Stuck With A Pack-A-Punch Camo.

Third Person Mode

  • Mouse And Keyboard: Hit The Escape Key Or The Pause Key And Hit Switch To First Or Third Person.
  • Controller: Simply Hold Down On The D-Pad.

Tombstone

Tombstone Lets You Keep The Items In Your Rucksack Even If You Die: Cans Of Juggernog, Speed Cola, Aetherium Crystals, Whatever It May Be.

  1. Die With Tombstone.
  2. When You Load Into Your Next Game, Check Your Tac Map And Even The Regular Gameplay Screen For A Tombstone Indicator.
  3. The Indicator For The Stone Is Exactly Where You Died In Your Previous Game.
  4. Go To The Stone And Claim Your Loot From The Previous Game's Rucksack.

Aether Blade

  • A Schematic. Throw It Like A Regular Grenade And It Throws Out A Shuriken, A Knife-Like Thing. Throw It At Zombies And It Clears Them Out.
  • You Recharge It So Quickly.
  • Tier 2 And Tier 3 Zombies Super Sprint Sometimes, And The Dogs Are Cracked Out, So Even Running With Your Fists Out You Can Easily Die. Don't Turn Around And Shoot: Turn Around And Throw The Aether Blade At Them.
