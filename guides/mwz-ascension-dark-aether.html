MW3 Zombies Ascension Dark Aether Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-11

Map And Contract Spots: Ascension Cheat Sheet.

Before You Start

  • The New Schematics Are The Grenade Bandolier And The Disciple Bottle. Plus How To Increase Your Stash Size From 20 To 30.
  • The Schematics Or Acquisitions From The Previous Act 4 Dark Aethers Are Needed. Not All Of Them, Only Certain Specific Ones: The Aether Blade, The Sergeant's Beret, And The Blood Burner Key If You Can't Find A Bike.
  • You Could Get Them From A Buddy. They're Good, So I'd Go Do Them Anyways.
  • Guides For Those Rifts: Union, Plus The Bad Signal And Countermeasures Videos.

Ascension Story Mission

  1. Select Act 4 Mission 4 Within The Zombies Main Menu.
  2. When In Game, Get A Good Setup Going, Then Enter The Ascension Portal At F5.
  3. Around The Area There Are Jump Pads, Unlocked By Shooting The Red Crystals. Use These Or The Scorcher To Get Around The Whole Map, And More Specifically To The Yellow Lights In The Sky.

First Obelisk

  1. At The First Yellow Light, Pick Up The Four Floating Relics Surrounding The Center Obelisk.
  2. Interact With The Center Obelisk.
  3. Kill The Purple Glowing Zombies Within Close Proximity To The Center Obelisk.

Second Obelisk

  1. Make Your Way To The Next Obelisk Marked On The Map, Again By Using The Scorcher Or Jump Pads.
  2. Same Process: Pick Up The Four Relics, Activate The Center Obelisk And Only Kill Purple Glowing Zombies Within Close Proximity To This Obelisk.
  3. This Time The Relics Fly All Over The Small Island And Need To Be Recollected. Use The Floating Circulating Trains, The Green Portal Directly Below You, Or Even The Scorcher To Collect Them Again.
  4. Collect Souls Again.

Train, Boat And Trucks

  1. Use The Special Jump Pads Or The Scorcher To Get To The Next Marked Area, Which Is On A Building.
  2. At This Building Is A Train. Jump On To Board It. It Takes You To An Island.
  3. On The Other Side Of That Island Is A Boat. Take It To The Marked Roof.
  4. Break The Crystal On The Back Of The Jump Pad On This Roof, Then Let The Game Take You Where You Need To Go.
  5. You'll Eventually Get To An Island You Have To Parkour. Break The Crystal At The Edge.
  6. Observe The Floating Trucks In The Background. Only Take That Jump Pad Once The Trucks Are Just About Right In Front Of You.
  7. It Shoots You Into The Truck. Spam Your Climb Button: X, A Or Space Bar, Depending On What You're On.
  8. Missing Isn't The End Of The World. Try Not To Fall All The Way Back Down: Redeploy And Land On Another Close Island If Possible.
  9. The Green Portal Shown In The Video Is A Lifesaver For Getting Back To The Top Of The Truck Island To Retry The Jump.
  10. Once On The Truck, Ride The Merry-Go-Round To The Next Island And Hop Off.
  11. On The Other Side Of The Island Is A Bus. Hop On, Be Patient, And Bus Hop All The Way To The Final Island.
  12. Take All The Blue Launch Pads. They Do All The Work For You.
  13. Run Down The Left Side Of The Ship They Bring You To, To The Marked Area. A Purple Portal Teleports You To The Ending Boss Area.

The Entity Boss Fight

  1. Jump From The Island You Spawn On And Immediately Redeploy, Flying Towards The Marked Island Where You'll See Ava. The Boss Fight Starts.
  2. First Phase: Launch Pad From Island To Island And Shoot All The Red Orbs On The Entity's Body. I Recommend You Go From Higher To Lower, As The Shoulders Seem To Bug Out At Certain Angles.
  3. If You Happen To Fall Off, It Won't Kill You. You'll Just Respawn.
  4. Once The Entity Is Defeated, Head To The Reward Rift And Interact. The Entity Respawns For A Final Phase.
  5. Final Phase: Avoid As Many Of The Attacks As You Can. It Seems None Of Them Are Particularly Lethal. They Could Damage You A Little, But They Probably Won't Kill You.
  6. Travel To The Marked Island Ava Is On And Collect Souls Within Close Proximity To Her.
  7. When She's Done, She Shocks The Entity, Making It Available For Damage. Repeat The Cycle Until The Entity Is Defeated.
  8. This Spawns A Reward Rift With The Mr. Peeks Relic Inside. Pick It Up, Then Exfil For The Cutscene.

Relics And Gold Upgrades

Just Like The Other Dark Aethers, You Have To Collect All Four Relics And Upgrade Them To Gold. Mr. Peeks Is The First Item, From The Story Mission. The Other Items Can Be Obtained In Any Order.

Purple Locked Diary

  1. Head To The Bridge At G6.
  2. Look In The Water For Runestones And Melee All Three To Activate Them. Make Sure You Don't Drown.
  3. The Triangle Glows Pink. Float In The Water Where Shown In The Video And The Triangle Forms Perfectly And Glows Green.
  4. This Spawns A Ton Of Mimics. You Need The Aether Blade For This Step, Through A Schematic Or Acquisition. Only Kill The Mimics With The Aether Blade.
  5. Keep Getting Aether Blade Kills Until You Get It (People Are Saying It's Five). You Get A Reward Rift With Your Purple Locked Diary.
  • This Step Is A Little Buggy: If Another Team Has Already Done It, It Doesn't Seem You Can Do It Too. Go See If The Runes Are There. If They're Not, Go Into A Fresh Game.

Gold Locked Diary

  1. Head To The Free Speed Cola Easter Egg Bird At D2.
  2. Climb Up The Middle Of The Bird Sculpture. You'll Find A Small Runestone Next To The Burnt Body.
  3. Interact With The Stone To Get PhD Flopper For Free. A Yellow Portal Spawns In Front Of The Bird. Enter It.
  4. Your Goal: Tac Sprint Into A Dolphin Dive Off The Edge Of The Sculpture And Land As Close To The Closed Blue Crate As You Can (Shown In The Video).
  5. If You Fail, Another Yellow Portal On The Bottom Of The Sculpture Brings You To The Top.
  6. When Done Properly, A Reward Rift Spawns With Your Gold Diary.
  • If You Don't Dolphin Dive Successfully, You Must Redeploy Or You'll Fall To Your Death. PhD Doesn't Break Fall Damage Unless You're Dolphin Diving.

Purple Giraffe Toy

  1. Activate The Sergeant's Beret, Another Schematic From A Previous Dark Aether.
  2. Sneak Up Behind A Mercenary And Do The Longer Melee Execution: Hold Down Your Melee Button.
  3. Killing The Mercenary Spawns A Portal With Your Purple Giraffe Toy.

Gold Giraffe Toy

  1. Crawl Into The Van At F4 In The Red Zone.
  2. Interact With The Stone To Spawn A Bunch Of HVT Bosses.
  3. Kill All The HVTs. If You Notice One Is Taking Zero Damage, Even In Its Weak Spots, Kill The Other Ones First. Then You Can Damage And Kill It.
  4. A Reward Rift Spawns With Your Gold Giraffe Toy.

Purple Drum

  1. Use A Blood Burner Bike. You Can Find One Randomly On The Map, But It's Almost Impossible. Or Use The Easy To Get Schematic For It, The Blood Burner Key.
  2. Drive It Quickly Over The Course At F4 In The Red Zone (Start Shown In The Video). Yes, You're Timed.
  3. There Are Arrows Guiding You. Follow The Arrows Or The Path You See On Your Screen. It Finishes At G6.
  4. Complete The Course On Time And A Reward Rift Spawns With Your Purple Drum. If Not, Try Again Faster.

Gold Drum

  1. Head To Shorok Opera House At F7 And Climb Up The Sculpture Outside To Find Your Final Relic Upgrade Rock.
  2. Interact With This Rock. It Starts A Challenging Sequence Of Zombies Spawning With A Mist Around Them, Representing Different Ammo Mods.
  3. Around The Area Are Glowing Pillars, Each Offering A Certain Ammo Mod. Look At A Pillar To See Its Effect.
  4. Pick Up Whichever Ammo Mod You Need And Kill The Matching Zombie With It. For Example, A Glowing Green Zombie Has To Be Killed With A Brain Rot Weapon.
  5. When Finished, A Reward Rift Spawns With Your Gold Drum.

Open The Rift

  1. Head To F5 And Look At The Symbols On Each Pedestal.
  2. Drop Each Item Into Its Correct Position. If It's Not Working, Interact With The Pedestal First, Then Hit The Drop Item Button. It Doesn't Seem To Be The Usual Interact Button This Time.
  3. This Spawns Your New Dark Aether Rift. Kill The Spawned Boss And Get A Guaranteed Sigil.
  4. Insert The Sigil Into The Front End Of The Rift And Off You Go.

Sigils And Elder Sigils

  • Sigil: 30 Minutes. One-Time Use Acquisitions And Zero Schematics. Earned Via Red Zone Contracts.
  • Elder Sigil: 15 Minutes, And An Increase In The Amount Of Zombies. Craftable Schematics And One-Time Use Acquisitions. Only Earned Via Dark Aether Contracts.
  • The Schematics In The Elder Sigil Dark Aether Are Random, Just Like The Previous Dark Aether. You Can Do One Run And Get Them All, Or Ten Runs And Get None Of Them.

Dark Aether Contracts

  • Use The Scorcher, The Jump Pads, Redeploy Rifts And/Or The Floating Train System Going Around The Map. I Recommend Spawning With The Scorcher.
  • The Contract Bunnies Are Marked In The Sky. In The Elder Sigil Dark Aether They Are Not Marked, So You Need To Know Their Specific Locations.
  • When You Complete All The Contracts, Maybe Fail One, Or Decide You're Done With The Dark Aether For Now, Exfil To Save Your Stuff.

Complete Them In Any Order:

  • PND Outlast: G2
  • Escort: E5
  • Spore Control: D2

Stash Increase

  • It's Not A Schematic. It's A Permanent Upgrade You Only Need To Pick Up Once.
  • Once You Get It, Your Stash Increases From 20 To 30.
