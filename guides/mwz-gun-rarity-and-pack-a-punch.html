MW3 Zombies Gun Rarity & Pack-A-Punch

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-11

Two Ways To Upgrade Your Gun: The Weapon Level Colors (Rarity) And The Pack-A-Punch Tiers.

Rarity Colors

Five Different Weapon Level Rarities, From Lowest To Highest:

  • Gray: Your Base Level.
  • Green: Uncommon.
  • Blue: Rare.
  • Purple: Epic.
  • Gold: The Highest.
  • With Each One, Your Weapon Increases In Damage. That's Basically All The Weapon Level Rarity Does.
  • Your Weapon Level Color Shows In The Lower Right Hand Corner.
  • Wonder Weapons, Like The Ray Gun And The Scorcher, Have A Sixth Color Just For Them.

Raise Rarity With Aether Tools

  • All You Have To Do Is Find An Aether Tool. Do Contracts, Complete Objectives, Just Kill Zombies, Go To Different Threat Zones, And You'll Eventually Come Across One.
  • The Tool's Color Is What Your Weapon Becomes. A Rare (Blue) Aether Tool Turns A Gray Weapon Blue.
  • Pick It Up And Equip It, And Your Weapon Changes Color.
  • Exfil With One To Keep It In Your Acquisitions, Or Craft One From A Schematic.

Pack-A-Punch Tiers

There's Three Threat Zones, And In Each One Is A Tier Of Pack-A-Punch:

  • Low Threat Zone: Tier 1 Pack-A-Punch.
  • Medium Threat Zone: Tier 2.
  • High Threat Zone: Tier 3, Your Max Damage Output.
  • There's A Different Pack-A-Punch Machine In Each Threat Zone.
  • Pack-A-Punch Makes The Weapon More Powerful. You May Gain Some Bonus Effects Too, Like A Faster Rate Of Fire In Certain Weapons, Or The Weapon Becomes Automatic.

Aetherium Crystals

  • Crystals Pack-A-Punch The Weapon The Same Way The Aether Tool Upgrades Its Color. They Take The Pack-A-Punch Level From 0 To 1, Or To 2, Or To 3.
  • Do Certain Contracts, Be In Certain Threat Zones, Kill Zombies, Kill AI, And You'll Eventually Come Across Crystals.
  • Exfil With Them Successfully And You Can Stash Them In Your Acquisitions.
  • Find The Schematic For Certain Crystal Levels And You Can Build It And Spawn Into The Map With It.
  • A Refined Aetherium Crystal Pack-A-Punches Your Gun To Level 2.

Max Damage

  • For The Maximum Possible Damage Output, You Need A Tier 3 Gold Weapon.
  • There's Nothing Else You Can Do To Make The Weapon More Powerful. If You Don't Like The Effectiveness, You've Got To Get A New Weapon.
  • Schematics For A Gold Legendary Aether Tool And A Tier 3 Flawless Pack-A-Punch Crystal: USB Red Worm Easter Egg.

Spawn In Upgraded

  • Put An Aether Tool From Your Acquisitions In Your Rucksack, And When You Spawn In You Can Use It Right Away To Upgrade Your Weapon Rarity.
  • Have The Schematic For A Purple Aether Tool And For A Pack-A-Punch Crystal? You Can Spawn In With A Tier 2 Purple Weapon Pretty Much Within 2 Seconds Of Being In The Game.
  • There's Cooldowns, Of Course. How They Work: Acquisitions And Schematics.
