Citadelle Des Morts Elemental Swords Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-09

Your First Step To Any Of The Swords Is The Base Sword. Each Sword Then Has Its Own Upgrade.

Base Sword

  1. Open Pack-A-Punch By Following All Of The Basic On-Screen Prompts.
  2. Get To At Least Round 10. At That Point The Knight Zombies Begin To Spawn.
  3. Kill A Knight Zombie To Get A Stamp.
  4. Make Your Way To The Dining Hall, Where Vulture Aid Is. In The Bottom Portion Of The Room Are Four Knights, Lined Up Side By Side And Across From Each Other.
  5. Look At The Chest Of Each Knight And Decide Which Sword You Want To Go For.
  6. Interact With It. The Sword Pops Into The Knight's Hand. Take It.
  • The Base Sword You Pick Up Only Works For That Specific Sword's Upgrade Path. You Can't Grab The Base Sword For The Fire Sword And Then Use It To Try The Raven Sword Upgrade, For Example.
  • Despite The Same Exact Performance, Look And Name, The Base Sword You Pick Up Is Tied To Your Specific Sword Quest.
  • Whether Solo Or Co-Op, All Swords Can Be Completed In A Single Match: Either One At A Time Or One Per Person.
  • At Any Time, Swords Can Be Swapped By Placing Them Back Down On Their Respective Pedestal In The Knight Room.
  • The Sword Replaces Your Standard Knife In The Dedicated Melee Slot Rather Than Consuming A Weapon Slot.
  • To Pull Out Your Sword, Simply Hold Down Your Melee Button.

Void Sword (Balmung, Raven's Shadow)

  1. Kill A Knight Zombie To Collect The Stamp. Place It At The Knight Statue With The Raven On Its Shoulder In The Dining Hall. This Gets You The Base Sword.
  2. Head To The Alchemical Lab, Located Directly Next To The Sword Room.
  3. Check The Five Possible Locations In This Room, Listed Below, For A Very Specific Bone Item. Each One Has A Little Label Underneath It Telling You Exactly What It Is.
  4. Find, Note And Pick Up Your Bone. It Can Vaguely Be Viewed In The HUD After Being Picked Up, But It's Almost Impossible To Tell What It Is From There.
  5. Head To The Tavern Cellar. Place Your Bone Down On The Center Of The Dial, Then Place Your Sword Into The Bottom Portion.
  6. Enter The Code For Your Bone: Raven Sword Bone Code Solver. Each Bone Is Tied Directly To A Unique Combination That Is The Same Every Single Time.
  7. Lock In Symbols From The Bottom, Not The Top. The Green Cursor On The Bottom Of The Dial Shows The Spot.
  8. Upon A Successful Code, The Dial Spins And A Pink Orb Spawns.
  9. Follow The Pink Orb To Specific Locations In The Tavern. Where It Stops, It Puts Holes In The Floor.
  10. Purple Orbs With Symbols On Them Begin To Spawn. Guide Them To The Void Holes In The Floor Just By Standing Right Next To Or On Top Of The Hole.
  11. This Has To Be Done In Three Different Locations In The Tavern.
  12. Follow The Pink Orb Around Until It Leads You Back Downstairs. Grab Your Upgraded Sword.

The Five Bones (Each Time The Same Bone Spawns In The Same Location)

  • Corvus Corone: A Double Raven Head. On The Bookshelf.
  • Pantheo Leo: Lion Jaw. Right Next To It, On The Same Bookshelf.
  • Oxudercinae: Some Kind Of Goby Fish. On The Shelf On The Other Side Of The Wall Opening.
  • Pandinus Imperator: A Scorpion. On The Table Across From The Fish.
  • Ovis Blanford: A Sheep Horn. On The Right Hand Side Of The Wall Leading Out Towards The Sword Room.

Fire Sword (Caliburn, Dragon's Fire)

  1. Kill A Knight Zombie To Collect A Stamp. Place It At The Knight Statue With The Dragon On Its Chest In The Dining Hall. This Gets You The Base Sword.
  2. Take It Around The Corner To The Dining Room, Right Upstairs From Juggernog, And Place It Into The Dragon Pedestal.
  3. Interact With A Fire Pit And Transport Its Fireball To The Sword Pedestal.
  4. Do This For All Three Fire Pits Around The Map.
  5. Once You Deliver All Three Fireballs, You'll See A Mini Animation. Pick Up Your Upgraded Sword.
  • While You Have The Fire On You, It Depletes Your Health. To Counter That, Melee Kill Zombies To Regain Your Health.

The Three Fire Pits

  • Village Ramparts: Next To The Cannon.
  • Town Square: In A Corner Of Spawn.
  • Town Square: Right Outside The Left Side Of The Spawn Door.

Light Sword (Solais, Lion Of Light)

  1. Kill A Knight Zombie To Collect A Stamp. Place It At The Knight Statue With The Lion On Its Chest In The Dining Hall. This Gets You The Base Sword.
  2. Yellow Parasites Now Begin To Spawn With Your Regular Rounds. Melee Kill One Of These Special Yellow Glowing Parasites With The Sword To Charge It And Make It Glow Yellow.
  3. Use It To Melee The Door In The Upper Village (Shown In The Video). It Ignites A Symbol On The Door, Removing The Charge.
  4. Do It Four Times, A Parasite And Then The Door Each Time, To Reveal The Four Symbols.
  5. Interact With The Door To Enter A Secret Room.
  6. At The End Of The Room, Place Down The Sword Into The Pedestal.
  7. Around The Base Of The Pedestal Is A Symbol. Around The Room Are A Bunch Of Floating, Moving Symbols. Shoot The One You See On The Base Of The Pedestal.
  8. Shoot It A Few Times With A Bullet Weapon And Have It Suck Into The Sword.
  9. Just Keep Matching The Symbol On The Pedestal. The Order Is The Same Every Game: Circle With A Dot, Snowflake, Triangle, Then Circle With A Line. See The Light Sword Symbols.
  10. When Done, Your Upgraded Sword Is Ready For Pickup.

Lightning Sword (Durendal, Stag Of The Storms)

  1. Kill A Knight Zombie To Collect A Stamp. Place It At The Knight Statue With The Stag On Its Chest In The Dining Hall. This Gets You The Base Sword.
  2. Collect The Three Lightning Rods, Listed Below.
  3. Place All Three Rods Down Into The Vase At The Hillside Ramparts, Which Is The Top Outside Floor Of The Castle. A Storm Spawns.
  4. Wait With Your Sword Out. Every Few Seconds It Gets Electrically Charged.
  5. Place The Charge Down Into The Vase By Meleeing It.
  6. Do It Three Separate Times.
  7. After The Three Electrical Charges Are Placed, Place Down The Sword To Retrieve The Fully Upgraded Version.

The Three Lightning Rods

  • Oubliette Room: With The Base Sword, Go Down To The Room Where Quick Revive Is To Get A Special Zombie With A Lightning Rod In It To Spawn. Find And Kill This Zombie.
  • Hilltop: Laying Right Next To The Crates By Deadshot Daiquiri.
  • Upper Village: Shoot The Electrical Box By Speed Cola With A Dead Wire Weapon.
