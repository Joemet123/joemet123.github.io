Astra Malorum Side Easter Eggs Guide

By Joemet123 · 2026-11-09

Ten Side Easter Eggs, Most Useful First. Separate Pages: LGM-1 Wonder Weapon And Free Power-Ups.

3 Free Perks: Gramophone Records

  • Reward: 3 Free Perks, Guaranteed, One From Each Gramophone.

Museum Infinitum: Melee Kills

  1. Head To Museum Infinitum, Where Speed Cola Is. You'll See A Mini Record Up High, Shown In The Video.
  2. Run Up To The Wall And Then Wall Jump Forward On It. When In The Air, Hold Interact To Pick Up The Record.
  3. Place The Record On The Gramophone Right Across From Speed Cola, In The Same Room.
  4. You Have From The Point The Music Starts Until It Ends, About 30 Seconds, To Get Melee Kills Near This Gramophone To Give It Souls.
  5. If You Give It Enough, Which Is 15 Plus Kills, I Believe, You Get A Free Perk.

Machina Astralis: Equipment Kills

  1. Go To Machina Astralis. Find A Record Inside The Wooden Crate Shown In The Video.
  2. Insert The Record Into The Gramophone Located In The Next Room, At The Archive Of Orbis.
  3. While The Music Plays, This Time Get Equipment Kills To Give It Souls.
  4. If You Get Enough, You'll Get Another Free Perk When The Music Ends.

The Luminarium: Field Upgrade Kills

  1. Jump To Pick Up The Record At The Top Of The Bookshelf.
  2. Place It On The Gramophone In The Corner Shown In The Video.
  3. While The Music Plays, Get Kills Using A Field Upgrade Weapon Like Mr. Peeks Or Dark Flare. Anything Else Is Annoying Or Just Doesn't Work.
  4. You'll Receive A Free Perk When The Music Ends If You Get Enough Kills.
  • At Any Point, If You Don't Get Enough Kills But Do Get Some, You Should Get Some Salvage.
  • You Can Repeat This Every Single Round If You Fail To Get The Perk, For Another Attempt.

Free Random Perk: Purple Skulls

  • Reward: A Random Perk For Everyone In Your Team.

Collect The 5 Skulls

You Need To Shoot 5 Skulls Around The Map, But First Pack-A-Punch Your Weapon, As It Is Required To Activate Each Skull. Once Active, You Can Pick Up The Skull For Later Use.

  • Skull 1: Museum Infinitum, In The Showcase.
  • Skull 2: Machina Astralis, Right Across From The Arsenal Machine.
  • Skull 3: Further Along The Way, On The Veilwalk, In The Bricks Shown In The Video.
  • Skull 4: Go To And Through The Pack-A-Punch Area. On The Floor In The Zombie Barrier In Stargazer's Courtyard.
  • Skull 5: The Final One, Wedged Down In The Spot Shown In The Video, Outside Going From The Luminarium To Spawn.

Place And Shoot

  1. Head Back To The Machina Astralis Area And Place Down Your Skulls One At A Time At The Table Shown In The Video.
  2. They Will Glow And Raise In The Order You Need To Shoot Them Back, So Note The Order Down Somehow. The Purple Skulls Solver Makes It Even Easier.
  3. Shoot The Skulls In The Order They Glowed And Raised.
  • If You Fail, Try Again Once Per Round Until Completed.

Free Cryofreeze Ammo Mod: Ghost Children

  • Reward: A Free Cryofreeze Ammo Mod, Weapon, Scorestreak, Salvage And Equipment.
  1. Wait For A Ravager (Max Ammo) Round, Then Interact With The Empty Display Case Next To The Barrier In Museum Infinitum, Shown In The Video. You'll Hear A Definitive Screech Come From It.
  2. On The Next Round, Inside The Museum, Any Other Display Case Will Loudly Make A Static Screech Noise. Interact To Make It Go Away, And You'll Hear A Noise Indicating You've Hit The Right One.
  3. Pass To The Next Round, Then Repeat The Process Of Listening And Then Interacting With The Rumbling Display Case.
  4. Once Done, Pass Another Round And Do It Again One Last Time.
  5. On The Round Following Your Fourth Correct Interaction With The Display Cases, Your Dancing Ghost Children Will Be Somewhere Around The Map.
  6. Run Up To The Kids Once You Find Them, And They'll Evaporate, Revealing Your Rewards.
  • You Do Not Need Power On For This.
  • Hitting The Wrong Display Case Does Not Fail The Step, So You Could Just Spam Them All If You Want.

Ghost Children Locations

5 Locations I Got Them To Spawn In My Game, Plus An Extra I've Seen. There Are Probably More.

  • Inside The Luminarium Trap Room.
  • Outside The Luminarium, Towards Abyssal Rim.
  • Inside The Observatory Dome, In The Corner.
  • Outside The Observatory Dome, In The Veilwalk.
  • Machina Astralis, Upper Floor.
  • Right Next To The Wisp Tea Perk Machine.

Free Gold Shotgun: Nikolai Easter Egg

  • Reward: A Guaranteed Gold Shotgun, As Early As Round 1. You Also Get Your 3 Molotovs Back.
  1. Spawn Into The Game As Nikolai.
  2. Make Your Way To The Abyssal Rim And Throw A Molotov At The Location Shown In The Video.
  3. Follow The Orange Footsteps To The Next Location, On Steps, Where You'll Have To Throw Another Molotov.
  4. Follow The Footsteps Again, Then Throw Another Molotov In The Direction They Lead.
  5. Follow The Footsteps One More Time To The Cave Opening. Right Next To It Will Be A Rock With A Little Toy.
  6. Interact With The Toy To Enter A Secret Area.
  7. Run Around The Room Back And Forth For A Bunch Of Mini Events To Play.
  8. At The End, Just Survive The Ravagers.
  9. You'll Spawn At The Cave's Entrance With Your Reward.
  • If Multiple People Are Nikolai, They Can All Do It For The Reward As Well. No Issues At All.

Wunderwaffe DG-2 Turret For Ol' Tessie

  • Reward: The Wunderwaffe DG-2 Turret On Ol' Tessie. You Can Use It Just Like You Did In Ashes Of The Damned.
  1. Make Your Way To The Abyssal Rim Area Of The Map, Next To Wisp Tea. You'll See The Rock Shown In The Video.
  2. Either Use A Frag Grenade That's Charged Perfectly Or Throw A Semtex Directly On It. Upon Detonation, The Rock Will Split Into Two.
  3. One Piece Of The Rock Will Sit Literally Right In Front Of You, Just Off The Edge Of The Map. Jump Off The Map Onto The Rock With A Little Wall Jump.
  4. Within One Jump, You'll Just Start Floating Away.
  5. You'll Float Off Into The Distance Until You Eventually Run Into The Turret. Hold Interact To Pick It Up.
  6. Enjoy The Ride For About 30 Seconds Or So, And Then You'll Be Back In Spawn.
  7. Build The Turret On Ol' Tessie By Just Holding Interact.
  • If You Fail This, You Will Fall Off But Respawn With 50 HP Back In The Main Map.

Insta-Kill Wisps: Lamps

  • Reward: Yellow Wisps Every Round. They're Powerful And Can Insta-Kill Multiple Enemies In One Strike.
  1. There Are 9 Special Lamps Around The Walls In The Outside Areas Of The Map. Check These 9 Lamps For One With A Glowing White Wisp Attached.
  2. Once The Wisp Is Located, Aim Down Sights On Its Lamp For 3 Seconds. It Will Then Fly Towards And Rotate Around Your Character.
  3. Place The Wisp Back Down At A New Unlit Lamp By Hitting Your Melee Button While Looking At It. The Light Inside Will Turn On.
  4. Retrieve It Back From The New Lamp The Same Way You Got It From The First: Aim Down Sights Again For 3 Seconds.
  5. Do This At All 9, But One At A Time.
  6. Once Done With All 9 Locations, Flip To A New Round.
  7. At The Start Of This And Every Round From Here On Out, You Can Retrieve A Yellow Wisp From All Your Lamps By Aiming Down Sights On Each. You Can Hold Up To 5 Total At A Time.
  8. Hit Your Melee Button At Any Point To Disperse A Wisp Towards What You Want.

Lamp Locations

  • 2 Outside The Luminarium, Going Towards Wisp Tea.
  • 3 Going From Another Side Of The Luminarium Towards Spawn.
  • 2 In Spawn.
  • 2 Final Lamps: Run Through Museum Infinitum, Going Towards Machina Astralis.

Slow Time: Clocks

  • Reward: Time Slows Down For 60 Seconds. It Does Seem To Slow Every Enemy In The Map, Making Stuff Easier.
  1. Melee Each Clock Around The Map, Hearing A Ding After Each One.
  2. You Are Limited In The Amount Of Time You Have To Hit Each Clock.
  3. After Meleeing The Final One, The Clock Will Pulsate And Time Slows Down.
  • Useful For The UFO Step Of The Main Quest, Before Entering Mars.

Clock Locations

  • Museum Infinitum, Against The Wall Shown In The Video.
  • The Luminarium, Right Next To The Bookshelf Shown In The Video.
  • Archive Of Orbis, Next To Stamin-Up.
  • Machina Astralis, Up In The Corner Shown In The Video.
  • Machina Astralis, On The Ground Near The Teleporter.

Friendly Ravager: Bongo

  • Reward: A Free Friendly Ravager Named Bongo. To Be Real, This Is Useless: He Doesn't Revive You, Has Low Health And Doesn't Really Kill Any Zombies.
  1. Turn On Power And Pack-A-Punch As Guided By The Game.
  2. Locate And Kill Oscar The Robot For A Shiny Trinket. It'll Be In Your Tactical Grenade Slot.
  3. Search In Every Barrier On The Map For A Hiding Green-Eyed Ravager. He'll Appear On Your Minimap As A White Dot.
  4. Go Around The Map Looking On The Outskirts Of The Minimap For That White Dot. When You Find It, Try To Connect It To One Of The Barriers With The Ravager.
  5. Once You Locate Him, Throw Your Trinket For Him To Consume It And Then Disappear.
  6. Repeat This Process One Time On Each Of The Next Two Rounds, For 3 Total Ravagers.
  7. After The Third Time, He'll Jump Out And Be Named Bongo.
  • In The Video The Trinkets Refill With Max Ammo, So You Could Refill Them Like That. Otherwise You'll Need 3 Total Oscars Killed, To Get A Shiny Trinket From Each.
  • Oscar Notes Are In The LGM-1 Guide.

Telescope Jumpscare

  • Reward: A Jumpscare For Your Friends. Small, But Could Be Funny.
  1. Turn On Power And Pack-A-Punch As Guided By The Game.
  2. Shoot 5 Of The Steaming Valves Around The Room. There's A Lot Of Them, But Only 5 Will Be Steaming, And Only One At A Time. They Appear As Soon As Power's On.
  3. A Telescope Will Move Into Position, And You'll Be Able To Interact With The Left Side Of The Piano To View The Telescope.
  4. Tell Your Friend To Search For And Find The Skull And Hover Over It. Maybe Tell Them It'll Give 2,000 Points For Free, Which It Does Not.
  5. Upon Hovering On The Skull For A Couple Of Seconds: Jumpscare.
  • The Same Valves Are Used Later On In An Easter Egg Step, The LGM-1 Unlock, So Nothing Should Be Suspected.

Music Easter Egg: 3 Mr. Peeks Headphones

  • Reward: An Avenged Sevenfold Song Plays.

Interact With 3 Mr. Peeks Headphones Around The Map.

  1. The Luminarium: The Juggernog Area. The First Pair Is Around The Bend, On The Bottom Of The Shelf Cabinet.
  2. Machina Astralis: Go Out And Over To The Machina Astralis Building. You'll Find Another Pair, Shown In The Video.
  3. Mars: The Third And Final Pair. The Spot Is Shown In The Video.
  • To Access Mars, You Need To Do A Vast Majority Of The Main Easter Egg.
