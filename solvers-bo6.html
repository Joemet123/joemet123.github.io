MKII Chalkboard

Solver

Pick Your Chalkboard Letters And It Lists All Four Possible Codes, One Per Printed Word.

How To Run The Step
  1. Inspect Your Chalkboard In-Game And Note The Letters On Its Bottom Left.
  2. On Round 10+, Kill The Lab Technician Zombie Downstairs And Take The Floppy Disk.
  3. Insert The Disk Into The East Foyer Computer Near The Library.
  4. After About 30 Seconds It Prints A Page Naming One Of Four Words: Moth, Worm, Yeti Or Crab.
  5. Look That Word Up Against Your Chalkboard Letters. That Number Is Your Code.
  6. Punch It In Downstairs In The Service Tunnel, Outside The SAM Room. It Frees The Doppleghast.

Gorgofex Solver

Solver

Two Letters From Two Monitors Make An Element. Its Atomic Number Is Your Door Code (Ru = Ruthenium = 44).

  • Deadshot Monitor: The First Letter Of Its Word Is Letter 1 Of Your Element.
  • Periodic Room Monitor: The First Letter Of Its Word Is Letter 2.
  • Sometimes Only One Monitor Shows A Word. That Is Normal.
Periodic Table Guide

Teleporter Secret Room Code

Solver

Pick The Four Folder Items You Found And It Puts Them In Code Order.

  • Three Folders Are In The Connected T1 Executive Suites And T1 Directors Office.
  • The Fourth Is In The Secret T2 Teleportation Lab You Unlocked With The Fowler Mangler.
  • Each Folder Has An Item Pictured On The Top. Interact With It To See Which.
