Nexus Pillar Handle Alignment

Solver

Three Geared Handles That Must All Point At The Same Temple. Turning One Moves The Other Two By Double.

This Is The One That Genuinely Needs Solving. The Handles Are Geared — Turning One Moves That Ring By One Position And The Other Two By Two Each, So You Cannot Count Each Ring's Gap On Its Own.

How To Read Your Handles And Lever
  • Handles Are Named By Distance From Pack-A-Punch. Inner Is Closest To PaP, Outer Is Furthest.
  • The Ring Has Six Spots: Four Temples Plus The House And One Empty Gap. The Gap Is A Real Position — Pick None Of The Above If A Handle Sits On It.
  • Spin Direction Comes From A Lever Under Pack-A-Punch. Horizontal (Not Pointing At The Stairs And Perk Machine) Is Counter-Clockwise. Vertical (Pointing At Them) Is Clockwise.
  • The Perk Machine There Starts As Deadshot And Later Becomes Wonderfizz, So It Is Named Generically.

Veytharion Block Puzzle

Reference

A Classic River Crossing. Four Blocks, One Buffer, Seven Trips. The Answer Is The Same Every Game.

Four Stone Blocks — WATER, FIRE, PLANT And HAND — Start In The Left Wall And All Four Have To Reach The Right.

Do This, Every Time

  1. Load FIRE First, Then HAND → Send RIGHT → Drop HAND First, Then FIRE.
  2. Load HAND On Its Own → Send LEFT. Nothing Else Moves.
  3. Load WATER First, Then HAND → Send RIGHT → Drop HAND First, Then WATER.
  4. Load FIRE First, Then HAND → Send LEFT → Drop HAND First, Then FIRE.
  5. Load PLANT First, Then HAND → Send RIGHT → Drop HAND First, Then PLANT.
  6. Load HAND On Its Own → Send LEFT. Nothing Else Moves.
  7. Load FIRE First, Then HAND → Send RIGHT → Drop HAND First, Then FIRE.

Finished Right Wall: WATER · PLANT · HAND · FIRE

The Rules And Your Starting Layout
  • FIRE Can Never Sit With WATER Or PLANT — Not On A Side, And Not Next To It In A Wall. HAND Cancels That, So HAND Is Your Buffer.
  • Nothing Moves Unless HAND Is In The Transporter With It. Put HAND On The Middle Bridge, Then Hit The Dial.
  • HAND Leaves Last And Lands First. Load The Other Block First, Then HAND; On Arrival Drop HAND First. That Keeps FIRE Buffered In Transit.

Start — Slot All Four Into The LEFT Wall

  • WATER · PLANT · HAND · FIRE
  • PLANT · WATER · HAND · FIRE
  • FIRE · HAND · WATER · PLANT
  • FIRE · HAND · PLANT · WATER

Any Of Those Four Works. FIRE Must Sit On An End With HAND Next To It — The Other Twenty Orders Fail The Step And Cost You A Round.

One Correction Worth Knowing: Some Written Guides Say To Bring FIRE And PLANT Back Together On The Middle Trip. That Strands FIRE With PLANT And WATER And Fails The Step. It Has To Be FIRE + HAND.

Scrolls Solver

Solver

A Three-By-Three Grid Where Hitting One Scroll Flips Its Neighbours Too. Match The Grid To Your Screen And It Returns The Shortest Set Of Hits.

Each Scroll Is Either OUT (Facing Outwards, Active) Or IN (Facing Inwards, Inactive). You Need Every Scroll Facing IN. Hitting One Flips It And The Scrolls Above, Below, Left And Right Of It, Which Is Why Brute-Forcing It By Hand Rarely Lands. The Answer Updates As You Tap.
