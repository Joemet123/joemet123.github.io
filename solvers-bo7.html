Veytharion Block Puzzle

Reference

Starting Orders That Work (Left Wall, Top To Bottom)

  1. WATER · PLANT · HAND · FIRE
  2. PLANT · WATER · HAND · FIRE
  3. FIRE · HAND · WATER · PLANT
  4. FIRE · HAND · PLANT · WATER

Then Send The Blocks Like This

  1. FIRE + HAND → Right
  2. HAND Alone → Left
  3. WATER + HAND → Right
  4. FIRE + HAND → Left
  5. PLANT + HAND → Right
  6. HAND Alone → Left
  7. FIRE + HAND → Right
  • Every Trip: Load The Other Block First, Then HAND. On Arrival, Drop HAND First.
  • FIRE Can Never Sit With WATER Or PLANT Unless HAND Is Between Them.
  • Nothing Moves Without HAND. Put It On The Middle Bridge, Then Hit The Dial.

Nexus Pillar Handle Alignment

Solver

Three Geared Handles Must All Point At The Same Temple. Enter Where Yours Sit.

How To Read Your Handles And Lever
  • Handles Are Named By Distance From Pack-A-Punch. Inner Is Closest To PaP, Outer Is Furthest.
  • The Ring Has Six Spots: Four Temples Plus The House And One Empty Gap. The Gap Is A Real Position. Pick None Of The Above If A Handle Sits On It.
  • Spin Direction Comes From A Lever Under Pack-A-Punch. Horizontal (Not Pointing At The Stairs And Perk Machine) Is Counter-Clockwise. Vertical (Pointing At Them) Is Clockwise.
  • The Perk Machine There Starts As Deadshot And Later Becomes Wonderfizz, So It Is Named Generically.

Free RG-MK2

Solver
  1. Turn On All 3 Generators And Build Pack-A-Punch On Tessie.
  2. Take All 7 Jump Pads At Least Once.
  3. Play 3–4 Rounds Until The Light At Janus Towers Plaza Flashes.
  4. Wait For A Fresh Sequence. Count The Flashes In Each Set (1–7). There Are 7 Sets.
  5. Each Number Is A Pad On The Map. Tap Them Below In Order To Get Your Route.
  6. Drive Tessie To The First Pad And Take The Pads In That Order.
  7. After The Last Pad, Launch Back To Ashwood. A Purple Portal Spawns In The Sky.
  8. Take The Pad That Lines Up With The Portal. If The Portal Sits Off The Pad's Arrow, Use That Pad From The Opposite Side.
  9. Fly Through The Portal: You Land At Ashwood With Free Loot And A Ray Gun Mark II.
