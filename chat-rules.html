The Six Rules

01I Love You, But I'm Not Your Therapist. If You're Going Through Something Heavy, I'm Not The Person To Unpack That With — But The Stream Might Be A Good Escape For A Few Hours.
02No Negativity. Keep It Chill.
03No Political Talk. We're Here For Zombies, Not Debates.
04Don't Self-Promote — Even If You've Been Here Since Day One. I Want The Best For You, But Talking About Your Stream Or Plans To Stream In My Chat Isn't The Move. Hard Work Pays Off — Do Your Thing On Your Own Time.
05Be Respectful Of Other People And Their Opinions. You Don't Have To Agree, But You Do Have To Be Decent.
06Watch Your Mouth As Much As You Can. We're All Human, But Keep It Reasonable.

⚠️ Break Any Of These And You're Getting Timed Out Or Banned. I'm A Nice Guy, But Don't Test It.
