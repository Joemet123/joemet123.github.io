Privacy Policy

This Covers The Website At joemet123.com. Last Updated 9 September 2026.

The Short Version

I don't run accounts, logins, or analytics on this site. The only information you hand me directly is what you type into the contact form.

Everything else on this page is about third-party services the site embeds — advertising, video players, fonts — which set their own cookies and see your visit. Those are described honestly below rather than buried.

Note: the separate policy at privacy-policy.html covers the Bubble Pop Master mobile app only. It does not apply to this website.

What I Collect Directly

Nothing automatically. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no newsletter, and no analytics package on this site.

If you use the contact form, you submit your name, your email address, and your message. That form is processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards it to my inbox. I use those details only to reply to you. I don't sell them, and I don't add you to any mailing list. FormSubmit handles the message in transit under its own privacy policy.

Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info/choices.

I don't receive your personal information from Google as part of this. I see aggregate performance figures only.

Embedded Services

Several parts of this site load content from other companies. When that content loads, those companies can see your IP address and may set their own cookies, whether or not you interact with them. Each operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Twitch — the stream player and the chat panel on the Live page.
  • YouTube — video thumbnails, and the player that opens when you click a video.
  • Discord — the server widget on the home page, plus a public member count.
  • PayPal — the donate button.
  • Google Fonts — the typeface used across the site.
  • RSS2JSON — fetches my public YouTube upload feed to populate the video carousel.

Affiliate Links

Some outbound links are affiliate links — currently GFuel and KontrolFreek. The KontrolFreek link routes through Commission Junction's tracking domain, which records that the click came from this site so any resulting purchase is credited to me.

It costs you nothing extra, and I earn a commission if you buy. Merch links send you to Streamlabs, which handles the entire purchase; I never see your payment details.

Your Choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, which will stop most of the tracking described above. Ad blockers also prevent the AdSense and embed scripts from loading. Nothing on this site breaks if you do either — embedded players simply won't appear.

If you're in the EU, UK, or another region with equivalent rules, you have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you. Since the only thing I hold is contact-form email, email me and I'll delete it.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13 and I don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent me information through the contact form, email me and I'll remove it.

Changes & Contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the change gets described here rather than quietly absorbed.
