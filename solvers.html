What A Solver Actually Is

Zombies Easter Eggs Are Full Of Steps That Are Not Hard So Much As They Are Fiddly. A Code That Depends On Two Words Sitting On Two Different Monitors. A Grid Of Nine Scrolls Where Flipping One Flips Four Of Its Neighbours. Three Geared Handles Where Turning One Moves The Other Two By Double. You Can Work All Of It Out By Hand, But Not While A Horde Is Chewing On You.

So Each Tool Takes The Thing You Can See On Your Screen And Hands Back The Thing You Need To Do. No Video To Scrub Through, No Wiki Tab To Alt-Tab Into. Tell It What Your Game Rolled And The Answer Updates Next To Your Inputs As You Go.

Everything Runs Locally In Your Browser. There Is No Account, No Upload, And Nothing You Type Ever Leaves Your Device — Which Also Means You Can Leave A Map Page Open On A Phone Next To You Mid-Round.

Solver

You Give It Your Inputs, It Calculates Your Answer. The Gorgofex Code, The Nexus Handles, The Kowakujo Scroll Grid.

Reference

Steps That Are Identical Every Single Game, Written Out Plainly. The Veytharion Block Puzzle, The Nyxara Dial Order, The Dravakar Riddles.

Before You Ask

Do These Cost Anything?

No. Every Solver And Cheat Sheet On This Site Is Free And Always Will Be. There Is Nothing To Sign Up For.

Will They Work On My Phone?

Yes. The Map Pages Are Built Mobile-First, So You Can Keep One Open On A Phone Beside You While You Play On Console Or PC.

Is This Cheating?

No. Nothing Here Touches The Game. These Are Reference Sheets And Calculators For Steps The Game Randomises Every Match — The Same Thing You'd Scribble On Paper, Just Faster.

Why Is My Answer Different To My Friend's?

Because Most Of These Steps Reroll Per Game. That Is The Whole Point Of A Solver — Put In What Your Match Gave You, Not What A Video Showed Someone Else.

Something Looks Wrong. Now What?

Tell Me In The Discord. Fixes Usually Land Faster Than New Tools Do.

Which Should I Open First?

If You Play Black Ops 7, Start There — It Is The Most Complete Set. Black Ops 6 Is Close Behind. Black Ops 3 Is Still Being Built Out.
