Bubble Pop Master — Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 August 2026

Bubble Pop Master does not collect any data about you or your child. None at all.

It is a bubble-popping game made for very young children, so the shortest honest privacy policy is the right one. This page explains what that means precisely.

What is collected

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party software development kits of any kind. It does not ask for your name, your email address, your location, your contacts, your photos, or your microphone or camera.

What leaves the device

Nothing. Bubble Pop Master makes no network requests. The only exception is Apple's own App Store, which handles the optional in-app purchase and the restoring of a previous purchase. Those transactions are between you and Apple; the developer never sees your payment details, and no personal information is passed to the developer as part of them.

What is stored, and where

The app remembers your settings and a running count of bubbles popped — for example the chosen background, the number of bubbles, whether sound is on, and the total pops. All of it is kept on the device itself, in the app's own storage. It is never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Bubble Pop Master is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's Kids Category requirements. Because the app collects no personal information whatsoever, there is nothing to disclose, nothing to share, and nothing for a parent to request or delete beyond removing the app.

The app contains no advertising, no links to websites, no social media features, and no way to communicate with anyone. The one in-app purchase sits behind a parental gate that a young child is not expected to be able to pass.

Tracking

Bubble Pop Master does not track users, does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), and does not share any data with data brokers or advertising networks. It will never present the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing it would ask permission for.

Motion

An optional "tilt to steer" setting, off by default, reads the device's accelerometer so the bubbles drift the way the device is leaning. Those readings are used to move bubbles on screen in that instant and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins collecting anything, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here rather than quietly absorbed.
