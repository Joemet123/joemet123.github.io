Overview

ProDuct is an app for iPhone and iPad that holds you to your daily routine: a schedule of tasks, an XP and level system, and a history of what you finished. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your schedule, tasks, notes and progress live on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage. They never come to us.

The only third party that receives data from the app is Google AdMob, which serves the banner ad that keeps ProDuct free. Ads can only be personalised if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you choose not to, the app never receives your device's advertising identifier and the ads are chosen from context alone. If you buy the optional Remove Ads upgrade, the banner is removed completely and Google's ad software is no longer started.

Contact and support

Questions, problems, feature requests or anything about your data: use the contact form or email Business@joemet123.com. Put "ProDuct" in the subject and you will have a reply within 30 days. Joemet123 LLC is the data controller for this app.

What the app stores

Your schedule and its tasks (names, times and notes), the decisions you make on them (completed, failed or seen), your XP, level and history, and your settings are saved on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage. The app does not ask you to sign in and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, calendar, photos, microphone, camera or location.

Deleting the app removes its data from that device. "Export data" in Settings creates a file that you save wherever you choose, and "Import data" reads a file that you pick; we never receive these files.

Widgets and Live Activities

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and the Live Activity show what is due today. They read a small summary of your day from storage shared only between ProDuct and its own widget on your device. Nothing about them leaves your device. You can turn the Live Activity off in the app's Settings, or in iOS Settings.

Notifications

If you turn on alerts, the app asks for permission to send notifications, and iOS schedules each reminder on your device. They are not sent through any server of ours.

Backups

If you are signed in to iCloud, ProDuct keeps a compressed backup of your schedule, history and settings in your own iCloud key-value storage so that a new device can restore it. It is stored by Apple under your Apple Account, is readable only by ProDuct on your devices, and is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy; we cannot see or access it. Signing out of iCloud, or turning iCloud off for ProDuct in iOS Settings, stops it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows one banner ad, served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure that ad, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • IP address, and an approximate, city-level location derived from it;
  • device model, operating system version and language;
  • whether an ad was shown, seen or tapped;
  • diagnostic and performance information about ad delivery;
  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow tracking.

Google uses this to choose and measure ads and to detect fraud and invalid traffic. How Google handles it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Your schedule, tasks, notes and progress are never given to the ad software.

Tracking permission and consent

A few seconds after the first banner appears, iOS asks once whether ProDuct may track you (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt); it does not ask again unless you reinstall. If you allow tracking, Google may use your advertising identifier to personalise and measure ads. If you ask the app not to track, it never receives that identifier and ads are contextual. You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Owners of Remove Ads are never asked.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested. The app also includes Apple's SKAdNetwork identifiers, which let ad networks count installs that came from an ad without identifying you.

Purchases (Apple)

The app sells exactly one thing: an optional, one-time "Remove Ads" purchase that removes the banner for good. Once you own it, the banner disappears straight away, and from then on the app does not start Google's ad software or show its consent form or the tracking prompt, so nothing further is sent to Google AdMob. It is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment and holds your payment details; the app only receives Apple's confirmation that the purchase completed. Purchases are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and the App Store terms, and "Restore purchase" in Settings re-checks with Apple on a new device.

Children

ProDuct is a general-audience app and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age: the app has no accounts and no way to send us anything from inside it. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at the address above and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

You control your data directly: edit or delete tasks and history in the app, use "Reset all data" in Settings, delete the app, or remove its iCloud data in iOS Settings. You can turn tracking off in iOS Settings at any time. For data processed by Google AdMob, you can exercise your rights with Google as described in its privacy policy. If you live in a region with data protection rights such as access, correction or deletion, write to us at the address above and we will help.

Security and retention

We operate no servers and hold no database, so there is nothing for us to retain, breach or hand over. Apple keeps your iCloud data for as long as you keep it in your account, and Google retains advertising data under its own policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date. If the app ever begins to collect something it does not collect today, that change will appear here and in the App Store's own privacy labels before it ships.
