A Satisfying Light Switch App For iPhone And iPad

EZ Light Switch Is A Free ASMR Fidget App That Is Just A Light Switch. Flip It And Glowing Bulbs Light Up A Living Fish Tank While Colourful Bubbles Burst Out All Around The Switch. Flip It Again And The Room Goes Dark.

There Is No Score, No Timer And No Way To Lose, Just A Switch That Answers The Instant You Touch It, Every Single Time. It Is A Quiet, Calm Fidget For Anyone Who Flips Switches Because It Feels Good, Including Autistic, ADHD And Sensory-Seeking People.

Screenshots

Five Switches To Flip

  • Rocker: A Wide Paddle With A Tiny Pilot Light.
  • Classic Toggle: The Traditional Bat-Handle Wall Switch, Sharp And Loud.
  • Push Button: Press It In And It Lights Up.
  • Pull Chain: Drag The Chain Down, Let Go And Listen To It Ring.
  • Big Lever: A Whole-Arm Lever. Deeply Unnecessary. Very Good.

Two Switches Are There When You Open The App, And The Other Three Open Up As You Flip, All Five Inside Your First Sitting.

Satisfying ASMR Clicks And Haptics

There Are No Recordings In This App. Each Click Is Synthesized From The Way A Real Mechanism Behaves: The Lever Leaves Its Detent, And A Few Milliseconds Later The Contact Slams Home. Flip The Same Switch A Hundred Times And It Never Sounds Looped. Matching Haptics Make Every Flip Feel As Good As It Sounds.

Six Rooms, One Fish Tank

Choose Ocean, Crimson, Workshop, Neon, Dusk Or Emerald From The Row Of Colours At The Bottom Of The Screen. Every Room Is A Fish Tank: Sunlight Falls Through The Water, Coral And Sea Grass Grow From A Sandy Floor, And A Crowd Of Fish, A Friendly Shark, An Octopus, Jellyfish And A Sea Turtle Drift Slowly Past. Real-Looking Glass Bulbs Glow Overhead, With Beams Of Light Falling Through The Water.

Bubbles For Every Flip

Every Time The Light Comes On, Colourful Bubbles Burst Out All Around The Switch, And Milestones And Newly Unlocked Switches Get An Even Bigger Burst. With Reduce Motion Turned On, The Tank Holds Still And The Bubbles Stay Away.

EZ Light Switch FAQ

Is EZ Light Switch Free?

Yes. EZ Light Switch Is Free To Download And Free To Play. A Small Banner Ad Runs Along The Top, And A One-Time Remove Ads Purchase (Shareable With Family Sharing) Takes It Away For Good.

What Is EZ Light Switch?

An ASMR Fidget App Built Around Light Switches: Flip A Switch, Light Up A Fish Tank And Pop Colourful Bubbles. There Is No Score, No Timer And No Way To Lose.

Does EZ Light Switch Work Offline?

Yes. Everything Runs On Your Device, So You Can Flip Switches Anywhere Without An Internet Connection. Only Ads And The Remove Ads Purchase Need One.

Is EZ Light Switch Good For Autistic Or Sensory-Seeking People?

It Was Designed With Them In Mind: Predictable, Calm, Quiet, With Switches That Answer The Instant You Touch Them. Nothing Flashes Or Times Out, And Reduce Motion Holds The Fish Tank Still And Keeps The Bubbles Away.

Do The Clicks Use Recorded Sounds?

No. Every Click Is Synthesized From How A Real Mechanism Behaves, So Flipping The Same Switch A Hundred Times Never Sounds Looped. Matching Haptics Make Each Flip Feel As Good As It Sounds.

Which Devices Does EZ Light Switch Run On?

iPhone And iPad Running iOS 26 Or Later. On iPad The Switch And The Fish Tank Fill The Whole Screen.

Do The Ads Track Me?

No. The Ads Are Contextual And Non-Personalised, The App Never Requests The Advertising Identifier And Nothing Is Tracked Across Other Apps. See The Privacy Policy For Details.
