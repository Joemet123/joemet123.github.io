Overview

EZ Light Switch is an iPhone and iPad app about flipping light switches. It is free to download, with optional in-app purchases and, from version 2.5, an Apple Watch app that comes with Pro. No accounts, no sign-in, no servers: your flip count stays on your device.

Joemet123 LLC receives no personal data from the app. The only third party whose software runs in it is Google AdMob, which serves the single banner ad that keeps the app free to download. The ad appears on iPhone and iPad; the Apple Watch app has no ads. Those ads are contextual only. The app never asks for your device's advertising identifier, shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt because it has nothing to ask for, and does not build a profile of you or follow you into other apps. Apple processes purchases, and if you buy Pro or Remove Ads the ad software is never started.

Contact and support

Questions, problems, feature requests or anything about your data: use the contact form or email Business@joemet123.com. Put "EZ Light Switch" in the subject and you will have a reply within 3 business days, and within 30 days for privacy requests. Joemet123 LLC is the data controller for this app.

If you email us or use the form on the support page, we receive the name, email address and message you send. The form is delivered by Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a service that passes the message to our inbox. We use these details only to reply to you, keep them only as long as needed for support or legal reasons, delete them on request, and never sell them or add you to a mailing list. Nothing inside the app sends us anything. The website has its own policy at joemet123.com/site-privacy.html.

From version 2.5 the app has an information button in its bottom row. It opens an About screen that shows the app version and links to this policy, to the support page and, while ads are shown, to the page for reporting an ad. The links open in your browser; the app itself sends nothing when you tap them.

What the app stores on your device

Your total flip count, your per-switch counts, your best session, which switch you last used, and your chosen room are saved only on your device, using Apple's standard on-device settings storage. They are never sent to us and we never see them.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. The app does not ask you to sign in and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, calendar, microphone, camera or precise location.

The app also keeps a locally cached record of whether you have removed ads, which it re-verifies with Apple each time it launches. From version 2.5 the same applies to whether you own EZ Light Switch Pro, and the app also keeps the app version in which it last asked for an App Store rating (see Rating requests below). Copies of these settings can also end up in the backups you make of your own device, under your Apple Account's backup settings.

The Apple Watch app

From version 2.5, EZ Light Switch Pro includes an Apple Watch app, where a tap flips the switch and the Digital Crown dims the light. Without Pro, the watch app shows only the offer to get Pro.

The Apple Watch app has no ads, no accounts and no third-party code. It collects nothing and sends nothing to us or to any third party; its only contact is with Apple, to check or complete a Pro purchase. It saves only the room you chose, the dimmer level you set and a cached record of whether you own Pro, on the watch itself, using Apple's standard on-device settings storage. Removing the app from your watch deletes what it saved.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

On iPhone and iPad the app shows one banner ad, served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Google acts as an independent controller of the data its software collects, under Google's own terms and privacy policy. To serve and measure that ad, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • IP address, and an approximate, city-level location derived from it;
  • device identifiers, model, operating system version and language;
  • ad-related data, such as which ads were requested, shown, seen or tapped;
  • crash reports, diagnostic and performance information about ad delivery.

Google uses this to choose an ad appropriate to the moment, to measure whether it was delivered and how it performed, to detect fraud and invalid traffic, and to keep the ad service working. How Google handles it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We see aggregate performance figures only, and never data about you.

Reporting an ad. If an ad looks inappropriate, if the ad shows an AdChoices icon (a small icon in its corner), tap it to see why the ad was shown and to report it to Google. You can also report it to us: from version 2.5, open the About screen and choose Report an ad, or go straight to the Report an ad form on the support page and tell us the date and what the ad showed. We use reports to block that advertiser or ad category in AdMob.

What the ads deliberately do not do

Every ad request this app makes is configured three ways, and all three are on at all times for everyone:

  • Non-personalised. Ads are chosen from the context, not from a profile of you. No interest profile is built or used.
  • Child age-restricted. As a precaution, requests are sent with Google's child-directed (age-restricted) treatment switched on, which turns off personalised advertising, remarketing and interest-based targeting on Google's side regardless of any other setting.
  • General audiences only. Ad content is capped at Google's "General" rating, the most restrictive one available.

Because of this, the app never requests the advertising identifier (IDFA), and it therefore shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt — there is nothing it would be asking permission for. From version 1.7, the app also takes part in Apple's SKAdNetwork, which tells an advertiser only that an install happened after one of their ads, in aggregate. It uses no advertising identifier and cannot be traced to you, your device or what you do in other apps, so your installs are not matched back to you. Nothing this app collects is linked with data from other companies' apps or websites for advertising, which is why it is declared as not tracking you. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google's consent library runs before any ad is requested. Because every request is marked as child age-restricted, Google serves only non-personalised ads there and does not ask for ad-personalisation consent, so you will normally see no consent form. If a consent form is ever shown, an "Ad privacy choices" button reopens it, so you can change or withdraw your choice at any time. From version 2.5 that button is in the About screen (the information button in the bottom row); in earlier versions it is in the bottom row itself. Outside the EEA and the UK the button does not appear.

Purchases (Apple)

The app sells one-time purchases through Apple's in-app purchase system; neither is a subscription. "Remove Ads" hides the banner ad permanently. From version 2.5 there is also "EZ Light Switch Pro", which hides the banner ad, adds six more rooms and unlocks the Apple Watch app. Both can be shared through Family Sharing. Apple processes the payment and holds your payment details; the app never sees your card or account information, only a confirmation from Apple that a purchase completed, which it uses to hide the ad and unlock what you bought. Purchases are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and the App Store terms.

A "Restore Purchases" button on the purchase screen, and on the Apple Watch app's Pro screen, re-checks with Apple on a new device (versions before 2.5 have a "Restore purchase" button). Once ads are removed, by either purchase, the app never starts the Google ad SDK or its consent form at all. Purchases are not refundable except as Apple's policies provide.

Rating requests

From version 2.5 the app may ask for an App Store rating, using Apple's built-in rating prompt, at most once per app version and only when you have been flipping for a while and have paused. Apple decides whether the prompt appears, draws it and handles your answer; the app does not see whether you rate or what you write, and nothing is sent to us. To remember which version it last asked in, the app saves that version number on your device.

Third parties and related companies

Google (AdMob and the consent library that ships with it) is the only third party whose software runs in the app. Apart from Apple, which processes purchases under its own privacy policy, it is the only third party that receives data from the app, and only to serve, measure and secure the banner ad. Its software never starts for anyone who has removed ads or bought Pro, and the Apple Watch app contains no third-party code. Nothing else from the app is shared with anyone.

Our commitment is this: any third party that receives user data from this app, such as an advertising network, an analytics tool or another SDK, and any parent, subsidiary or other related company of Joemet123 LLC, must give that data the same or equal protection as this policy states and as Apple's App Review Guidelines require. Today no parent, subsidiary or related company receives any data from the app. We use Google's software only in the most protective configuration it offers (non-personalised, marked child age-restricted, limited to the General content rating, and with no request for the advertising identifier), so that the data it receives is limited to what is described under Advertising above. If a third party were ever added, or that configuration were to change, this policy would be updated before the change ships.

Children

The app is made for a general audience, is rated 4+ and is not in Apple's Kids Category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age, including children under 13: the app has no accounts, no forms and no way to send us anything from inside it. The only data collected when you use the app is the technical ad-request data Google's software collects, described under Advertising above.

Because people of all ages use it, every ad request is nonetheless sent with Google's child-directed treatment switched on and non-personalised, which is the most protective configuration Google offers. This is a precaution, not a description of who the app is for. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example in an email or through the support form, contact us at the address above and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Because the app keeps your data only on your device, you control it directly: deleting the app (and the Apple Watch app) removes everything it saved, including the cached purchase records. Your purchases stay with Apple and can be restored. The app asks for no consent of its own, because it collects nothing of its own. Every ad request is non-personalised and marked child age-restricted, so there is no ad-personalisation consent to give; if Google's consent form is ever shown (in the EEA or the UK), the "Ad privacy choices" button reopens it so you can change or withdraw your choice at any time.

To stop all ad-related data collection, buy Pro or Remove Ads, after which the Google ad software is never started on your device, or delete the app. For the data processed by Google AdMob, you can exercise your rights with Google as described in its privacy policy; we cannot look you up in Google's systems, but we will gladly help you find the right place to ask. To have an email or support-form message deleted, write to Business@joemet123.com and we will delete it within 30 days. If you live in a region with data protection rights such as access, correction, deletion or withdrawing consent, write to us at the same address and we will help.

Security and retention

We operate no servers for this app and hold no database for it, so there is nothing for us to retain, breach or hand over from the app itself. What the app saves stays on your device until you delete the app, and copies in your own device backups follow your Apple Account's backup settings. Emails and support-form messages are kept only as long as needed to answer you and for support or legal reasons, and are deleted on request. Google retains advertising data under its own retention policies, and Apple retains purchase records under its own.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date. If the app ever begins to collect something it does not collect today, that change will appear here and in the App Store's own privacy labels before it ships.

The 17 October 2026 update describes version 2.5: Pro, the Apple Watch app, rating requests and the About screen. It also adds a section on third parties and expands the text on retention, your choices and reporting an ad. Sentences that name a version apply from that version; earlier versions behave as described by the rest of the policy.
