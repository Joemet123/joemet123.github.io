Overview

Sensory Sink (formerly Water Play) is a kitchen sink to play with, made for toddlers and for autistic kids who are drawn to water. It does not collect any data about you or your child. None at all.

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software of any kind. The only company that ever sees anything is Apple, and only if you choose to buy the one in-app purchase. Everything below says what that means precisely.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: Business@joemet123.com. Put "Sensory Sink" in the subject and you will have a reply within 30 days. Joemet123 LLC is the data controller for this app.

What is collected

Nothing. The app does not ask for your name, your email address, your location, your contacts, your photos, or your microphone or camera. It runs no analytics and shows no advertising.

What leaves the device

Nothing from you. Sensory Sink itself has no networking code: it makes no requests to any server, including the developer's own, and it works the same with the device in aeroplane mode. The one exception is Apple's own App Store framework (StoreKit), which handles the app's single optional purchase and Restore Purchases. That exchange is between your device and Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; the developer never receives your name, your Apple ID or any payment details, and cannot see who bought it.

The in-app purchase

Sensory Sink has one optional purchase, Unlock Everything: a one-time, non-consumable purchase that opens more window themes, wall options, toys and water sounds. There is no subscription and nothing else to buy. It sits behind a grown-up gate (a multiplication question that has to be typed in) so a child tapping around the settings cannot reach it. Purchases and refunds are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store.

What is stored, and where

Only your settings. Sensory Sink remembers the choices made on its settings sheet — which window, wall, toys and water sound you picked — in the app's own preferences on the device, so the sink looks the same next time. Nothing else is saved: no progress, no counters, no files, and nothing is ever uploaded. Deleting the app deletes those preferences. Whether Unlock Everything has been bought is recorded by Apple with your Apple ID, not by the app, which is why Restore Purchases works on a new device.

Children

Sensory Sink is made for children and is offered in the App Store's Kids category for ages 5 and under. It complies with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and with the GDPR's rules for children's data (Article 8).

  • It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.
  • It contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or software development kits.
  • It has no links out of the app, no social features and no way to contact anyone.
  • Its one optional purchase, and the option to restore it, sit behind a grown-up gate: a multiplication question that has to be typed in.

Parents can email Business@joemet123.com with any question. There is nothing to review or delete, because nothing is stored off the device.

Your rights

Because the app holds nothing about you, there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete on our side. If you believe otherwise, write to the address above and we will look into it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.
