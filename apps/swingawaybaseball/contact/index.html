Overview

Something not working, a swing that felt wrong, or an idea for the ballpark? Send a message below and a person will reply.

Email: Business@joemet123.com
Put Swing Away Baseball in the subject line. We answer within 3 business days, and within 30 days for privacy requests.

Send a message

Prefer Email? Write To Business@joemet123.com Directly.

What to include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About).
  • What you were doing when it happened: the mode (Home Run Derby, Practice or Game), the difficulty, and what you expected instead.
  • If the game felt slow or choppy, whether Settings → Graphics was on High or Low, and whether Low Power Mode was on.
  • A screenshot or screen recording if you have one.

Ads and ad privacy

The game is free and shows ads only at breaks in play: never while you bat. If your region requires it, Ad privacy choices on the menu reopens Google's consent screen. Tracking can be turned off at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; the game plays exactly the same either way. If an ad seems inappropriate, tell us what it showed and roughly when.

Your data

Swing Away Baseball has no accounts and no servers of ours. Your records and settings stay on your iPhone. Messages sent here are used only to answer you. The full privacy policy has the details.
