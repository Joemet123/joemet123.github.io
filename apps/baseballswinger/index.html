Overview

No account, and your game stays on your phone. Baseball Swinger never asks who you are. Your records and settings are saved only on your device. The free game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google processes some device information to show and measure those ads. Saying no to tracking changes nothing about the game.

Contact

Baseball Swinger is published by Joemet123 LLC, the data controller for this app. Questions or requests about your data: use the contact form or email Business@joemet123.com, with "Baseball Swinger" in the subject. You will have a reply within 30 days.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your Home Run Derby bests and your Game win–loss–tie record for each difficulty, your longest home run, whether you have seen the swing tutorial, and your choices: batter, bat, batting side, difficulty, mode, number of innings, time of day, camera view, strike-zone guide, graphics quality, sound and haptics. These stay in the app's own storage on your device and are never uploaded to us; we have no server. Deleting the app deletes them, though an iCloud or computer backup of your device may keep a copy under your backup settings.

The game never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Advertising

The free game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Ads appear only at breaks in play, never while you bat: a banner on the menu, the pause screen and the results screen, and a full-screen ad shortly after a finished round. Quitting to the menu never triggers a full-screen ad. Ads are requested with Google's general-audience content rating.

To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may process your IP address (and the approximate location it suggests), device and app information such as the model, operating system and app version, ad impressions, taps and other ad interactions, and diagnostic, security and fraud-prevention information, including identifiers used for those purposes. If you allow tracking (below), Google may also use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalise ads. How Google uses this information is set out in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent and tracking

Before any ad is requested, Google's User Messaging Platform checks whether your region requires a privacy choice and, if so, shows you one. Where Google requires it, the menu shows Ad privacy choices so you can change your answer at any time. Only after that does the game ask Apple's App Tracking Transparency question. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the app cannot read your advertising identifier and ads can still appear, but they are not personalised using that identifier. The game plays exactly the same either way. You can change the tracking setting later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Messages you send us

If you use the contact form or email us, we receive your name, your email address and whatever you write. We use them only to answer you, and keep the correspondence only as long as needed for support or legal reasons. The contact form is delivered by Web3Forms, which passes the message to our inbox. Please don't send anything sensitive that your question doesn't need.

This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, processes normal connection information such as your IP address; see GitHub's Privacy Statement. The rest of joemet123.com is covered by the site privacy policy.

Children

Baseball Swinger has no accounts and never asks for a birth date or any personal information, from anyone of any age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through the contact form or email, tell us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. We hold nothing about you except messages you send us, and we will act on any request about those. For advertising data, use the Ad privacy choices in the menu, the iOS Tracking setting, or Google's own privacy tools; we are happy to help point you to the right place.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.
