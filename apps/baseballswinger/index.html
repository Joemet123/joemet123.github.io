Overview

Your game saves stay on your device. Baseball Swinger does not require an account. Advertising-enabled versions use Google AdMob, which processes information to deliver and measure ads. You can play without granting tracking permission.

Who is responsible

Baseball Swinger is published by Joemet123 LLC. For privacy questions or requests, email Business@joemet123.com and include “Baseball Swinger” in the subject.

Game information stored on your device

The game stores your high score, longest home run, 3-Out Challenge progress, tutorial status, sound and haptics preferences, and choices such as your batter, bat, batting side, camera, and power-up on your device. We do not receive these game saves on a server. The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location.

Deleting the app normally removes its local saves. A device backup managed by Apple may retain or restore app data according to your backup settings.

Advertising

Where advertising is enabled, Baseball Swinger uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). The current game may show an interstitial ad after a completed 3-Out Challenge run. Free Hit mode is ad-free, and voluntarily returning to the menu does not trigger an interstitial. Wall signs can be local sponsor or house artwork; displaying a local sign alone does not send data to an advertising network.

After Google's consent flow is resolved where required, the app may request Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. If you allow tracking and applicable consent choices permit it, Google may use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and other permitted signals for personalized advertising and measurement. If you deny ATT, the app does not receive access to IDFA, gameplay is unchanged, and ads may still be served using data and methods that do not require that permission. Google and its service providers may process:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it;
  • device and app information, such as operating system, device type, and app identifiers;
  • ad delivery, impressions, clicks, and other ad interactions;
  • diagnostic, performance, security, and fraud-prevention information, including identifiers used for these purposes.

This information is used for ad delivery and measurement, security, fraud prevention, and service operation. Google's use, sharing, retention, and international processing of information are described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Development builds may use Google's test ads. Versions with advertising disabled do not initialize the ad service.

Consent and privacy choices

In advertising-enabled releases, Google's User Messaging Platform checks whether a privacy message is required before ad requests are allowed. Where a message is required, it provides the applicable choices. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, the menu displays Ad privacy choices so you can reopen it. If the app cannot obtain an allowed ad-request state, it continues without network ads.

You can find this policy from the game menu. Denying Apple's tracking permission does not block gameplay and does not block every kind of data processing involved in advertising. Where ATT is requested, you can deny it without losing access to the game.

Support and external links

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you choose to send. We use it to respond and resolve the issue, and retain correspondence only as reasonably necessary for support, security, or legal obligations. Please do not send sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

Opening this policy or an advertiser's destination takes you to a website governed by its own privacy practices. This policy site is hosted using GitHub Pages; hosting providers may process normal connection and security information, such as your IP address. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Children and families

The game does not ask players to provide a birth date or create an account. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. Ad content is requested with Google's general-audience content rating; this setting alone is not an age-verification system.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Use the contact address above for information you have sent us. For advertising information controlled by Google, consult Google's privacy tools and policy; we can help direct your request. Local game saves are controlled on your device.

Security and retention

We use reasonable measures to protect support correspondence. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Local saves remain until removed or replaced on your device. Advertising and hosting providers retain information under their own policies and applicable obligations.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change. Material changes will be communicated where required.
