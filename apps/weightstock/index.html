Overview

WeightStock is a weight tracker for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own: your readings, notes and goal are stored on your device and never come to us.

WeightStock never sends your weight or health data anywhere. The only third party that can receive any data from the app is Google AdMob, which serves the one banner ad that keeps WeightStock free, and only after you allow the ad in the app; it receives no health or weight data. The app never asks to track you, so ads are not personalised. If you say no to the ad, or buy the optional WeightStock Pro purchase, the app requests no ads.

Contact and support

Questions, problems, feature requests or anything about your data: Business@joemet123.com. Put "WeightStock" in the subject and you will have a reply within 30 days. Joemet123 LLC is the data controller for this app.

"Send feedback" in the app's Settings, under Help, opens an email to that address in your mail app with the app's version, your iOS version and your device model filled in, so a problem can be reproduced. It contains none of your readings, and nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.

What the app stores

The weights and body-fat percentages you log, the notes and tags you attach to them (a tag is a short label such as “Salty meal” or “Travel”, or words of your own), your goal, and your settings (units, direction, chart choices) are saved on your device, inside the app. Deleting a reading removes it; "Erase all data" in Settings removes everything; deleting the app removes all of it from that device. The app does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

If you turn on the app lock (WeightStock Pro), iOS checks your face, fingerprint or device passcode itself; the app only learns whether the check succeeded and never sees or stores biometric data. Your choice of app icon or theme is a setting like any other.

Automatic backups and the activity log

So your history is never lost, the app saves your readings, tags, notes and settings to a file inside the app on your device after every change, and keeps a copy once a day (the last two weeks, then one a month for a year) and before anything drastic, such as erasing everything or restoring a backup. It also keeps a short activity log: one line for each change to your readings, such as a reading logged, edited, deleted, imported or saved to Apple Health. These files stay on your device. You can see them in the Files app under On My iPhone › WeightStock, restore a backup from Settings › Data › Automatic backups, and they are part of your device's own backup if you use iCloud Backup or a computer, which Apple handles. The app never uploads them and never stores them in iCloud itself. "Erase all data" empties the app and then asks whether to delete these files too; if you keep them, you can undo the erase from Automatic backups. Deleting the app removes the files from that device.

To use WeightStock on another device, you can send a backup there yourself (for example with AirDrop) and restore it, or turn on saving to Apple Health on one device and syncing on the other, so that Apple's Health app carries your weigh-ins between your own devices under Apple's own privacy terms. The forecast on the chart is worked out on your device from your own readings.

Widgets

If you add a WeightStock widget to your Home Screen, Lock Screen or StandBy, the app keeps a small snapshot for it to draw: your latest weight, recent readings and your goal. The snapshot is held in the iOS keychain on your device, in an access group shared only by the app and its widget. It is not backed up, not synced to other devices and never sent anywhere, and widgets show no ads. "Erase all data" in Settings removes it, and it is cleared again when the app is installed. If you delete the app, iOS may keep that small item until the app is next installed; no other app can read it.

Apple Watch

The WeightStock watch app shows your latest weight, the week's change and the last 30 days. The iPhone app sends it that snapshot through Apple's Watch Connectivity, directly between your iPhone and your paired watch; a weight you log on the watch goes back to the iPhone app the same way. Nothing passes through us. The watch keeps only the last snapshot and any weigh-in still waiting for your iPhone.

Siri, Shortcuts and controls

You can log a weight or ask for your latest one with Siri, the Shortcuts app or the Action button, and open the log from a Control Center button. Your iPhone must be unlocked for either. Apple handles the voice request under Apple's Privacy Policy; WeightStock receives only the number you gave, saves it on your device like any reading you type, and says your latest weight only when you ask.

Weekly summary notification

The weekly summary is optional and off until you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, the app schedules one notification on your device for Monday morning, using iOS local notifications: there is no push service and no server, and iOS asks for your permission the first time you turn it on. The notification says how your week changed and how many days you weighed in; it never shows your weight itself. Turn it off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional. If you choose to sync, iOS asks for permission to read four things: weight, body fat percentage, resting heart rate and blood pressure. Resting heart rate and blood pressure are shown under Other measurements, beside how your weight has moved, with a note that this is only what was logged and not medical advice. If you turn on "Sync when the app opens" in Settings, new readings are read on your device when you open the app. You can turn that off in Settings, and remove the app's access at any time in the Health app.

Writing to Apple Health is optional too, and off until you turn on "Save weigh-ins to Apple Health" in Settings. iOS then asks for permission to write weight and body fat percentage, and the app saves the weights and body fat you log in WeightStock, on your iPhone or your watch, to Apple Health, marked as entered by you. It never writes sample data or anything it did not get from you. Before its first write it keeps a copy of your Apple Health weight and body-fat history in a file on your device (Files › WeightStock › Apple Health Backup). That copy stays on this device only: it is left out of the device's backups, because Apple Health backs up its own data, and it is encrypted while the device is locked. When you edit or delete a reading in WeightStock, the app asks before it changes or deletes it in Apple Health, and it can only delete readings it saved itself; readings another app put into Apple Health are left for you to manage in the Health app. Each change it makes in Apple Health is noted in the activity log on your device.

Data read from or written to Apple Health is used only to draw your charts and work out the numbers shown in the app. It never leaves your device through the app, is never stored in iCloud by the app, is never used for advertising or marketing, never given to Google or anyone else, and never sold.

Importing, exporting and sharing

"Import data" reads a file you pick (an Apple Health export, or an export from RENPHO, Withings, Fitbit, Happy Scale, Libra, MyFitnessPal or another app) on your device; nothing is uploaded. Backups, CSV exports and PDF reports are files made on your device that go wherever you choose through the iOS share sheet. "Share your progress" draws an image on your device, with the WeightStock mark on it, which likewise goes only where you send it, and it can hide your actual weight so it shows changes only. We never receive any of these files or images.

Sample data

"Explore with sample data" fills the app with a made-up history generated on your device. It contains no real person's readings and is removed when you log or import your own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version can show one banner ad, served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. No ad is requested, and nothing is sent to Google, until you answer the app's own question about the ad with "Allow the ad"; "No ads" keeps the app free of ads, and you can change your answer at any time under Settings › WeightStock Pro › Show the ad. The app never shows Apple's tracking prompt and never receives your device's advertising identifier, so ads are chosen without tracking you across other apps. To serve and measure the ad, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • IP address, and an approximate, city-level location derived from it;
  • device model, operating system version and language;
  • whether an ad was shown, seen or tapped;
  • diagnostic and performance information about ad delivery.

Google uses this to choose and measure ads and to detect fraud and invalid traffic. How Google handles it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Your readings, notes and Apple Health data are never given to the ad software.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can reopen it at any time from the "Privacy choices" button in the app's Settings, under WeightStock Pro. The app also includes Apple's SKAdNetwork identifiers, which let ad networks count installs that came from an ad without identifying you.

Ads are limited to Google's General content rating. "About the ad" in Settings, under WeightStock Pro, explains in the app what chooses the ad, and its "Report an ad" button opens an email to us so we can have an offensive or unsuitable ad blocked. You can also report an ad to Google from the small AdChoices icon in its corner.

Purchases (Apple)

The app sells exactly one thing: WeightStock Pro, an optional, one-time purchase (not a subscription). It unlocks custom chart ranges (any calendar month or any two dates), notes on readings, CSV and PDF export, the Face ID lock, the themes other than Night, the alternate app icons and the medium and large home-screen widgets, and it removes the banner ad; the app then stops requesting ads on every device signed in to your Apple Account. It is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment and holds your payment details; the app only receives Apple's confirmation that the purchase completed. Purchases are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and the App Store terms, and "Restore purchase" in Settings re-checks with Apple on a new device. A free 14-day trial of WeightStock Pro is offered as a separate, free in-app item: it costs nothing, never renews, and switches Pro off by itself after 14 days.

App Store ratings

Now and then, after you save a reading, the app may ask iOS to show Apple's standard rating prompt. Apple decides whether it appears and handles whatever you choose; the app receives nothing back.

Not medical advice

WeightStock is a tracking tool. It does not give medical advice, and its charts and projections are not a diagnosis. Talk to a doctor before starting a weight-loss or weight-gain plan.

Children

WeightStock is a general-audience app and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age: the app has no accounts, and the only thing that can reach us from it is an email you choose to write and send yourself. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at the address above and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

You control your data directly: edit or delete any reading in Settings, under All readings, back up or export everything, restore an automatic backup, erase all data in Settings, turn off saving to Apple Health, remove Apple Health access in the Health app, or delete the app. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the "Privacy choices" button reopens Google's consent form. For data processed by Google AdMob, you can exercise your rights with Google as described in its privacy policy. If you live in a region with data protection rights such as access, correction or deletion, write to us at the address above and we will help.

Security and retention

We operate no servers and hold no database, so there is nothing for us to retain, breach or hand over. Your readings, tags, automatic backups and activity log stay on your device until you delete them, and Google retains advertising data under its own policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date. If the app ever begins to collect something it does not collect today, that change will appear here and in the App Store's own privacy labels before it ships.
